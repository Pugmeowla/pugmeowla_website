Abilities#

I can't unlock anything in the skill tree#

  • Abilities cost Aspects of Existence, taken from your inventory. Loot Infinity Temples.
  • A lot of abilities need another one first. Check the "Requires" line on the stone's page.
  • Black Hole and Meteor Storm also need the Power and Space Stones at the same time, and Immortality needs all six.
  • If you've snapped, nearly everything is locked for a Minecraft day. See The burn.

An ability isn't in my ability bar#

Passives and upgrades (Water Breathing, Speed, Spatial Lock and so on) never go in the bar, they just work. Some abilities only show up sometimes: Rocket Burst below level 11, Power Cyclone only at level 11, Exit Soulworld only in the Soulworld, skin abilities only while you hold the unlock item, and the Snap only with six stones.

Size Changing or Invisibility does nothing#

Install Pehkui. They use its /scale command.

Time Freeze or Slow Time froze everyone#

That's how they work, they change the whole server's tick rate. Turn the ability off to go back to 20 TPS.

Stones and containers#

"The Power Stone has already been taken from an Orb"#

There's one Power Stone per world and someone already has it. It's in their inventory, a chest, a gauntlet or on the ground somewhere. The Time Stone and the Eye of Agamotto work the same way.

I keep getting the Orb combination wrong#

Every wrong turn sends you back to the start, but it also tells you the other direction was right for that step. Write the sequence down as you go. The length is in config/infinity-common.toml. An operator can also use /infinity unlock_orb.

Holding the Power Stone hurts me#

The bare Power Stone in your hand isn't protected. Put it in the gauntlet, the Curios head slot or a Cosmi-Rod.

My stone disappeared from the ground#

Stones are unique. While anyone is carrying a stone, copies of it on the ground vanish. Check if someone already has it.

My Eye of Agamotto vanished from my inventory#

When the Time Stone leaves your worn gauntlet, the gauntlet removes any Eye of Agamotto from your inventory. Put the Eye in a chest first.

I can't find a structure, or the explorer map is blank#

There's one of each stone structure per world, thousands of blocks from the origin (see Finding the Stones). The maps for Morag, Svartalfheim and the End are filled from the Overworld and can stay blank. Use /locate structure in that dimension, or go and look.

How do I get to Vormir, Morag or Svartalfheim?#

Use the Space Stone's Teleport GUI and type infinity:vormir (or the others) and some coordinates. With Ad Astra you can fly there in a Tier 4 rocket, or use Cosmic Horizons. See Dimensions.

The Soul Guardian is gone#

An operator can run /infinity soulguardian respawn. If it's only been called away, the Soul Guardian Telephone can bring it over, and it goes back to Vormir by itself after a minute.

Gauntlet#

The gauntlet isn't showing on my arm#

  • In the off hand it doesn't show in first person. Use the Curios hands slot.
  • The visibility (eye) button on the Curios hands slot hides it.

A skin won't go on#

The gauntlet can't already have a skin (go back to the original first), you have to be wearing it, and the unlock item has to be in your main hand. While you're burnt, the Burnt Gauntlet can only be replaced by the Nano Gauntlet skin.

I'm stuck in the Soulworld#

Leave the Snap cell through the door and walk to X 0, Z 0. There's a shaft down to an End portal. If you came in with the Soul Stone, use Exit Soulworld.

Addon files#

My custom stone, skin or gauntlet doesn't show up#

  • Restart the game. These files are only read at startup.
  • The file has to be in the infinity namespace (data/infinity/gauntlet_skins/…), not your own.
  • File names starting with _ are skipped.
  • Search logs/latest.log for Gauntlet stone, Gauntlet skin, Custom gauntlet or Ground item. Every skipped file or ignored value is logged once with the reason.
  • Skins need a registered unlock_item, and stones and ground items need a registered item.

My skin just looks like the normal gauntlet#

Its texture or model is missing, so it fell back to the default and logged it. Check the names: textures/models/<id>.png and textures/item/gauntlet_skins/<id>.png, and all three .geo.json files for a custom model.

Still stuck?#

Ask on the Discord and post your mod list and logs/latest.log.
