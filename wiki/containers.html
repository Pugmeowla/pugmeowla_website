Tesseract#

Tesseract

infinity:tesseract

Holds the Space Stone. Gives the Space Stone power while held in either hand. Hitting it on the ground releases the stone.

The Tesseract from the Norse Village holds the Space Stone. Held in either hand it gives you the Space Stone's power and counts as a Space source for Meteor Storm. Its glowing core is drawn spinning inside the cube, the way Heroes Expansion did it. Drop it and hit it to break it open: the Space Stone falls out with a blue burst of light.

The Aether is the Reality Stone in its fluid form. Either hand gives the Reality Stone's power. Hit it on the ground to turn it back into the stone. To make an Aether again, hold the Reality Stone in your off hand and an Empty Aether in your main hand and use:

Insert Reality Stone#

With the Reality Stone in your off hand, turns an Empty Aether into the Aether.

  • Key press
  • No unlock needed

infinity:empty_aether · palladium:command

Or right-click an Empty Aether lying on the ground while holding the Reality Stone.

Orb#

Orb

infinity:orb

A combination-locked container for the Power Stone. Crack the lock with Rotate Left / Rotate Right to open it.

How to get it
Armor stand inside the Morag Temple (Morag).
Rarity
Epic
Category
Stone Containers

Read more →

The Orb from the Morag Temple is a combination lock. It does not give the Power Stone's power while closed: you have to open it. Hold it (the off hand is recommended) and its abilities appear in the ability bar:

Rotate Left / Rotate Right#

Turn the Orb’s combination lock one step.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.5 s
  • No unlock needed

infinity:orb · geckolib:render_layer_animation

Open / Close Orb#

Once unlocked, opens the Orb to take or insert the Power Stone (right-click).

  • Toggle
  • No unlock needed

infinity:orb · palladium:dummy

Lock Orb#

Locks the Orb again; you’ll need the combination to reopen it.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.5 s
  • No unlock needed

infinity:orb · palladium:command

Cracking the combination#

  • Every world has its own random sequence of left and right turns, 15 turns long by default (configurable, see Configuration). Changing the length makes a new sequence.
  • Use Rotate Left and Rotate Right. A correct turn just clicks. A wrong turn plays a low note, puffs smoke and resets your progress to the start.
  • Because a wrong turn tells you which way was right, write the sequence down as you go: after a mistake, start again and take the other direction at that step.
  • After the last correct turn an iron door opens, sparks fly, and the Orb is unlocked for you (unlocking is per player, the sequence is per world).

Opening it and taking the stone#

  • Once unlocked, Open / Close Orb opens the shell and the Power Stone inside grows into view.
  • With the Orb open, right-click with it to take the Power Stone. There is only one Power Stone per world: if anyone has already taken it, you'll see "The Power Stone has already been taken from an Orb."
  • To put it back, open the Orb and right-click while holding the Power Stone in your other hand. The world's Power Stone is then in an Orb again.
  • Lock Orb resets your unlock, if you want to crack it again.

Operators can skip the puzzle with /infinity unlock_orb.

Eye of Agamotto#

Eye of Agamotto

infinity:eye_of_agamotto

Amulet worn on the chest (iron armor) or in the Curios necklace slot. Opens to reveal the Time Stone, and gives the Time Stone power while it holds it.

How to get it
Armor stand inside the Sanctum Sanctorum (Overworld). Also craftable (see JEI).
Rarity
Epic
Category
Stone Containers

The Eye from the Sanctum Sanctorum is an amulet. Wear it on your chest (it counts as iron armor) or in the Curios necklace slot. While it still holds the Time Stone, wearing it or holding it gives you the Time Stone's power. Its abilities:

Open / Close Eye#

Opens or closes the worn Eye of Agamotto.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 2 s
  • No unlock needed

infinity:eye_of_agamotto · infinity:eye_of_agamotto

Remove / Insert Time Stone#

Take the Time Stone out of the open Eye, or put a held one back.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.5 s
  • No unlock needed

infinity:eye_of_agamotto · infinity:eye_of_agamotto

  • Open Eye opens it like Doctor Strange's, with an animation, and the stone glows once it's open.
  • Remove Time Stone (while open) gives you the stone. The Eye stays, now empty. There's only one Time Stone per world: if it's already been taken, you'll see "The Time Stone has already been taken from an Eye of Agamotto."
  • Insert Time Stone (while open and empty) needs the Time Stone in either hand.
  • Hitting the Eye while it lies on the ground also lets the stone go; the Eye stays where it was.
  • Old Empty Eye of Agamotto items from earlier versions still work and turn into the normal Eye when you insert the stone.

Cosmi-Rod#

Cosmi-Rod

infinity:cosmi-rod

Netherite-tier sword with a socket for any one stone (right-click to open it). Gives that stone's power while held, plus the Shockwave ability.

How to get it
Item frame in the Crashed Kree Ship (Overworld plains).
Rarity
Epic
Category
Weapons & Tools

Read more →

The Cosmi-Rod, Ronan's Universal Weapon, is in the Crashed Kree Ship. It's a netherite-tier weapon with a socket for one stone:

  • Right-click it in your main hand to open its one-slot menu and put in any stone, including addon stones.
  • While held in either hand it gives the socketed stone's power, and the stone is drawn on the rod in its own color.
  • The tooltip shows the socketed stone. Older per-stone Cosmi-Rod items from earlier versions become an empty rod.

Holding the rod also gives its own ability:

Shockwave#

Concussive blast from the hand that flings and hurts everything in front of you.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 5 s
  • No unlock needed

Fires for 7 ticks, dealing 5 damage per tick to everything in a box in front of you that grows up to 4 blocks, and launching it along your look direction. 5 second cooldown. Available while the Cosmi-Rod is in either hand.

infinity:cosmi_rod · infinity:shockwave

Summon / Return Soul Guardian#

Calls the Soul Guardian from Vormir to you. Sneak + right-click sends it back.

  • Right-click
  • Cooldown 1200 s
  • No unlock needed

infinity:soul_guardian_telephone · palladium:command

  • Right-click to bring the Soul Guardian from Vormir to you. It only works if the Guardian is loaded, which the telephone takes care of by force-loading the Vormir area for the call.
  • The Guardian is sent back to Vormir automatically after 1,200 ticks (one minute).
  • Sneak + right-click sends it back straight away.
  • The telephone's cooldown is 24,000 ticks (one Minecraft day, 20 minutes).

Items on the ground#

The stones, every container, the gauntlet, the Cosmi-Rod and the swords are ground items when dropped or thrown:

  • They land where you threw them and lie still on the ground instead of floating and spinning.
  • They can't be destroyed, burnt or blown up, never despawn, and rest at the bottom of the world instead of falling out of it.
  • Walking over them doesn't pick them up: right-click to pick one up. They keep all their data (a gauntlet's stones and skin, names, enchantments).
  • Containers that hold a stone break open when hit; stones and empty containers combine when you right-click one with the other.
  • While a player carries a stone, any copy of that stone lying on the ground disappears, so stones can't be duplicated.

Addons can make their own items behave like this: see Ground Items.
