Stone structures#

One of each per world, on a ring around 0, 0.

Norse Village#

Overworld, about 18,500 to 35,300 blocks out. Has the Tesseract.

Sanctum Sanctorum#

Overworld, about 7,300 to 14,000 blocks out. Has the Eye of Agamotto.

Sanctuary#

The End, about 7,300 to 14,000 blocks out. Has the Scepter.

Aether Chamber#

Underground on Svartalfheim, about 4,400 to 8,400 blocks out. Has the Aether, guarded by Malekith.

Morag Temple#

On the ocean floor of Morag, about 18,500 to 35,300 blocks out, with Arrows of Harming VII traps. Has the Orb.

Vormir's Edge#

Built in one go the first time the world loads, at 0 63 0 on Vormir, with the Soul Guardian at 212 121 99.

Other structures#

Infinity Temple#

Common across the Overworld. The chest has 1 to 4 Aspects of Existence and other loot.

Crashed Kree Ship#

In plains. Has the Cosmi-Rod.

Dwarf Star Meteor#

Craters with Dwarf Star Meteor all over the Overworld.

Uru Meteor#

Craters with Uru Ore on Svartalfheim.
