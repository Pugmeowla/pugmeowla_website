Weapons#

The Double-Edged Sword (12 damage, 1.0 speed) and Single-Edged Sword (10 damage, 2.0 speed) are netherite tier. The Scepter is an iron tier sword. The Cosmi-Rod has a separate item for each stone, see Cosmi-Rod.

Thanos armor#

Classic and MCU sets of netherite armor, creative only.

Fancy Bottle#

Hold a Fancy Bottle and spend 3 experience levels in its power screen to turn it into a Bottle o' Enchanting.

Materials#

Titanium, steel, gold titanium alloy, dwarf star alloy and uru, each with dusts, ingots, nuggets and plates. Uru Ore is in the meteor craters on Svartalfheim, and Dwarf Star Meteor in Overworld craters.

Blocks#

Storage blocks for each metal, the ores, and the ground blocks of Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim. There's no Temple Beacon in 7.3.
