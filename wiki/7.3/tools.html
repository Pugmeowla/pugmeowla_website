Tools

The Cosmi-Rod and the Soul Guardian Telephone in 7.3.

Cosmi-Rod#

In 7.3 there's a separate Cosmi-Rod item for each stone. Hold an empty Cosmi-Rod in your main hand, hold a stone in your off hand and right-click to put it in. Sneak and right-click to take it out. The stone's Cosmi-Rod gives that stone's power in your main hand.

Insert / Remove Stone#

Right-click with the rod in your main hand and a stone in your off hand to put it in. Sneak and right-click to take it out.

  • Right-click
  • No unlock needed

infinity:cosmi-rod · palladium:command

Soul Guardian Telephone#

Summon / Return Soul Guardian#

Calls the Soul Guardian from Vormir. Sneak and right-click to send it back.

  • Right-click
  • Cooldown 1200 s
  • No unlock needed

infinity:soul_guardian_telephone · palladium:command

The Guardian goes back to Vormir after 1,200 ticks (a minute), and the phone has a 24,000 tick (one day) cooldown.
