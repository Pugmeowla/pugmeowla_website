Compatibility

The mods 7.3 needs and the ones it works with.

Needed#

Palladium, KubeJS, CoreWithStuff, Chrono API, Pehkui, Curios API, GeckoLib, Pantheon Sent, Colourful Hearts and Huge Structure Blocks. 7.3 is an addon pack that loads as a mod, and a lot of it runs on KubeJS scripts and Palladium functions.

Works with#

ModWhat you get
Ad AstraMorag, Svartalfheim and Vormir as planets. Needs a Tier 4 rocket.
Cosmic HorizonsThe Marvel solar system with the planets in it.
Tinkers' ConstructCasting the gauntlet (it replaces the normal recipe) and molten metals.
Fsang's HeropackShared metal tags and an uru plate made from Fsang's uru.
Satsu's Iron Man AddonNanotech suit support.
CreateAn extra way to make Aspects of Existence.
Pugmeowla's InfintrixOmnitrixes work as gauntlets.
Pugmeowla's Herbs of WakandaThe Black Panther necklace gives the gauntlet power.
PowerborneA thrown Stormbreaker switches off the Soul Stone's defenses.
