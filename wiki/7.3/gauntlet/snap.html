The Snap

Six stones, one wheel, five choices.

On this page
  1. The Snap wheel

The Snap wheel#

With all six stones in your gauntlet and no burn, hold the Snap Wheel key and pick one:

Kill Half of All Entities (Sent to Soulworld)#

Each mob and player has a 50% chance to turn to dust and be sent to the Soulworld.

  • Wheel
  • No unlock needed

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:command

Kill All Hostile#

Sends every hostile mob to the Soulworld.

  • Wheel
  • No unlock needed

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:command

Kill Other Players#

Sends every other player to the Soulworld.

  • Wheel
  • No unlock needed

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:command

Bring Back the Snapped#

Teleports everything that was snapped to the Soulworld back to you.

  • Wheel
  • No unlock needed

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:command

Destroy Stones#

A 7 second ritual that breaks all six stones and sends you to the Soulworld.

  • Wheel
  • No unlock needed

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:animation_timer

What happens#

  • Kill Half: every mob and player except you has a 50% chance of being snapped. They turn to dust, go invisible for a moment and get sent to 1000 78 1000 in the Soulworld with slow falling.
  • Kill All Hostile and Kill Other Players send their targets to the same spot.
  • Bring Back the Snapped teleports everything that was snapped back to you.
  • Destroy Stones breaks all six stones, takes their powers away and sends you to the Soulworld at Y 72.

The burn#

Every option burns you. Your health drops to half a heart, a lot of stone abilities lock, your arm looks burnt and you can't snap again. After one full Minecraft day it wears off. Putting on the Nano Gauntlet skin takes the burn away straight away.
