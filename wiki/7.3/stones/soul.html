Soul Stone

A soul for a soul.

On this page
  1. Getting it
  2. Abilities
  3. Skill tree costs

Getting it#

  1. Kill one of your own tamed pets to get a Best Friend's Soul.
  2. Go to Vormir with /space_tp or a rocket.
  3. Trade the soul to the Soul Guardian at the top of Vormir's Edge.

Abilities#

Glow#

Hold to see everything within 100 blocks outlined in orange.

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:entity_glow

Locate Entity Command#

Lets you use /locateplayer .

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Glow

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:dummy

Saturation#

You never get hungry.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Glow

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command

Effect Immunity#

Clears most bad effects every tick.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Saturation

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command

Regeneration#

Very strong permanent Regeneration.

  • Passive
  • 16 Aspects of Existence

Requires Saturation

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command

Health#

Close to infinite max health.

  • Passive
  • 64 Aspects of Existence

Requires Regeneration

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

Immortality#

You can not die. Needs all six stones.

  • Passive
  • 128 Aspects of Existence

Requires Health

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

Comes with Invulnerability, which a KubeJS script uses to cancel every hit, with soul particles.

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:immortality

Summon Zombie#

Summons six zombies on your team.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command

Enter / Exit Soulworld#

Takes you and everything within 5 blocks to the Soulworld. Use it again to go back.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Exit sends everyone within 5 blocks to the Overworld at Y 72 above the same X and Z, with slow falling. It doesn't remember where they came from.

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command
