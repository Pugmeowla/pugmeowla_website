Power Stone

Power levels, beams, TNT and meteors. Don't hold it bare.

On this page
  1. Overview
  2. Abilities
  3. Skill tree costs

Overview#

The Power Stone is in the Orb in the Morag Temple. You have to unlock the Orb to get it out (see the Orb). In 7.3 its power is called infinity:power_stone_passive, and you get it from holding the stone, the gauntlet or the Power Cosmi-Rod.

Unprotected

Holding the bare stone without it also being in the gauntlet sets off a ring of TNT around you after half a second and gives you Wither. Put it in the gauntlet or a Cosmi-Rod.

Abilities#

Impower#

Health Boost VI and Resistance IV for 90 seconds.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 120 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:power_stone_passive · palladium:command

Knockback Resistance#

Nothing knocks you back.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Impower

infinity:power_stone_passive · palladium:attribute_modifier

Power Blast#

Hold to fire a 100 block beam that smelts blocks and starts fires. Damage is 2^level.

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Impower

At level 11 with all six stones the beam turns rainbow, does near infinite damage and summons TNT where it hits blocks. Fired from your off hand.

infinity:power_stone_passive · palladium:energy_beam

Power Levels 1 to 11#

Eleven levels you buy one by one. Each doubles your base attack damage.

  • Upgrade
  • 434 Aspects of Existence

They cost 2, 4, 8, 12, 18, 26, 36, 48, 64, 88 and 128 Aspects of Existence. Use Damage Control to pick the active one. See Power Levels.

infinity:power_stone_passive · palladium:dummy (×11)

Damage Control#

Turn it on, then scroll to set your active power level.

  • Scroll
  • No unlock needed

Requires Power Levels 1 to 11

Your base attack damage is set to 2^level (1 at level 0, 2048 at level 11) and goes back to 1 when you lose the stone.

infinity:power_stone_passive · palladium:command

Cosmic Explosion#

Hold to set off a ring of TNT 8 blocks around you, over and over. Explosions don’t hurt you.

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Power Blast

infinity:power_stone_passive · palladium:command

Meteor Storm#

Drops 20 deepslate meteors from 70 blocks up, spread up to 50 blocks around you.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Cosmic Explosion

Needs stones Space Stone

Needs the Space Stone too. Spamming it can lag.

infinity:power_stone_passive · palladium:command

Radiation Texture Toggle#

Free. Click it to hide or show the radiation cracks on your skin.

  • Key press
  • Free unlock

infinity:power_stone_passive · palladium:command
