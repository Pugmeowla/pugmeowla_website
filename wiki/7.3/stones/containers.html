Orb#

The Orb has a combination lock. Hold it and use Rotate Left and Rotate Right. A wrong turn plays a low note, puffs smoke and sends you back to the start. In 7.3 the combination is the same in every world (it's on Finding the Stones if you want it). If you'd rather work it out, get a pen and paper ready: it's trial and error, so write down every turn as you find it. Unlocking is per player and lasts until you lock it again.

Rotate Left / Rotate Right#

Turns the Orb’s combination lock one step.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.5 s
  • No unlock needed

infinity:orb · geckolib:render_layer_animation

Open / Close Orb#

Once unlocked, opens the Orb so you can take or put back the Power Stone.

  • Toggle
  • No unlock needed

infinity:orb · palladium:dummy

Lock Orb#

Locks the Orb again for you.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.5 s
  • No unlock needed

infinity:orb · palladium:command

Once it's open, right-click to take the Power Stone, or right-click with the Power Stone in your other hand to put it back. Only one Power Stone can be taken per world. 7.3 keeps track of that with a barrier block at 69420 -64 69420 on Morag.

Tesseract#

Gives the Space Stone's power in either hand. Drop it and hit it to get the stone out.

Insert Reality Stone#

With the Reality Stone in your off hand, turns an Empty Aether into the Aether.

  • Key press
  • No unlock needed

infinity:empty_aether · palladium:command

Eye of Agamotto#

Wear it on your chest or in the Curios necklace slot, or hold it, for the Time Stone's power. Drop it and hit it to get the stone out, which leaves an Empty Eye of Agamotto.

Scepter#

An iron tier sword with the Mind Stone. In your main hand it fires the Scepter Power Blast. Drop it and hit it to get the stone and an Empty Scepter.

On the ground#

Stones, containers and the gauntlet dropped near a player turn into props on invisible armor stands. Hitting a container that holds a stone breaks it open.
