Space Stone

Teleporting, telekinesis and black holes.

On this page
  1. Overview
  2. Abilities
  3. Skill tree costs
  4. Notes

Overview#

The Space Stone is in the Tesseract in the Norse Village. Hold the stone or the Tesseract, put it in the gauntlet or use the Space Cosmi-Rod to get its power. Drop the Tesseract and hit it to get the stone out.

Abilities#

Speed#

Adds 0.05 to your movement speed, about 50% faster on foot.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:space_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

Step Assist#

Walk up full blocks without jumping.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Speed

infinity:space_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

Fall Damage Immunity#

No fall damage.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Step Assist

infinity:space_stone · palladium:damage_immunity

Force Field#

A shield bubble that blocks attacks but stops you moving.

  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Fall Damage Immunity

Gives a huge armor and toughness boost and draws a shield around you while you're rooted in place. A KubeJS script cancels hits under 23 damage from anything with an attacker, and every hit from Iron Golems, Wardens, Vindicators, Piglin Brutes and Zombified Piglins.

infinity:space_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

Water Breathing#

Breathe underwater.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Force Field

infinity:space_stone · palladium:command

Telekinesis#

Hold to grab a mob or player (up to 20 blocks) and move it around.

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Force Field

Needs CoreWithStuff. Scroll to move what you're holding between 2 and 30 blocks away and left-click to throw it. Bedrock, barriers and obsidian can't be grabbed.

infinity:space_stone · corewithstuff:telekinesis

Teleport#

Teleports you to where you are looking, through a short portal.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Telekinesis

infinity:space_stone · palladium:command

Teleport Command#

Lets you use /space_tp .

  • Passive
  • 10 Aspects of Existence

Requires Teleport

Takes you and everything within 3 blocks to those coordinates in any dimension. See /space_tp.

infinity:space_stone · palladium:dummy

Teleport GUI#

A wormhole menu from CoreWithStuff. Locked for good without CoreWithStuff.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Teleport

infinity:space_stone · corewithstuff:wormhole_gui

Black Hole#

Makes a black hole that pulls in everything within 7 blocks. Press again to switch it off.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Telekinesis

Needs stones Power Stone

Needs the Power Stone as well. Black Hole Off takes its place in the bar while one is active.

infinity:space_stone · palladium:command

Notes#

  • Telekinesis and the Teleport GUI come from CoreWithStuff. Telekinesis only picks up mobs and players in 7.3, not blocks.
  • The Teleport Command is the main way to reach the planets without a rocket. See /space_tp.
