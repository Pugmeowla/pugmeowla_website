Where a stone's power comes from#

SourceStones
Holding the stone (either hand)All six. The Power Stone blows up around you if you do this.
Holding the container (either hand)Space (Tesseract), Mind (Scepter), Reality (Aether), Time (Eye of Agamotto)
Wearing the Eye of AgamottoTime, on the chest or in the Curios necklace slot
Curios head slotOnly the Mind Stone gives its power there in 7.3
The Infinity GauntletEvery stone in it
That stone's Cosmi-Rod, in your main handOne stone

In 7.3 the Power Stone's power is called infinity:power_stone_passive. The others use the same names as 8.0.

Abilities that need more than one stone#

  • Black Hole needs the Space and Power Stones.
  • Meteor Storm needs the Power and Space Stones.
  • The rainbow Power Blast needs level 11 and all six stones.
  • Immortality and the Snap need all six.
