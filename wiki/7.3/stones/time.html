Time Stone

Speed the world up, slow it down or stop it.

On this page
  1. Overview
  2. Abilities
  3. Skill tree costs

Overview#

The Time Stone is in the Eye of Agamotto in the Sanctum Sanctorum. Hold or wear the Eye (chest or Curios necklace), hold the stone, put it in the gauntlet or use the Time Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Eye and hit it to get the stone out.

Abilities#

Fast Forward Daylight Cycle#

Hold to speed through the day (100 ticks of daytime every tick).

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command

Stop Projectile#

Stops projectiles in the air near you. They carry on after you leave.

  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command

Time Command#

Lets you use /time_set .

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

infinity:time_stone · palladium:dummy

Daylight Cycle Toggle#

Free. Click it to switch the doDaylightCycle game rule.

  • Key press
  • Free unlock

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command

Slow Time#

Unlocks Change Flow of Time. Turn it on and scroll to change the server tick rate.

  • Scroll
  • 5 Aspects of Existence

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

Runs Chrono API's chrono settickrate for the whole server. Turning it off sets it back to 20.

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command (chrono)

Freeze Time#

Unlocks Time Freeze. Pauses the whole server with chrono pausetime.

  • Toggle
  • 10 Aspects of Existence

Requires Slow Time

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command (chrono)

Screen Toggle#

Free. Hides or shows the green screen tint.

  • Key press
  • Free unlock

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command
