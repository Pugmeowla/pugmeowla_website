Mind Stone

Intangibility, flight and pacifying mobs.

On this page
  1. Overview
  2. Abilities
  3. The Scepter's beam
  4. Skill tree costs

Overview#

The Mind Stone is in the Scepter in the Sanctuary in the End. Hold the stone or the Scepter, wear the stone in the Curios head slot, put it in the gauntlet or use the Mind Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Scepter and hit it to get the stone out.

Abilities#

Intangibility#

Walk through blocks and mobs. Nothing can hurt you while it is on.

  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Your reach and hitbox shrink to almost nothing while it's on (Pehkui), and a KubeJS script cancels all damage. No cooldown in 7.3.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:intangibility

Pacify#

Mobs within 5 blocks stop attacking you. Sneak and use it to undo.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Intangibility

You and everything within 5 blocks join the peaceful team. Use it while sneaking (Un-Pacify) to take them off it again.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Night Vision#

Permanent Night Vision.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Intangibility

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Hero of the People#

Permanent Hero of the Village VI.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Pacify

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Recipes#

Unlocks every recipe in your recipe book.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Flight#

Fly. Works from the gauntlet, a held Mind Stone or Scepter, the head slot or a Mind Cosmi-Rod.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:flight

Flight Toggle#

Free. Click it in the skill tree to turn Mind Stone flight off or back on.

  • Key press
  • Free unlock

Requires Flight

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

The Scepter's beam#

Scepter Power Blast#

Hold to fire a beam from the Scepter. 20 damage, 50 blocks, sets things on fire.

  • Hold
  • No unlock needed

Works while the Scepter is in your main hand. A Mind Stone in the Curios head slot fires the same beam from your head.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:energy_beam
