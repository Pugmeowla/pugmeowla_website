Reality Stone

Block duplication, size changing, invisibility and the weather.

On this page
  1. Overview
  2. Abilities
  3. Skill tree costs

Overview#

The Reality Stone is the Aether, deep in the Aether Chamber on Svartalfheim. Hold the stone or the Aether, put it in the gauntlet or use the Reality Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Aether and hit it to get the stone out, and use an Empty Aether to put it back.

Abilities#

Effects#

Gives everything within 100 blocks Blindness, Nausea, Darkness and Slowness for 100 seconds.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

You don't get them yourself.

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:command

Size Changing#

Unlocks Size Control. Turn it on and scroll to grow up to 5× or shrink to 0.1×.

  • Scroll
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Same steps as 8.0: down goes 0.9×, 0.8× and on to 0.1×, up goes 2× to 5×. Turning Size Control off puts you back to normal. Uses Pehkui.

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:dummy + palladium:command

Invisibility#

Properly invisible. No body, no armor, no name tag.

  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:invisibility

Change the Weather#

Hold for a wheel with Clear, Rain and Thunder.

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:ability_wheel

Block Saving#

Unlocks Block Duplication: while it is on, blocks you look at get added to your inventory.

  • Upgrade
  • 128 Aspects of Existence

Use Block Duplication On and Block Duplication Off in the ability bar to switch it.

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:dummy
