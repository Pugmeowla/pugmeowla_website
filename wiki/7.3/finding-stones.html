The structures are placed the same way as in 8.0, one of each per world, thousands of blocks from 0, 0. The distances are on Structures. /locate structure works in the right dimension.

Getting to the planets#

  • The Space Stone: unlock Teleport Command and use /space_tp <x> <y> <z> <dimension>, for example /space_tp 212 130 99 infinity:vormir. See Commands.
  • Ad Astra: Morag, Svartalfheim and Vormir are Ad Astra planets. You need a Tier 4 rocket.
  • Cosmic Horizons: the planets are in its Marvel solar system.

The Orb's combination#

In 7.3 the Orb has the same 15 turn combination in every world. Each wrong turn sends you back to the start. If you just want the answer:

Left, Right, Right, Left, Right, Left, Left, Right, Right, Left, Right, Left, Right, Right, Left

More on the Orb.

The Soul Stone and the Soul Guardian#

Kill one of your own tamed pets and you get a Best Friend's Soul. Take it to the Soul Guardian at the top of Vormir's Edge, around 212 121 99 on Vormir. Its trades, each usable once:

You giveYou get
Best Friend's SoulSoul Stone
Clock and 16 Aspects of ExistenceSanctum Sanctorum Explorer Map
Eye of Ender and 16 Aspects of ExistenceNorse Village Explorer Map
Nether Star and 16 Aspects of ExistenceMorag Temple Explorer Map
Red Dye and 16 Aspects of ExistenceAether Chamber Explorer Map
Book and 16 Aspects of ExistenceSanctuary Explorer Map

The Soul Guardian Telephone can call the Guardian to you.
