Pugmeowla'sInfinity Stone Core 7.3

7.3 is the version you get from CurseForge and Modrinth right now. It's a Palladium addon pack with KubeJS scripts, and it has the Infinity Gauntlet, all six stones and their containers, the structures and the planets. These pages cover how 7.3 works.

The six stones

Space StoneTeleporting, telekinesis and black holes. Mind StoneFlight, intangibility and pacifying mobs. Reality StoneBlock duplication, size, invisibility and weather. Power StonePower levels, beams, TNT and meteors. Time StoneSpeed up, slow down or freeze the world. Soul StoneThe Soulworld, a zombie army and near immortality.

7.3 and 8.0

8.0 is a big rewrite into a proper Java mod. If you're playing 7.3, these are the main things that work differently from the 8.0 pages:

  • 7.3 needs KubeJS and CoreWithStuff. 8.0 doesn't.
  • The gauntlet has one fixed slot per stone and opens through CoreWithStuff's menu.
  • The Cosmi-Rod turns into a different item for each stone, and Mind, Reality and Space have smaller trees.
  • Skins belong to the player, not the gauntlet.
  • The Snap is a wheel with five options, and half the world is sent to the Soulworld instead of killed.
  • The Orb's combination is the same in every world.
  • Commands aren't under /infinity, they're /space_tp, /time_set, /locateplayer and so on.
