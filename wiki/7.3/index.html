Version 7.3 · Current release · Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge · Palladium

Pugmeowla'sInfinity Stone Core

A full Infinity expansion for 1.20.1, made as a Palladium addon pack that loads like a mod, with KubeJS scripts doing the rest. It isn't just a port: it brings over the Infinity content from Lucraft Core, Heroes Expansion, Speedster Heroes, Star Tech, Man! The Legendary Mod? and Infinity Craft, and builds on it with new abilities, structures and planets. Find the stones across the Overworld, the End and four other planets, work through each stone's skill tree, and then decide if you want to snap. 7.3 is the version on CurseForge and Modrinth right now.

The six stones

Power StonePower levels, beams, TNT and meteors. Space StoneTeleporting, telekinesis and black holes. Reality StoneBlock duplication, size, invisibility and weather. Soul StoneThe Soulworld, a zombie army and near immortality. Time StoneSpeed up, slow down or freeze the world. Mind StoneFlight, intangibility and pacifying mobs.
