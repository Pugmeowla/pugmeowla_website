What 7.3 is#

Pugmeowla's Infinity Stone Core is the official 1.20.1 port of Infinity Craft. In 7.3 it's a Palladium addon pack that loads like a mod, with the gameplay done through Palladium powers, functions and KubeJS scripts. It has the six Infinity Stones, the Infinity Gauntlet and its skins, the containers and the Cosmi-Rod, a structure for every stone and four planets.

What to install#

ModNeeded?Used for
Minecraft 1.20.1 and Forge 47+Required
PalladiumRequiredPowers, abilities, skill trees and the addon pack itself.
KubeJSRequiredThe commands, the Force Field and Intangibility protection, Immortality, molten metals and Telekinesis.
CoreWithStuffRequiredThe gauntlet's stone menu, Space Stone Telekinesis and the Teleport GUI.

Tinkers' ConstructOptionalCasting the gauntlet, molten metals.

More detail on Compatibility.

Controls#

  • Abilities use Palladium's ability bar and keys, and the skill trees are in Palladium's powers screen.
  • The gauntlet's stone menu opens through CoreWithStuff. Hold the gauntlet in your main hand and right-click, or use the gauntlet's Open GUI ability while you wear it. See The Infinity Gauntlet.
  • Scroll abilities (power levels, Slow Time, Size Changing) use the mouse wheel while their toggle is on.

Buying abilities#

Each stone is a Palladium power with a skill tree. Click an ability to buy it with Aspects of Existence from your inventory. Many need another ability first. Costs are on Progression.

How a playthrough goes#

  1. Loot Infinity Temples for Aspects of Existence, and village weaponsmith chests for the Infinity Gauntlet Cast.
  2. Make the Infinity Gauntlet (recipe in JEI).
  3. Get the Space Stone from the Norse Village and unlock its teleporting, or build a rocket.
  4. Collect the rest. The route is on Finding the Stones.
  5. Fill out the trees and decide whether to snap.
The Power Stone

Holding the Power Stone in your hand without protection blows up everything around you with TNT and gives you Wither. Keep it in the gauntlet or in a Cosmi-Rod.
