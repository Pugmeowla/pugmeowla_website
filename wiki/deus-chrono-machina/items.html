Galvan gadgets#

Galvan Compass

chrono:galvan_compass

Right-click to highlight ores within 10 blocks. Sneak + right-click to point at Maltruant's fragments and altar. Gives off light while held.

How to get it
Crafted: a Clock surrounded by four Upgrade Chips, with Galvan Metal in the corners.
Rarity
Rare
Category
Galvan Gadgets

Galvan EMP

chrono:galvan_emp

Sends out electromagnetic pulses that make nearby mobs take more damage and weaken Chronosapiens. Charge it by holding Omni Crystals in your main hand and right-clicking with the EMP in your off hand.

How to get it
Crafted: a Nether Star between Timeclock Chip I and II, with two Upgrade Crystals and four Timeclock Gears.
Rarity
Epic
Category
Galvan Gadgets

Right-click to highlight ores within 10 blocks for a moment (up to 15 at once). Sneak + right-click to point it at the nearest of Maltruant's fragments, and at the Maltruant Altar. The needle also shows on your HUD.

The EMP is your counter to Chronosapiens: inside its field they're stunned, and every mob takes more damage. Maltruant knows this and tries to stay out of it.

Time Reborn#

Time Reborn (chrono:time_reborn, up to level 5) goes on weapons and chestplates. Time Beasts drop it as a level I book; Maltruant has a 25% chance of a level V book.

Weapon
Hits deal +2 damage and give Slowness II for 5 seconds. Kills have a 25% chance to drop a Time Reborn Device holding the mob, if its max health is at most 30 × the level.
Chestplate
10% chance to cancel a hit. Once every 3 minutes, a hit that would kill you is cancelled and time rewinds you instead.

Armor Break#

Armor Break (chrono:armor_break) is a status effect from Maltruant's guard-breaking attacks. It strips armor while it lasts, so stop and heal rather than trading hits.
