Getting the DNA

The Chronosapien isn't in your Omnitrix from the start. You build a sample of Chronosapien DNA out of Timeclock parts and inject it.

Quick tutorial#

  1. Collect Timeclock Gears Timeclock Gear: kill Time Beasts, mine Chrono Ore, loot villages and bastions.
  2. Get some Upgrade Chips and Upgrade Crystals from Alien Evolution.
  3. Craft a Timeclock Key, both Timeclock Chips and a Timeclock Core.
  4. Craft Chronosapien DNA and right-click it. The Chronosapien is now in your Omnitrix.

All together that's 15 Timeclock Gears (6 for the Key, 2 for each Chip, 4 for the Core, 1 more for the DNA), 6 Upgrade Chips and 5 Upgrade Crystals.

Time Beasts guard Maltruant's Area, a small ruin in the biomes where Alien Evolution's Galvan capsules land. They're the fastest way to farm gears, and also drop the Key (5%) and, very rarely, a ready-made DNA sample (0.1%).

Recipes#

The Upgrade Chip, Upgrade Crystal and Galvan Metal come from Alien Evolution. Check JEI for how to get them in your version.

A Timeclock Chip on its own in the grid turns into the other one, so a spare of either works.

Injecting the DNA#

Hold the DNA and right-click. "Chronosapien DNA was injected into the Omnitrix." means it worked and the DNA is used up. It won't be used if:

  • your Omnitrix already has Chronosapien DNA,
  • your Omnitrix playlists are full (make room first), or
  • Alien Evolution didn't load the Chronosapien alien file (a broken install).

Once you have him, see Clockwork and his skill tree.
