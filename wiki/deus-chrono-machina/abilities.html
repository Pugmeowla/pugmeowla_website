Temporal rays#

Pick a ray in the Temporal Arsenal, then hold the key to fire. All rays reach 64 blocks, need Temporal Energy to start and share a 5 second cooldown. Rays that hit blocks age them into Aged Blocks.

Basic Temporal Ray#

A temporal beam from the chest: 3 damage, 64 blocks. Hitting blocks ages them.

  • Hold
  • Cooldown 5 s

Energy 100 to fire

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:energy_beam

Enhanced Temporal Ray#

A stronger ray that slows its targets and speeds up time-based block changes (crops, saplings).

  • Hold
  • Cooldown 5 s

Energy 100 to fire

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:energy_beam

Temporal Ray Overcharge#

While a ray is firing, overcharge it for +2 damage, more sparks and a bigger beam.

  • Hold

Energy +1 while it runs

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Stasis Ray#

Stops its target in time. Only while no timeflow is running.

  • Hold
  • Cooldown 5 s

Energy 100 to fire

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:energy_beam

Restoration Ray#

Restores the target's flow of time: unfreezes it and clears harmful effects.

  • Hold
  • Cooldown 5 s

Energy 100 to fire

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:energy_beam

While Slow Time is on: puts the target back in an earlier state, or heals it when there is none.

  • Cooldown 5 s

10K Hand Cannon#

With the Prototype or 10K Omnitrix uniform, Temporal Charge is replaced by a hand cannon: hold right-click to charge an erasure beam or an energy shot.

  • Right-click
  • Cooldown 2 s

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:energy_beam

Timeflow#

Hold the Timeflow key to charge, then let go. The charge decides how long it lasts. Only one timeflow runs at a time; End Timeflow stops it early. Slowed and stopped mobs really are slowed: their tick rate changes, so their attacks, movement and animations all wait.

Slow Time#

Hold to charge, then everything around you slows to the chosen time-flow rate. You stay at full speed.

  • Hold
  • Cooldown 4 s

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Accelerate Time#

Hold to charge, then speed up the time around you to the chosen rate while you stay in control.

  • Hold
  • Cooldown 4 s

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Change Flow of Time#

While Slow or Accelerate Time is on, toggle this and scroll to pick one of five time-flow rates. Shown on screen.

  • Scroll

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Daylight Cycle#

While Accelerate Time is on, speed up the day and night cycle too.

  • Toggle

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Time Control#

Hold to charge, then stop time completely around you. You can still move, attack and walk on water.

  • Hold
  • Cooldown 4 s

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Beyond Time#

During Time Control, lets nearby mobs and players move again for a while.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 1 s

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Restop Time#

During Time Control, freezes nearby mobs and players again.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 1 s

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Speed Up#

During Time Control, move at high speed.

  • Toggle

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Infinite Timeflow#

During Time Control, scroll to push the stopped timeline forwards or rewind it, while everything else stays frozen.

  • Scroll

chrono_machina:chronosapien

Time Rewind#

Reverses time across the area around you: blocks and mobs go back to where they were.

  • Hold
  • Cooldown 4 s

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Ends the running timeflow early and brings back normal time.

  • Key press

Clockwork#

Temporal Charge#

Hold right-click with an empty hand to turn the key on your head and fill the Temporal Energy meter. Slows you while you wind it.

  • Right-click
  • Cooldown 4 s

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:animation_timer

Temporal Singularity#

Gather an Omnitrix-colored orb of temporal energy. When it bursts it freezes everything alive and turns the terrain gray.

  • Hold

chrono_machina:chronosapien

Temporal Anchor#

Standing still, record your state. When it runs out, when you press it again, or when you would die, you go back to it. Same dimension only.

  • Key press

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Detransform All#

Forces every transformed player within 10 blocks back to human.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 3 s

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:command

Chronosapien Body#

The clockwork body takes no fire or freezing damage, and shrugs off falls.

  • Passive

chrono_machina:chronosapien · palladium:damage_immunity

Activation types#

Hold
Hold the key down. Rays fire while held; timeflows charge while held and start when you let go.
Key press
Press its key once.
Toggle
Press once to turn it on and again to turn it off.
Scroll
Use the mouse wheel while it's available.
Right-click
Hold right-click with an empty hand.
Passive
Always on.
Unlock
An entry in the skill tree. Unlocking it opens up the abilities listed on it.

Cooldowns are in seconds (20 ticks is 1 second).
