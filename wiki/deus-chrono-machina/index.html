Version 7.0 · Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge · Alien Evolution addon

Rolmi & Pugmeowla'sDeus Chrono-Machina

Adds Clockwork, the Chronosapien, as a transformation for the Omnitrix in Alien Evolution. Hunt Time Beasts for Timeclock Gears, craft Chronosapien DNA, and inject it into your Omnitrix. Then slow, speed up, stop and rewind time, erase mobs from the timeline, fuse with Ship into the Upgrade form, and gather the five fragments that bring back Maltruant.
