The people behind Deus Chrono-Machina.

Made by#

Pugmeowla

Original owner

Started the mod, then handed it over to Rolmi. Still publishes it and keeps this wiki.

Rolmi

Successor

The mod as it is today: Rolmi took the project on and built almost all of it, from Clockwork's abilities to Maltruant, the mobs and the world.

Built on#

  • Alien Evolution, for the Omnitrix, the alien system and Ship's Galvanic Mechamorph species.
  • Palladium, which runs Clockwork's powers, and Chrono API.
  • Music discs by Rod Abernethy.
  • Clockwork, Maltruant, Exo-Skull, Subdora and Ship are from Ben 10.
