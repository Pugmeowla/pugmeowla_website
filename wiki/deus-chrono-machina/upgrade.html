Upgrade Fusion

Clockwork fused with Ship, a Galvanic Mechamorph. Twice the stats, a self-charging core, timeflows with no time limit, and Galvan weapons.

Getting Ship#

  • Find one. Alien Evolution crash sites have a small chance (0.1%) of a wild Ship nearby.
  • Tame it with a Galvan Circuit. Feed it Upgrade Crystals to change its color.
  • Carry it: sneak + right-click your Ship with an empty bucket to get a Bucket of Ship.

More about Ship on the Mobs page.

Fusing and separating#

  1. Transform into the Chronosapien.
  2. Right-click your Ship. It wraps around you and you become the Upgrade fusion (the "Clockwork-Techno Bubble Fusion" advancement).
  3. To split up, use the Omnitrix's detransform or separate action. Ship pops back out next to you.

The fusion ends if your health drops to 2 hearts or less, so it can't carry you through a fight you're losing.

Alliance Across Time

Clockwork's skill tree carries over. On top of it the fusion has Ship's Galvan powers. Without Ship, the Chronosapien Solo Skills power lets Clockwork switch some of them (Shoulder Bombs, Galvan Beam, Drill Hand, Leap and Sense) on by himself once the matching skill tree entries are unlocked.

Upgrade abilities#

Temporal Charge (Upgrade)#

The Upgrade's temporal core fills itself; no winding needed. Its timeflows have no time limit.

  • Passive

chrono_machina:chronosapien_upgrade · palladium:command

Galvan Beam#

A Galvanic Mechamorph beam. Beam Color switches its color.

  • Hold

chrono_machina:chronosapien_upgrade · palladium:energy_beam

Shoulder Bombs#

Fires explosive bombs from the shoulders, in two stages.

  • Key press

chrono_machina:chronosapien_upgrade · palladium:projectile

Drill Hand#

Turns a hand into a drill (Tool Hands) for faster digging, with Drill Buff while it spins.

  • Toggle

chrono_machina:chronosapien_upgrade · palladium:command

Leap#

A big jump forwards.

  • Key press

chrono_machina:chronosapien_upgrade · palladium:command

Super Punch#

Launches a punch at range. Battle Mode switches the Upgrade into its fighting stance.

  • Key press

chrono_machina:chronosapien_upgrade · palladium:projectile

Alliance Across Time#

A special passive for Ben 10 and Ben 23 teaming up against Maltruant. The last entry of the Upgrade skill tree.

  • Passive

chrono_machina:chronosapien_upgrade · palladium:dummy
