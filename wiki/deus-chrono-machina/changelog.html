7.0#

Compatibility#

  • Updated Clockwork's configuration and code for the latest Alien Evolution.

Content and balance#

  • Restored Omnitrix charging (the Recharge Ray) and the Timeout skill (Omnitrix Timeout Ray).
  • Improved the rewind experience.
  • Reworked Maltruant's AI and skills, with new animations and features. He's harder to fight now. The Diamond Champion and full diamond armor are still the best choices against him, and Clockwork can now clash beams with him.
  • Increased the bonuses from Maltruant's components and the Timeclock Gear.
  • Increased Clockwork's base attributes.

Performance#

  • Ray effects and skills cost less to render.
  • Less visual stutter during Time Stop.

Bug fixes#

  • Maltruant's components no longer disappear when you decouple the Omnitrix or remove the transformation.
  • Maltruant's fragments are now used up properly after summoning.
  • Maltruant no longer teleports into walls.
  • Models render again with older Palladium versions.
