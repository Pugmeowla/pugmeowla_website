Clockwork

A Chronosapien, the time-controlling clockwork species. Legendary rarity, with a 1,000-point Temporal Energy meter he winds up himself.

Legendary

The clockwork body doesn't burn or freeze, and he can't run for long: after 5 seconds of sprinting he slows down, and at 8 seconds he has to stop for a moment. Clockwork can't wear some equipment (the slots Alien Evolution locks for big aliens), but he can wear Maltruant's fragments and the Timeclock Key to change how he looks. Worn fragments also raise his stats, and right-clicking a Timeclock Gear as Clockwork boosts a random attribute.

Temporal Energy#

Almost everything Clockwork does runs on the Chronosapien meter, shown on your HUD while you're transformed:

  • It holds 1,000 points. Rays need at least 100 (the Erasure Ray 200); timeflows drain it while they run.
  • Hold right-click with an empty hand to turn the key on his head (Temporal Charge) and fill it.
  • Out of form it slowly refills by itself, 1 point a tick.
  • In the Upgrade fusion the core fills by itself and timeflows have no time limit.

Controls#

Clockwork uses Alien Evolution's ability keys. His powers are split into three groups:

Temporal Arsenal
Picks which temporal ray you fire. The list changes with the timeflow: normally Basic, Enhanced, Erasure, Stasis and Restoration; Slow Time adds Timeout and Reversion; Accelerate Time adds Recharge.
Timeflow
Hold to charge, then let go: Accelerate, Slow, Stop or Rewind. A longer charge lasts longer.
End Timeflow
Ends the running timeflow early.
