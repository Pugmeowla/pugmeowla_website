Skill Tree

Clockwork starts with the Basic Temporal Ray. Everything else is bought with Chronosapien skill points on the Omnitrix's Skills screen.

The tree#

Click an entry (or tab to it and press Enter) to see what it does. Lines show what has to come first.

How unlocking works#

  • Each entry with a line into it costs 1 Chronosapien skill point. Clockwork earns them by levelling up in Alien Evolution, like every other alien.
  • An entry needs every entry that leads into it. The Enhanced Temporal Ray needs both Temporal Reversion and Time Control, so you have to go down both branches.
  • Basic Temporal Ray and Temporal Mainspring are yours from the start. Omnitrix Timeout and Recharge Ray are free too: they open as soon as you have the Enhanced Temporal Ray.
  • Buying the whole tree takes 9 skill points.

The Upgrade fusion has the same tree with the same unlocks, but its last entry is Alliance Across Time instead of Temporal Mainspring.
