The Infinity Gauntlet

Six sockets, every stone at once. How to get the gauntlet and how it works.

Getting a gauntlet#

Infinity Gauntlet

infinity:infinity_gauntlet

Six stone slots. Wear it in the off hand or the Curios hands slot to use every inserted stone at once. Fire resistant.

How to get it
Craftable, or cast with Tinkers' Construct (see JEI). Needs the Infinity Gauntlet Cast.
Rarity
Epic

Infinity Gauntlet Cast

infinity:infinity_gauntlet_cast

Needed to make the Infinity Gauntlet.

How to get it
Village weaponsmith chests (weight 3 of 97 per roll).

Rare

Find an Infinity Gauntlet Cast in a village weaponsmith's chest, then make the gauntlet; the recipe (crafting table or Tinkers' Construct casting) is shown in JEI. The gauntlet doesn't stack, has Epic rarity and is fire resistant.

Wearing it#

The gauntlet gives you the infinity:infinity_gauntlet power, and with it the power of every stone inside, while it's in one of these places:

SlotNotes
Curios hands slotRecommended. Drawn on your arm, in first person too. The Curios visibility (eye) button hides it and its stones.
Off handWorks the same, but isn't visible in first person.
Main handWorks, with its own held model, but leaves your main hand busy; some abilities are designed for the off hand.

If you have more than one, the one giving powers is the off hand gauntlet, then the Curios hands one, then any custom gauntlet you're wearing. When the gauntlet is worn your off arm is shown even while holding nothing in it.

Other things that act as a gauntlet
  • Pugmeowla's Infintrix (with Alien Evolution, Into the Omniverse or Omnitrix Evolution): an equipped Omnitrix works as a gauntlet. Hold a stone and right-click, no Infinity Gauntlet needed. This is a custom gauntlet defined by that addon.
  • Pugmeowla's Herbs of Wakanda: the Black Panther necklace, worn on the chest or in the Curios necklace slot, gives the Infinity Gauntlet power.

Controls#

InputDoes
G (Open Gauntlet)Opens the stone menu of the gauntlet in your main hand, otherwise the one you're wearing (including custom gauntlets). Rebind it under Controls → Infinity Stones.
Right-click with the gauntlet in your main handOpens its stone menu.
Shift-click a stone in your inventory (menu open)Puts it into a free slot: one of the six Infinity Stones goes to its own slot if that's free.
Palladium ability keysUse the stones' abilities and the gauntlet's own (the Snap, skins).

The stone menu and slots#

  • There are six slots, one per knuckle socket. Their default order is Space, Mind, Reality, Power, Time, Soul.
  • Any stone fits any slot. There's no fixed slot per stone; the stone is drawn at whichever socket you put it in.
  • The same stone can't be in two slots, and each slot takes one stone.
  • Anything defined as a stone fits, including stones added by addons (see Custom Stones).
  • The gauntlet you opened from your hand is locked in the hotbar while the menu is open, so you can't move it out from under yourself.
  • Stones are stored on the gauntlet item itself (its NBT), so they travel with it: drop it, store it, hand it to a friend.

Inserting a stone plays the insert sound, puffs particles in the stone's color and plays a short hand animation; removing one plays the removal sound. The stone's power is added or removed straight away.

Time Stone and the Eye

When the Time Stone stops being in your worn gauntlet, the gauntlet runs clear @s infinity:eye_of_agamotto, removing any Eye of Agamotto from your inventory. Keep the Eye somewhere else (a chest) if you move the stone between them.

Tooltip and look#

  • The tooltip lists the stones inside, each in its own color, in a fixed order (Power, Space, Reality, Soul, Time, Mind, then addon stones).
  • The inventory icon colors each socket pixel with the stone in that slot.
  • When worn, held or lying on the ground, each stone is drawn as a glowing cube on its socket. Skins change the gauntlet around the stones; see Gauntlet Skins.
  • On the ground, the gauntlet keeps its stones and skin and is drawn standing up; right-click to pick it up.

The gauntlet's own skill tree#

The infinity:infinity_gauntlet power has a tree too. It holds information entries (use the Curios hands slot, Trinkets for Fabric, Infintrix omnitrixes, the Snap and its aftermath), one entry per skin that applies it, and Return to Original Gauntlet. The Snap abilities appear in the ability bar once all six stones are in.

Trinkets

The mod ships Trinkets data (a glove slot) for a Fabric build, but the current release is Forge only, so use Curios.
