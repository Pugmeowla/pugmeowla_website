Gauntlet Skins

Nineteen ways to wear infinity. Each skin is unlocked by right-clicking with an item.

On this page

Applying a skin#

  1. Wear your Infinity Gauntlet (off hand or Curios hands slot) with no skin on it.
  2. Hold the skin's unlock item in your main hand. The skin's apply ability shows up in the gauntlet's ability bar.
  3. Right-click. One unlock item is used up, the skin is applied and a sound plays (an anvil by default).

The skin is stored on that gauntlet, so it keeps the skin wherever it goes: worn, held, on the ground or in a chest. Each skin also has an entry in the gauntlet's skill tree with its description. If you hold an Infinity Gauntlet in your main hand, skin changes apply to that one instead of the worn one.

A few skins behave differently: the Gauntlet with Fingers applies as soon as you hold its item, the Nano Gauntlet can be applied over the Burnt Gauntlet, and the Fantastic Four Skin is hidden from the ability bar.

Removing a skin#

Return to Original Gauntlet#

Removes the current skin and gives its unlock item back.

  • Key press
  • No unlock needed

Only shown while a skin is applied. The Burnt Gauntlet can't be removed this way while you're still burnt.

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:command

To switch from one skin to another, return to the original gauntlet first, then apply the new skin.

Commands#

/infinity gauntlet skin set <skin> and /infinity gauntlet skin clear change the skin of the gauntlet in your main hand (or your worn one) directly. See Commands.

Making your own#

A skin is one JSON file plus three textures; no Java, models or render layers needed. See Skin Files.
