The Snap

With all six stones in one gauntlet, you can change the world with a snap of your fingers. It will cost you.

Requirements#

  • All six stones in your gauntlet (you have all six stone powers at once).
  • You're not still burnt from a previous Snap.

The four abilities then appear in the gauntlet's ability bar. Each one needs a second press within 3 seconds to go through; the first press only shows a warning such as "Press again to snap away half of all life".

The four abilities#

Kill Half of All Entities#

The Snap: half of the living entities in your dimension turn to dust.

  • Key press
  • No unlock needed
  • Press twice to confirm

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

Press twice within 3 seconds. See The Snap.

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:command

Kill All Hostile#

Snaps every hostile mob in every dimension into a sealed Soulworld cell.

  • Key press
  • No unlock needed
  • Press twice to confirm

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:command

Kill Other Players#

Snaps every other player into the Soulworld cell.

  • Key press
  • No unlock needed
  • Press twice to confirm

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:command

Destroy Stones#

A 7 second ritual that destroys all six stones in the gauntlet.

  • Toggle
  • No unlock needed
  • Press twice to confirm

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:animation_timer

What happens when you snap#

A flash goes off at your hand and a Wither scream and a thunder crack are heard everywhere. Over the next second every target dissolves into white dust, one batch per tick.

SnapWhoWhere they go
Kill HalfHalf of all living entities in your current dimension, players included, except you. It's half of each chunk separately (an odd one out is a coin flip), so no area is spared or wiped out entirely.Killed.
Kill All HostileEvery hostile mob in every dimension: everything in the #infinity:snap_hostile tag (zombies, skeletons, creepers, spiders, endermen, illagers, the Warden, the Wither, the Ender Dragon and more).The Snap cell in the Soulworld.
Kill Other PlayersEvery other player on the server.The Snap cell in the Soulworld.

The Snap cell is a sealed, lit bedrock room at 1000 78 1000 in the Soulworld with a single oak door. Snapped players can walk out and find their way home: see The Soulworld.

Only one Snap can run on a server at a time. If one is already running (or the Soulworld is missing) you'll see "A snap is already in progress or the Soulworld is unavailable."

Destroy Stones#

Turn it on with two presses and you start a 7 second ritual: you're slowed to a standstill, a beacon hum plays, and every second a burst of one stone's color appears with a rising chime. A single press before the end cancels it. When it completes:

  • Every stone in your gauntlet is destroyed.
  • You're burnt (see below) and sent to the Soulworld at Y 72.
  • You get the Peace in our Time advancement.

The burn#

Every Snap and Destroy Stones burns the player who did it:

  • Your max health drops to half a heart (and you're healed to that).
  • Most stone abilities are locked. Only the Space Stone's Teleport GUI and Jump Teleport, and the Power Stone's Radiation Texture Toggle, keep working. You can't snap again.
  • Your arm is drawn burnt, and the gauntlet gets the Burnt Gauntlet skin. If it had another skin, that skin's unlock item is given back to you.
  • It lasts one full Minecraft day (24,000 ticks of game time, 20 minutes). Dying doesn't end it early.

When the day is over your health and abilities come back on their own.

Repairing the gauntlet#

The Nano Gauntlet skin (unlocked with a Palladium Vibranium Flux Capacitor) is the only skin that can be applied over the Burnt Gauntlet while you're burnt. It repairs the look of the gauntlet and your arm, but the half-heart health and locked abilities still last until the day is over.

For server admins#

Operators can trigger every Snap without a gauntlet with /infinity gauntlet snap. The command burns the player who runs it, like the real thing.

Advancements: I Am Inevitable ("What did it cost?") for snapping away half of all life with the gauntlet, and Peace in our Time for destroying the stones.
