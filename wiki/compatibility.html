Configuration#

The mod has one common config file, config/infinity-common.toml:

config/infinity-common.toml
[orb]
	#Number of left/right turns in the Orb's combination lock.
	#Each world gets its own random combination; changing this generates a new one.
	#Range: 1 ~ 64
	combinationLength = 15

Everything else is configured through data: the addon systems, Palladium power files, and tags such as #infinity:snap_hostile and #infinity:projectiles.

Upgrading old worlds#

The mod converts content from earlier versions automatically:

  • Old armor stand props (stones and containers placed on the ground) become ground items when their chunk loads.
  • The old per-stone Cosmi-Rods become a single empty Cosmi-Rod.
  • Old Empty Eye of Agamotto items still work and become the normal Eye.
  • The Orb's "stone taken" state moves from an old marker block on Morag to world data; old force-loaded chunks on Morag and the Soulworld are released.
  • Worlds that already had Vormir built keep it; the Soul Guardian isn't spawned twice.
  • Projectiles stopped by the old Time Stone function are released.

Known limitations#

  • Slow Time and Time Freeze change the tick rate of the whole server.
  • Explorer maps for structures outside the Overworld (Morag Temple, Aether Chamber, Sanctuary) are filled from the Overworld and may stay blank.
  • /infinity gauntlet skin needs no permission level, and clear returns the skin's unlock item.
  • Large Meteor Storms can cause lag.
  • Size Changing, Invisibility scaling and the Intangibility hitbox need Pehkui.
  • Some in-game descriptions are older than the code: the Soul Guardian Telephone sends the Guardian back after one minute, not ten, and Summon Zombie summons six zombies, not seven.
