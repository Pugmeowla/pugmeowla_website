Mind Stone

Control minds, including your own. The yellow stone sits in Loki's Scepter.

Overview#

Mind Stone

infinity:mind_stone
Color
Yellow (#FFD300)
Container
Scepter
Found in
Sanctuary, The End
Power
infinity:mind_stone

You have the Mind Stone's power while holding the stone or the Scepter in either hand, wearing the stone in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, or socketed in a Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Scepter and hit it to split it into the Mind Stone and an Empty Scepter; right-click the Empty Scepter on the ground with the stone to put it back together.

Abilities#

Pacify#

Turns mobs within 5 blocks peaceful towards you. Sneak to undo.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

You and every entity within 5 blocks join the peaceful team, so pacified mobs stop targeting you. Using it while sneaking (Un-Pacify) removes nearby entities from the team again. Pairs well with the Soul Stone's Summon Zombie.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Flight#

Fly freely once unlocked.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Unlocks while the stone comes from the gauntlet, a held Mind Stone or Scepter, the Curios head slot or a Cosmi-Rod. The free Flight Toggle unlock switches it off and on again if you'd rather use another mod's flight.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:flight

Flight Toggle#

Free unlock: clicking it in the skill tree turns Mind Stone flight off or back on.

  • Key press
  • Free unlock

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Intangibility#

Phase through blocks and entities; all damage is negated.

  • Toggle
  • Cooldown 10 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

While on you pass through blocks (vertically too while flying), every hit is negated, and your reach and hitbox are shrunk to almost nothing (via Pehkui). 10 second cooldown after use.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:intangibility

Recipes#

Unlocks every recipe in your recipe book.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Hero of the People#

Permanent Hero of the Village VI.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Night Vision#

Permanent Night Vision.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Mind Illusions#

Creates 3 copies of yourself for 60 seconds.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 20 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Each clone looks exactly like you (skin, armor, held items, render layers and name tag), has 20 health, follows you like a tamed wolf, defends itself and you, and attacks whatever you hit. Mobs targeting you pick randomly between you and nearby clones. Clones vanish after 60 seconds or when you die and never drop anything. 20 second cooldown.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:clones

Mind Reading#

Look at a creature (up to 32 blocks) to see its health, effects, gear, inventory and target.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 1 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Opens a read-only view of the entity: a name tag whose tooltip lists type, health, absorption, armor, owner, current target and (for players) food and level; up to 8 active effects; its six equipment slots; and the inventory of a player, an item-carrying mob (villagers, allays, piglins) or a chested horse. Nothing can be taken out.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:mind_reading

Disguise#

Wear any player's skin and name by typing their username.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 1 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Opens a menu where you type a Minecraft username (3–16 letters, digits or underscores). Everyone sees you with that player's skin and name tag. Reset in the menu returns to your own skin. The disguise is only worn while the ability is unlocked, so losing the stone takes it off.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:disguise

The Scepter's beam#

Scepter Power Blast#

Hold to fire a beam from the Scepter: 20 damage, 50 block range, sets targets on fire.

  • Hold
  • No unlock needed

Available on the Mind Stone's ability bar while the Scepter is in your main hand. A Mind Stone worn in the Curios head slot fires the same beam from the forehead.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:energy_beam

Mechanics#

Pacify and the peaceful team#

Pacify works through a scoreboard team called peaceful, which the mod creates when the world loads. Mobs on your team don't target you. The Soul Stone's Summon Zombie puts its zombies on the same team, so the two stones together give you a loyal undead army.

Clones#

Clones are a separate entity (infinity:clone). Casting Mind Illusions again replaces your current clones. They're never saved with the world, so they're gone after a restart, and they don't count for Soul Storage. Killing a clone also doesn't count as killing a pet for the Best Friend's Soul.

Disguise#

The disguise name is saved on your player and synced to everyone, along with your name tag. It needs the username to be a valid Minecraft name; the skin itself is fetched like any player skin.
