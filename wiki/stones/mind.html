Mind Stone

Flight, illusions, mind reading and disguises. The yellow stone sits in Loki's Scepter.

Overview#

Mind Stone

infinity:mind_stone
Color
Yellow (#FFD300)
Container
Scepter
Found in
Sanctuary, The End
Power
infinity:mind_stone

You get the Mind Stone's power by holding the stone or the Scepter in either hand, wearing the stone in the Curios head slot, putting it in your gauntlet, or putting it in a Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Scepter and hit it to split it into the Mind Stone and an Empty Scepter. Right-click the Empty Scepter on the ground with the stone to put it back together.

Abilities#

Pacify#

Mobs within 5 blocks stop attacking you. Sneak and use it to undo.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

You and everything within 5 blocks join the peaceful scoreboard team, so those mobs leave you alone. Use it while sneaking (Un-Pacify) to take nearby mobs off the team again. Works well with the Soul Stone's Summon Zombie.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Flight#

Fly.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Works when the stone comes from the gauntlet, a held Mind Stone or Scepter, the Curios head slot or a Cosmi-Rod. If you'd rather use another mod's flight, the free Flight Toggle turns it off and on.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:flight

Flight Toggle#

Free. Click it in the skill tree to turn Mind Stone flight off or back on.

  • Key press
  • Free unlock

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Intangibility#

Walk through blocks and mobs. Nothing can hurt you while it is on.

  • Toggle
  • Cooldown 10 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

You pass through blocks (up and down too while flying) and every hit is cancelled. Your reach and hitbox shrink to almost nothing while it's on, which uses Pehkui. 10 second cooldown afterwards.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:intangibility

Recipes#

Unlocks every recipe in your recipe book.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Hero of the People#

Permanent Hero of the Village VI.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Night Vision#

Permanent Night Vision.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Mind Illusions#

Three copies of you for 60 seconds.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 20 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

The clones look exactly like you, with your skin, armor, held items, render layers and name tag. Each has 20 health. They follow you like a tamed wolf, fight back, and attack whatever you hit. Mobs after you pick randomly between you and the clones near you. They vanish after 60 seconds or when you die and never drop anything. 20 second cooldown.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:clones

Mind Reading#

Look at a mob or player (up to 32 blocks) to see its health, effects, gear, inventory and target.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 1 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Opens a view you can't take anything out of. The top row has a name tag whose tooltip shows the type, health, absorption, armor, owner and current target (plus food and level for players), followed by up to 8 active effects. Below that are the six equipment slots, then the inventory of a player, a mob that carries items (villagers, allays, piglins) or a horse with a chest.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:mind_reading

Disguise#

Type a username and wear that player's skin and name.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 1 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

The name can be 3 to 16 letters, digits or underscores. Everyone sees you with that player's skin and name tag. Reset puts your own skin back. The disguise is only worn while the ability is unlocked, so losing the stone takes it off.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:disguise

The Scepter's beam#

Scepter Power Blast#

Hold to fire a beam from the Scepter. 20 damage, 50 blocks, sets things on fire.

  • Hold
  • No unlock needed

Shows up in the Mind Stone's ability bar while the Scepter is in your main hand. A Mind Stone in the Curios head slot fires the same beam from your forehead.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:energy_beam

How some of it works#

Pacify and the peaceful team#

Pacify uses a scoreboard team called peaceful that the mod makes when the world loads. Mobs on your team don't target you. The Soul Stone's Summon Zombie puts its zombies on the same team, so with both stones you get a zombie army that's on your side.

Clones#

Clones are their own entity, infinity:clone. Casting Mind Illusions again replaces the ones you have. They aren't saved with the world, so a restart gets rid of them, and they don't give souls. Killing one also doesn't count as killing a pet for the Best Friend's Soul.

Disguise#

The name you pick is saved on you and sent to everyone, along with your name tag. It has to be a valid Minecraft name. The skin is loaded the same way as any player skin.
