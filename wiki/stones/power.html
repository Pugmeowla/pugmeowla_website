Power Stone

Raw destructive energy that doubles with every level. Handle with care.

Overview#

Power Stone

infinity:power_stone
Color
Purple (#C32AD1)
Container
Orb
Found in
Morag Temple, Morag
Power
infinity:power_stone

You have the Power Stone's power while holding it, wearing it in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, or socketed in a Cosmi-Rod. Unlike the other containers, the Orb does not give you the stone's power: you have to crack its lock and take the stone out. There is only one Power Stone per world.

Cosmic radiation

Holding the bare Power Stone in your main or off hand, without it also being in your gauntlet, head slot or a Cosmi-Rod, is unprotected. After half a second the stone overloads: you get Wither, you're rooted in place, a cyclone of energy fires rockets around you, and radiation cracks spread over your skin. Move it somewhere protected as soon as you can.

Abilities#

Energy Blast#

Fires a magenta energy bolt. Damage: 3 × 2^level.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.25 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

A glowing projectile that damages the first entity it hits. Its damage doubles with every Power Stone level: 3 at level 0, 6144 at level 11, and infinite at full power.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:energy_blast

Knockback Resistance#

Full knockback resistance.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Energy Blast

infinity:power_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

Power Blast#

Hold to fire a 100-block beam that smelts blocks and starts fires.

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Energy Blast

Damage is 2^level. At full power (level 11 with all six stones) the beam turns rainbow and causes an explosion (power 4) wherever it hits a block, every tick. Fired from the off hand.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:power_blast

Power Levels 1–11#

Eleven purchasable levels. Each one doubles your base attack damage.

  • Upgrade
  • 434 Aspects of Existence

Buy the levels in order (2, 4, 8, 12, 18, 26, 36, 48, 64, 88 and 128 Aspects of Existence; 434 in total), then use Damage Control to pick your active level. See Power Levels.

infinity:power_stone · palladium:dummy (×11)

Damage Control#

Toggle on, then scroll up/down to set your active power level.

  • Scroll
  • No unlock needed

Requires Power Levels 1–11

The level can't go above the highest level you've bought. The purple bar shows the current level (0–11). While the Power Stone is active your base attack damage is set to 2^level; it goes back to 1 when the stone is removed.

infinity:power_stone · palladium:command + infinity:power_level_change

Cosmic Explosion#

Controls the stone’s raw energy: unlocks Rocket Burst and Power Cyclone.

  • Upgrade
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Power Blast

infinity:power_stone · palladium:dummy

Rocket Burst#

Below full power: launches five homing rockets.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 5 s
  • No unlock needed

Requires Cosmic Explosion

Rockets explode on impact and never hurt you (you're immune to explosions while it fires). Their damage scales with level ÷ 11, so they do no damage at level 0. Replaced by Power Cyclone at level 11. 5 second cooldown.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:rocket_burst

Power Cyclone#

At level 11 only: hold to become a cyclone of energy that fires a rocket every second.

  • Hold
  • No unlock needed

Requires Cosmic Explosion

You're rooted in place, immune to explosions, and the stone's radiation spreads over your skin without harm (the overlay can be turned off with Radiation Texture Toggle).

infinity:power_stone · infinity:power_cyclone

Meteor Storm#

Shatters a moon and drops the fragments on the spot you look at.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Cosmic Explosion

Needs stones Space Stone

Needs the Space Stone as well (from the gauntlet, a held Tesseract or Space Stone, the head slot or a Cosmi-Rod). Targets up to 256 blocks away. The moon's size is 1 + your power level. One storm per player at a time; very large storms can lag.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:meteor_storm

Radiation Texture Toggle#

Free: clicking it in the skill tree turns the radiation skin overlay off or on.

  • Key press
  • Free unlock

Levels in short#

The Power Stone has a level from 0 to 11, stored in the DamageCycle scoreboard objective and shown on the purple bar. You raise the maximum by buying levels in the skill tree and pick the current level with Damage Control and the mouse wheel. While the stone is active your base attack damage is 2level, and Energy Blast, Power Blast, Rocket Burst and Meteor Storm all scale with it. At level 11 with all six stones the level counts as maxed: damage becomes infinite. Full table: Power Levels.

Radiation overlay#

The glowing cracks drawn on your skin during Power Cyclone (and when the stone overloads) can be switched off with the free Radiation Texture Toggle unlock. It still works while you're burnt from the Snap.
