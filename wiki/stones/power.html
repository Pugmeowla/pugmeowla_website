Overview#

Power Stone

infinity:power_stone
Color
Purple (#C32AD1)
Container
Orb
Found in
Morag Temple, Morag
Power
infinity:power_stone

You get the Power Stone's power by holding it, wearing it in the Curios head slot, putting it in your gauntlet, or putting it in a Cosmi-Rod. The Orb is different from the other containers: it does not give you the power. You have to unlock it and take the stone out.

Cosmic radiation

Holding the Power Stone in your main or off hand, when it isn't also in your gauntlet, head slot or a Cosmi-Rod, isn't safe. After half a second it becomes unstable and explodes: you can't move, a cyclone of energy fires rockets around you, and radiation cracks spread over your skin. You should quickly contain the stone.

Abilities#

Energy Blast#

A magenta energy bolt whose damage scales with your power level.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.25 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Hits the first thing in its path. The damage scales with your power level on the purple bar.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:energy_blast

Knockback Resistance#

Nothing knocks you back.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Energy Blast

infinity:power_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

Power Blast#

Hold to fire a 100 block beam that smelts blocks and starts fires.

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Energy Blast

Does 2^level damage. At full power (level 11 with all six stones) it turns rainbow and explodes (power 4) wherever it hits a block. Fired from your off hand.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:power_blast

Power Levels 1 to 11#

Eleven levels you buy one by one. Each doubles your base attack damage.

  • Upgrade
  • 434 Aspects of Existence

They cost 2, 4, 8, 12, 18, 26, 36, 48, 64, 88 and 128 Aspects of Existence, 434 in total. Use Damage Control to pick which level is active. Full table on Power Levels.

infinity:power_stone

Damage Control#

Turn it on, then scroll to set your active power level.

  • Scroll
  • No unlock needed

Requires Power Levels 1 to 11

You can't go above the highest level you've bought. The purple bar shows the level (0 to 11). While you have the Power Stone your base attack damage is 2^level, and it goes back to 1 when you lose the stone.

infinity:power_stone · palladium:command + infinity:power_level_change

Cosmic Explosion#

You can control the raw energy now. Unlocks Rocket Burst and Power Cyclone.

  • Upgrade
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Power Blast

infinity:power_stone · palladium:dummy

Rocket Burst#

Below level 11, fires five homing rockets.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 5 s
  • No unlock needed

Requires Cosmic Explosion

The rockets explode when they hit and never hurt you, and you're immune to explosions while they fire. Damage scales with level ÷ 11, so they do nothing at level 0. At level 11 this becomes Power Cyclone. 5 second cooldown.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:rocket_burst

Power Cyclone#

Level 11 only. Hold to turn into a cyclone of energy that fires a rocket every second.

  • Hold
  • No unlock needed

Requires Cosmic Explosion

You can't move, explosions don't hurt you, and radiation cracks spread over your skin without harming you. The cracks can be hidden with Radiation Texture Toggle.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:power_cyclone

Meteor Storm#

Breaks a moon apart and drops the pieces where you are looking.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Cosmic Explosion

Needs stones Space Stone

Needs the Space Stone too, from the gauntlet, a held Tesseract or Space Stone, the head slot or a Cosmi-Rod. Reaches up to 256 blocks. The moon is 1 + your power level blocks across. You can only have one storm going at a time, and big ones can lag.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:meteor_storm

Radiation Texture Toggle#

Free. Click it in the skill tree to hide or show the radiation cracks on your skin.

  • Skill tree switch
  • Free unlock

infinity:power_stone · infinity:toggle

Levels#

The Power Stone has a level from 0 to 11. It's kept in the DamageCycle scoreboard objective and shown on the purple bar while you have the stone. Buy the levels in the tree one after another, then pick the one you want:

  1. Turn on Damage Control in the ability bar.
  2. Scroll up to go up a level and down to go down. You can't go past the highest level you've bought.

Radiation cracks#

The glowing cracks on your skin during Power Cyclone (or when the stone overloads) can be turned off with the free Radiation Texture Toggle. It still works while you're burnt from the Snap.
