Reality Stone

Rewrite the world block by block. The red stone hides inside the Aether.

Overview#

Reality Stone

infinity:reality_stone
Color
Red (#FF0130)
Container
Aether
Found in
Aether Chamber, Svartalfheim
Power
infinity:reality_stone

You have the Reality Stone's power while holding the stone or the Aether in either hand, wearing the stone in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, or socketed in a Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Aether and hit it to release the stone. To put it back, hold the stone in your off hand and use Insert Reality Stone with an Empty Aether, or right-click an Empty Aether lying on the ground while holding the stone.

Abilities#

Block Saving#

Unlocks Copy Block and Paste Block.

  • Upgrade
  • 128 Aspects of Existence

Copy Block (key press) saves the block you look at, up to 30 blocks away (not unbreakable blocks). Paste Block (key press) turns the block you look at into your saved block; while sneaking it changes every non-air block within a radius of 5. Paste respects build permissions and claim protection. The saved block is kept through death and relogging and shown as the Paste ability's icon.

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:dummy

Size Changing#

Unlocks Size Control: toggle it, then scroll to grow up to 5× or shrink down to 0.1×.

  • Scroll
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Turn on Size Control from the ability bar, then scroll: scrolling down shrinks you in steps (0.9×, 0.8× … 0.1×) and scrolling up grows you (2×, 3×, 4×, 5×; movement scales with size when growing). Turning Size Control off resets you to normal size. Uses Pehkui.

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:dummy + palladium:command

Invisibility#

True invisibility: no body, no armor, no name.

  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:invisibility

Change the Weather#

Hold to open a wheel and pick Clear, Rain or Thunder.

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:ability_wheel

Size Changing steps#

While Size Control is on, each scroll moves one step. The height and width scales applied through Pehkui:

DirectionSteps
Scroll up (grow)2× → 3× → 4× → 5× (movement speed scales too)
Normal1×
Scroll down (shrink)0.9× → 0.8× → 0.7× → 0.6× → 0.5× → 0.4× → 0.3× → 0.2× → 0.1×
Pehkui

Size Changing, and the scaling part of Invisibility, use Pehkui's /scale command. Without Pehkui installed these do nothing.

Copy and paste details#

  • Copy works up to 30 blocks away and refuses unbreakable blocks.
  • Paste replaces the looked-at block, or every non-air block in a sphere of radius 5 while sneaking.
  • Paste checks build permissions and protection, so it won't grief claimed land, and never touches unbreakable blocks.
  • Your saved block survives death and relogging, and the Paste ability's icon shows it.
