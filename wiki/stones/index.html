Using a stone's power#

Each stone is a Palladium power: infinity:space_stone, infinity:mind_stone, infinity:reality_stone, infinity:power_stone, infinity:time_stone and infinity:soul_stone. You have the power while any of these is true:

SourceWhich stonesNotes
Holding the bare stone (either hand)All sixThe Power Stone is dangerous to hold this way.
Holding its container (either hand)Space (Tesseract), Mind (Scepter), Reality (Aether), Time (Eye of Agamotto, while the stone is in it)The Orb doesn't give the Power Stone's power.
Wearing the Eye of AgamottoTimeChest slot or Curios necklace, while the stone is in it.
Curios head slotAll sixRequires Curios.
Infinity Gauntlet or a custom gauntletEvery inserted stoneThe worn gauntlet (off hand / Curios hands) or the main hand.
Cosmi-Rod with the stone socketedAny one stoneHeld in either hand.

Abilities you unlock are saved on you, not on the stone. If you lose the source, the power goes away; get it back and your unlocks are still there. Power-hungry abilities like flight check the source again when you use them.

With all six stones in your gauntlet you also get the Six Stones, One Gauntlet advancement.

Things every stone does#

  • On the ground: a dropped stone lies still on the ground, can't be destroyed, burnt, blown up or despawn, and rests at the bottom of the world instead of falling out. Right-click to pick it up.
  • No duplicates: while a player carries a stone, copies of it lying on the ground disappear.
  • One Power Stone and one Time Stone per world: the Orb and the Eye of Agamotto each remember, for the whole world, whether their stone has been taken out.
  • Look: every stone is LucraftCore's stone cube tinted with its color, unlit, with two scrolling glints. The same look is used in the inventory, in the gauntlet and on the ground.
