Soul Stone

A soul for a soul. The orange stone waits on Vormir, and its price is something you love.

Overview#

Soul Stone

infinity:soul_stone

Orange (#DE7300)
Container
None
Found on
Vormir
Power
infinity:soul_stone

You have the Soul Stone's power while holding it, wearing it in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, or socketed in a Cosmi-Rod.

Obtaining it#

  1. Kill one of your own tamed pets. The first time you do, you get a Best Friend's Soul ("How Could You").
  2. Travel to Vormir with the Space Stone's Teleport GUI (dimension infinity:vormir, around X 212, Z 99).
  3. Trade the Best Friend's Soul to the Soul Guardian for the Soul Stone.

Details about the Soul Guardian and its other trades are on Finding the Stones.

Abilities#

Soul Storage#

Every entity you kill stores one soul, up to 50 (orange bar).

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Clones and mobs whose soul was already drained don't count. See Soul Storage.

infinity:soul_stone · infinity:soul_storage

Soul Heal#

Spend 1 soul to restore 2 hearts.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.5 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Soul Storage

infinity:soul_stone · infinity:soul_heal

Soul Drain#

Rip the soul out of the mob you look at (8 blocks), leaving it standing still in greyscale.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.5 s
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Soul Heal

Stores 1 soul. Used while sneaking it gives a drained mob its soul back for 1 soul. Players, bosses (#forge:bosses), clones and mobs that already have no AI can't be drained. The drained state is saved on the mob.

infinity:soul_stone · infinity:soul_drain

Glow#

Hold to see every entity within 100 blocks outlined in orange.

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:entity_glow

Locate Entity Command#

Lets you use /infinity soul locateplayer .

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Glow

infinity:soul_stone · infinity:locate_player

Summon Zombie#

Summons six zombies that join your peaceful team.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Three named zombies (Transfer, Pugmeowla and Dark Zombie) and three plain ones. You and the zombies join the peaceful team, so they won't attack you.

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command

Enter / Exit Soulworld#

Takes you and everything within 5 blocks into the Soulworld; use again to return.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Everyone arrives at Y 72 above the same X/Z. Each entity remembers where it came from, so Exit Soulworld (shown while you're inside) sends them all back to their original spot.

infinity:soul_stone · infinity:pocket_dimension_enter / exit

Saturation#

You never get hungry.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command

Effect Immunity#

Clears most negative effects (poison, wither, slowness, blindness…) every tick.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command

Regeneration#

Permanent, extremely strong Regeneration.

  • Passive
  • 16 Aspects of Existence

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command

Health#

Nearly infinite max health.

  • Passive
  • 64 Aspects of Existence

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

Immortality#

Complete immortality. Needs all six stones.

  • Passive
  • 128 Aspects of Existence

Requires Health

Needs stones Space Stone Mind Stone Reality Stone Power Stone Time Stone Soul Stone

All damage is negated before it lands (shown with soul particles), including damage that bypasses invulnerability.

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:immortality + infinity:invulnerability

The Soulworld#

Enter Soulworld takes you, and everything within 5 blocks, to infinity:soulworld: an endless orange plain of smooth red sandstone under a layer of water. Everyone arrives at Y 72 above the same X and Z. Use the same ability slot again (it becomes Exit Soulworld) to send everyone back to exactly where they were. More on Dimensions.

When the defenses switch off#

  • After the Snap: while you're burnt, Health, Regeneration and Immortality (like most stone abilities) are locked.
  • Powerborne's Stormbreaker: while you wear the Infinity Gauntlet and a thrown Stormbreaker from the Powerborne addon is within 10 blocks of you, Saturation, Effect Immunity, Regeneration, Health and Immortality are locked and you take 1 damage every tick. See Compatibility.
