Space Stone

Bend distance itself. The blue stone is your ticket to every other world in the mod.

Overview#

Space Stone

infinity:space_stone
Color
Blue (#0255FF)
Container
Tesseract
Found in
Norse Village, Overworld
Power
infinity:space_stone

You have the Space Stone's power while holding the stone or the Tesseract in either hand, wearing the stone in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, or socketed in a Cosmi-Rod. Get it out of the Tesseract by dropping the Tesseract and hitting it.

Get this stone first: its Teleport GUI is how you reach Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim.

Abilities#

Force Field#

A shield bubble that blocks attacks but roots you in place.

  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

While the field is up you get a huge armor and armor toughness bonus, a shield is drawn around you and you can't walk, strafe or jump. Hits from entities dealing less than 23 damage are blocked outright, and hits from Iron Golems, Wardens, Vindicators, Piglin Brutes and Zombified Piglins are always blocked. Damage without an attacker (falling, fire, suffocation) still gets through.

infinity:space_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier + infinity:force_field

Jump Teleport#

Opens a short-lived portal to the block you are looking at and jumps you through.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Force Field

Opens a pair of portals, one in front of you and one at the block you look at (up to 256 blocks away), and lands you on safe ground next to that block. The pair closes after 3 seconds; anything can walk through until then. Still usable while burnt by the Snap.

infinity:space_stone · infinity:jump_portal

Teleport GUI#

Pick any dimension and coordinates, then open a linked portal pair.

  • Key press
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Force Field

Opens the Space Teleport menu with a dimension field (with suggestions for every loaded dimension) and X / Y / Z fields. Open Portal creates a linked pair: one portal 2.5 blocks in front of you and one at the destination. The portals stay open until someone hits one of them, which closes both. This is the main way to reach the mod's dimensions without a space mod. Still usable while burnt by the Snap.

infinity:space_stone · infinity:portal

Telekinesis#

Pick up an entity or block, move it around and throw it.

  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Force Field

Turning it on grabs the entity or solid block you look at (up to 32 blocks away) and holds it in front of you. Scroll to move it between 2 and 24 blocks away, left-click to throw it (it damages whatever it hits), or toggle off to set it down; blocks are placed back. Unbreakable blocks and blocks with block entities (chests, furnaces…) can't be picked up.

infinity:space_stone · infinity:telekinesis

Spatial Lock#

Right-click while holding something with Telekinesis to freeze it in space.

  • Upgrade
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Telekinesis

An upgrade for Telekinesis. While holding something, right-click to lock it where it is: it can't move, fall or be knocked back (a held block stays floating) until you pick it up with Telekinesis again. The lock is saved with the world.

infinity:space_stone · infinity:spatial_lock

Black Hole#

Summon a growing black hole that follows your aim, then throw it.

  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Teleport GUI

Needs stones Power Stone

Requires both the Space Stone and the Power Stone. Turning it on summons a black hole in front of you that grows, follows your aim and pulls in entities and blocks. Left-click to throw it; it collapses on its own later. Toggling off before throwing collapses it immediately.

infinity:space_stone · infinity:black_hole

Water Breathing#

Breathe underwater.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:space_stone · palladium:command

Fall Damage Immunity#

Negates all fall damage.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:space_stone · palladium:damage_immunity

Speed#

Adds 0.05 to your movement speed attribute (+50% for a player).

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:space_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

Step Assist#

Walk up full blocks without jumping.

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Speed

infinity:space_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

How portals behave#

  • A portal is a pair: one end opens 2.5 blocks in front of you, the other at the destination. Each end grows open over 3 seconds.
  • Anything that walks into either end, players and mobs alike, comes out of the other one on safe ground. Each entity has a one second cooldown so it doesn't bounce back.
  • Teleport GUI portals stay open until someone hits one end; both ends then close. The chunks at both ends stay loaded while the pair is open.
  • Jump Teleport portals close on their own after 3 seconds. If no block is in range you get the message "No block found within range for the portal."
  • The menu's background, the portals' color, size, and a fire style (an orange ring of flames like Doctor Strange's sling ring) are configurable by addons: see Ability Reference.

Telekinesis controls#

InputWhile holding something
Ability keyToggle off: set it down (blocks are placed back where possible).
Mouse wheelMove it closer or further (2–24 blocks).
Left-clickThrow it. Faster, bigger things hurt more when they hit.
Right-clickSpatial Lock it in place (after unlocking Spatial Lock).

A held block becomes a floating block entity. If it can't be placed back it drops as an item after 30 seconds in the air. Picking up a spatially locked target frees it.

Tips#

  • Force Field roots you in place: use it to tank a fight, not to escape one. Jump Teleport is the escape.
  • Black Hole and Meteor Storm both need the Power Stone too. With both stones in a gauntlet you get all of them.
  • The Teleport GUI and Jump Teleport keep working after the Snap, when the other stone abilities are locked.
