Time Stone

Speed the world up, slow it down, or stop it entirely. The green stone rests in the Eye of Agamotto.

Overview#

Time Stone

infinity:time_stone
Color
Green (#13CF55)
Container
Eye of Agamotto
Found in
Sanctum Sanctorum, Overworld
Power
infinity:time_stone

You have the Time Stone's power while holding it, wearing it in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, socketed in a Cosmi-Rod, or while an Eye of Agamotto that still holds the stone is held, worn on your chest or in the Curios necklace slot. There is only one Time Stone per world: see the Eye of Agamotto for taking it out.

Whenever a time power is running, your arm is raised, green time rings are drawn on you and a green tint covers your screen (switch the tint off with the free Screen Toggle).

Abilities#

Fast Forward Daylight Cycle#

Hold to race through the day (+100 ticks of daytime every tick).

  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command

Stop Projectile#

Projectiles that come within 2.5 blocks freeze in mid-air.

  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

Arrows, spectral arrows, tridents, ender pearls, snowballs, eyes of ender, fireballs, eggs and experience bottles (the #infinity:projectiles tag) hang in the air for 50 seconds, then carry on with their original motion. They stay stopped through a world reload.

infinity:time_stone · infinity:projectile_stop

Time Command#

Lets you use /infinity time set .

  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

See /infinity time set. The world always moves forward to the requested time of day.

infinity:time_stone · infinity:time_set

Daylight Cycle Toggle#

Free: clicking it flips the doDaylightCycle game rule.

  • Key press
  • Free unlock

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command

Slow Time#

Unlocks Change Flow of Time: toggle it on, then scroll to change the server tick rate.

  • Scroll
  • 5 Aspects of Existence

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

While Change Flow of Time is on, scrolling down slows the world (each step removes 4 ticks per second, down to 2 TPS) and scrolling up speeds it up (each step adds 4, up to 40 TPS). The green bar shows the step; the middle is normal speed. Your step is remembered between uses and turning it off returns the server to 20 TPS. You keep moving at normal speed. This affects the whole server.

infinity:time_stone · infinity:time_flow

Freeze Time#

Unlocks Time Freeze: pauses the entire world while you keep moving.

  • Toggle
  • 10 Aspects of Existence

Requires Slow Time

Press once to freeze time and again to resume. Everything except you stops; you can also walk on water while time is frozen. Affects the whole server.

infinity:time_stone · infinity:time_freeze

Screen Toggle#

Free: turns the green screen overlay shown while using time powers off or on.

  • Key press
  • Free unlock

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command

Slow Time steps#

Change Flow of Time changes the server's tick rate through Chrono API. Each step is 4 ticks per second:

Step−5−4−3−2−1012345
Ticks per second4036322824201612842

Scroll down to slow time (positive steps) and up to speed it up. The green bar shows the step, with the middle being normal speed. While time is slowed you get hidden Speed and Haste so you keep up, and you're whitelisted from the tick-rate change.

Server-wide

Change Flow of Time and Time Freeze affect the whole server, every player included, until you turn them off. On a shared server, agree on it first.

The time command#

With Time Command unlocked and the Time Stone active, /infinity time set <time> works for you without operator rights. It always moves the world forward to the requested time of day, so setting 1000 at 13000 skips to the next morning. See Commands.
