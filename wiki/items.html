Thanos armor#

Two full sets, Classic (comics) and MCU, each with a helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. Both are netherite-material armor with custom Palladium render layers, and the Classic chestplate hides your own body parts so the armor model replaces them. They're in the creative tab; there's no survival recipe.

Fancy Bottle#

Hold a Fancy Bottle in your main hand and it grants a small power whose single entry costs 3 experience levels. Buying it uses up the Fancy Bottle and gives you a Bottle o' Enchanting, so you can store experience.

Materials#

The mod adds five metal families, each with dust, ingot, nugget and plate forms (gold-titanium also has a handle, uru a blade), plus iron, gold and coal dusts and plates. They go into the gauntlet, the swords, the Soul Guardian Telephone and skin unlock items. Titanium Ingot Steel Ingot Gold-Titanium Alloy Ingot Dwarf Star Alloy Ingot Uru Ingot

  • Uru: mine Uru Ore in meteor craters on Svartalfheim.
  • Dwarf Star Alloy: from Dwarf Star Meteor blocks in Overworld meteor craters.
  • Titanium pairs with Palladium's titanium; titanium, gold-titanium alloy and ferro-titanium-gold alloy also exist as Tinkers' Construct materials.
  • The Hammer used for plates and dusts is given back to you after crafting.

Blocks#

Storage blocks for each metal, the ores (Uru Ore, Dwarf Star Meteor), the terrain blocks of the alien worlds (Vormir Sand, Vormir Stone, Morag Stone, Svartelheim Stone, Svartelheim Sand) and the Temple Beacon found in Infinity Temples. Vormir Stone is very hard (7.5) and needs the right tool to drop.

Creative tabs#

Items are split over two creative tabs: Infinity Stone Core (stones, containers, gauntlet, Cosmi-Rod, swords, armor, special items) and Infinity Stone Core Resources (hammer, materials and blocks). The Temple Beacon and molten buckets aren't in a tab.
