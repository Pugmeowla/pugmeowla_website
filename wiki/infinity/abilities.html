Power Stone#

Power Stone · See the Power Stone page for its skill tree.

Energy Blast#

A magenta energy bolt whose damage scales with your power level.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.25 s
  • 2 Aspects of Existence

Hits the first thing in its path. The damage scales with your power level on the purple bar.

It leaves from the stone: your arm snaps up and it fires from the hand with the gauntlet or the stone, from the top of a Cosmi-Rod's staff, or from the middle of an open Orb, and still goes where your crosshair is.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:energy_blast

Knockback Resistance#

Nothing knocks you back.

  • Passive
  • 3 Aspects of Existence

Requires Energy Blast

infinity:power_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier

Power Blast#

Hold to fire a 100 block beam that smelts blocks and starts fires.

  • Hold
  • 6 Aspects of Existence

Requires Energy Blast

Does 2^level damage. At full power (level 11 with all six stones) it turns rainbow and explodes (power 4) wherever it hits a block. Fired from your off hand.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:power_blast

Power Levels 1 to 11#

Eleven levels you buy one by one. Each doubles your base attack damage.

  • Upgrade
  • 434 Aspects of Existence

They cost 2, 4, 8, 12, 18, 26, 36, 48, 64, 88 and 128 Aspects of Existence, 434 in total. Use Damage Control to pick which level is active. Full table on Power Levels.

infinity:power_stone

Damage Control#

Turn it on, then scroll to set your active power level.

  • Scroll
  • No unlock needed

Requires Power Levels 1 to 11

You can't go above the highest level you've bought. The purple bar shows the level (0 to 11). While you have the Power Stone your base attack damage is 2^level, and it goes back to 1 when you lose the stone.

infinity:power_stone · palladium:command + infinity:power_level_change

Super Punch#

While Damage Control is on, right-click to punch a shockwave that flings everything in its path and smashes blocks.

  • Right-click
  • Cooldown 2 s
  • No unlock needed

The punch travels 12 blocks, hitting everything within 2 blocks of its path once and breaking the blocks it passes through (never unbreakable ones). It does 3 damage, doubled for every power level and infinite at full power, and the kick pushes you back harder the higher your level. It only works while Damage Control is switched on: turn Damage Control off and right-click goes back to normal. 2 second cooldown.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:super_punch

Cosmic Explosion#

You can control the raw energy now. Unlocks Rocket Burst and Power Cyclone.

  • Upgrade
  • 12 Aspects of Existence

Requires Power Blast

infinity:power_stone · palladium:dummy

Rocket Burst#

Below level 11, fires five homing rockets.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 5 s
  • No unlock needed

Requires Cosmic Explosion

The rockets explode when they hit and never hurt you, and you're immune to explosions while they fire. Damage scales with level ÷ 11, so they do nothing at level 0. At level 11 this becomes Power Cyclone. 5 second cooldown.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:rocket_burst

Power Cyclone#

Level 11 only. Hold to turn into a cyclone of energy that fires a rocket every second.

  • Hold
  • No unlock needed

Requires Cosmic Explosion

You can't move, explosions don't hurt you, and radiation cracks spread over your skin without harming you. The cracks can be hidden with Radiation Texture Toggle.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:power_cyclone

Meteor Storm#

Breaks a moon apart and drops the pieces where you are looking.

  • Key press
  • 32 Aspects of Existence

Requires Cosmic Explosion

Needs stones Space Stone

Needs the Space Stone too, from the gauntlet, a held Tesseract or Space Stone, the head slot or a Cosmi-Rod. Reaches up to 256 blocks. The moon is 1 + your power level blocks across. You can only have one storm going at a time, and big ones can lag.

infinity:power_stone · infinity:meteor_storm

Space Stone#

Space Stone · See the Space Stone page for its skill tree.

Force Field#

A shield bubble that blocks attacks but stops you moving.

  • Toggle
  • 4 Aspects of Existence

While it's up you get a huge armor and toughness boost, a shield is drawn around you, and you can't walk, strafe or jump. Hits from anything dealing less than 23 damage are blocked. Iron Golems, Wardens, Vindicators, Piglin Brutes and Zombified Piglins are always blocked. Damage with no attacker (falling, fire, suffocation) still hurts.

infinity:space_stone · palladium:attribute_modifier + infinity:force_field

Jump Teleport#

Opens a quick portal to the block you are looking at and jumps you through.

  • Key press
  • 6 Aspects of Existence

Requires Force Field

One portal opens in front of you and one at the block you're looking at, up to 256 blocks away. You land on safe ground next to that block. Both close after 3 seconds and anything can use them until then. With Immersive Portals they're real portals you walk through yourself, and they stay open for 7 seconds. Still works while you're burnt from the Snap.

infinity:space_stone · infinity:jump_portal

Teleport GUI#

Type a dimension and coordinates and open a portal there.

  • Key press
  • 10 Aspects of Existence

Requires Force Field

Opens the Space Teleport menu. The dimension field suggests every dimension the server has, and you fill in X, Y and Z. Open Portal puts one portal 2.5 blocks in front of you and the other at the destination. They stay open until someone left-clicks the rim of one, which closes both. This is how you get to the mod's planets without a rocket. Still works while you're burnt from the Snap.

With Immersive Portals installed, an open portal shows the other side through it and you walk straight through, from either side. See Immersive Portals.

infinity:space_stone · infinity:portal

Telekinesis#

Pick up a mob or a block, move it around and throw it.

  • Toggle
  • 8 Aspects of Existence

Requires Force Field

Turn it on while looking at an entity or solid block (up to 32 blocks away) and it floats in front of you. Scroll to move it between 2 and 24 blocks away and left-click to throw it. Thrown things act like a projectile, they hurt whatever they hit. Turn it off to put it down, and blocks realign to the grid and are placed back down. Unbreakable blocks and blocks with block entities (chests, furnaces and so on) can't be picked up.

infinity:space_stone · infinity:telekinesis

Spatial Lock#

Right-click while holding something with Telekinesis to freeze it in place.

  • Upgrade
  • 6 Aspects of Existence

Requires Telekinesis

Locked things can't move, fall or be knocked back. A held block stays floating. Pick it up with Telekinesis again to free it. Locks are saved with the world.

infinity:space_stone · infinity:spatial_lock

Black Hole#

Summon a black hole that follows your aim, then throw it.

  • Toggle

Requires Teleport GUI

Needs stones Power Stone

Needs the Power Stone as well. The black hole grows in front of you, follows where you look and pulls in mobs and blocks. Left-click throws it and it collapses by itself a bit later. Turning it off before you throw it collapses it straight away.

Reality Stone#

Reality Stone · See the Reality Stone page for its skill tree.

Block Saving#

Unlocks Copy Block and Paste Block.

  • Upgrade
  • 64 Aspects of Existence

Copy Block saves the full block you're looking at, up to 30 blocks away, looking through grass, flowers and other partial blocks. Only full blocks can be saved, so you can't paste floating grass or wheat off farmland, and unbreakable blocks can't be copied. Paste Block turns the block you're looking at into your saved block, or every non-air block within 5 blocks if you're sneaking. Paste respects build permissions and claims. Your saved block stays through death and relogging and shows as the Paste icon.

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:dummy

Size Changing#

Unlocks Size Control. Turn it on and scroll to grow up to 5× or shrink to 0.1×.

  • Scroll
  • 8 Aspects of Existence

Turn on Size Control in the ability bar and scroll. Down shrinks you (0.9×, 0.8× and so on down to 0.1×) and up grows you (2×, 3×, 4×, 5×, and you move faster when you're bigger). Turning Size Control off puts you back to normal size. Uses Pehkui.

infinity:reality_stone · palladium:dummy + palladium:command

Soul Stone · See the Soul Stone page for its skill tree.

Everything you kill gives you a soul, up to 50 (the orange bar).

  • Passive
  • 4 Aspects of Existence

Soul Heal#

Spend 1 soul to heal 2 hearts.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.5 s
  • 6 Aspects of Existence

Requires Soul Storage

Most useful early on, before you can afford Health and Regeneration.

infinity:soul_stone · infinity:soul_heal

Soul Drain#

Pull the soul out of the mob you look at (8 blocks). It stands still and turns grey.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 0.5 s
  • 12 Aspects of Existence

Requires Soul Heal

Gives you 1 soul. Use it while sneaking to give a drained mob its soul back for 1 soul. Players, bosses (#forge:bosses), clones and mobs that already have no AI can't be drained. The mob stays drained after a reload.

infinity:soul_stone · infinity:soul_drain

Glow#

Hold to see every mob and player within 100 blocks outlined in orange.

  • Hold
  • 3 Aspects of Existence

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:entity_glow

Locate Entity#

Lets you use /infinity soul locateentity to find a player or named mob, and makes Mind Reading show where its target is.

  • Passive
  • 4 Aspects of Existence

Requires Glow

The command finds the online player with that name, otherwise the closest living thing with that name (a mob named with a name tag), in your dimension first and then the others. It tells you the coordinates and the dimension. With it unlocked, the Mind Stone's Mind Reading also shows where its target is and can read a mind by name from anywhere. Anything with soul immunity can't be found.

infinity:soul_stone · infinity:locate_player

Summon Zombie#

Summons up to six zombies in Thanos armor on your team, one stored soul each.

  • Key press
  • 3 Aspects of Existence

Three named ones (Transfer Zombie, Pugmeowla Zombie and Dark Zombie) and three normal ones, never babies. Each costs one soul from Soul Storage, so with fewer souls you get fewer zombies (named ones first) and with none you get nothing. You and the zombies join the peaceful team so they don't go for you.

infinity:soul_stone · palladium:command

Enter / Exit Soulworld#

Takes you into the Soulworld, along with every mob, animal and player within 5 blocks of you. Use it again to come back.

  • Key press
  • 8 Aspects of Existence

Everyone arrives at Y 72 above the same X and Z. Each mob remembers where it came from, so Exit Soulworld (which replaces the ability while you're inside) sends them all back to their own spot.

infinity:soul_stone · infinity:pocket_dimension_enter / exit

Time Stone · See the Time Stone page for its skill tree.

Hold to speed through the day (100 ticks of daytime every tick).

  • Hold
  • 2 Aspects of Existence

infinity:time_stone · palladium:command

Stop Projectile#

Projectiles that come within 6 blocks of you freeze in the air.

  • Toggle
  • 10 Aspects of Existence

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

Works on every projectile except fishing bobbers: arrows, tridents, ender pearls, snowballs, fireballs and other mods' projectiles too. Your own aren't stopped. A frozen projectile can't move, fall or hit anything for as long as it stays in range. Once you move away or turn the ability off, it hangs for 2 more seconds and then carries on the way it was going. Frozen projectiles stay frozen through a world reload.

infinity:time_stone · infinity:projectile_stop

infinity:time_stone · infinity:toggle

Slow Time#

Unlocks Change Flow of Time. Turn it on and scroll to change the server tick rate.

  • Scroll
  • 12 Aspects of Existence

Requires Fast Forward Daylight Cycle

While Change Flow of Time is on, scroll down to slow the world (4 fewer ticks per second per step, down to 2 TPS) or up to speed it up (4 more per step, up to 40 TPS). The green bar shows the step and the middle is normal speed. Your step is remembered for next time. Turning it off puts the server back to 20 TPS. You keep moving at normal speed. It affects the whole server. Needs Chrono API.

infinity:time_stone · infinity:time_flow

Freeze Time#

Press once to freeze and again to unfreeze. You can walk on water while time is frozen. It affects the whole server. Needs Chrono API.

Mind Stone#

Mind Stone · See the Mind Stone page for its skill tree.

Pacify#

Mobs within 5 blocks stop attacking you. Sneak and use it to undo.

  • Key press
  • 10 Aspects of Existence

You and everything within 5 blocks join the peaceful scoreboard team, so those mobs leave you alone. Use it while sneaking (Un-Pacify) to take nearby mobs off the team again. Works well with the Soul Stone's Summon Zombie. Anything with mental immunity, like another Mind Stone user, isn't pacified.

infinity:mind_stone · palladium:command

Mental Immunity#

Always on with the Mind Stone. Other players can't read your mind, pacify you or fool you with a disguise.

  • Passive
  • No unlock needed

Hidden from the skill tree and ability bar. Someone else's Mind Reading shows "That mind is shielded", their Pacify skips you, and you see a disguised player's real skin and name. Your own abilities still work on you.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:mental_immunity

Intangibility#

Walk through blocks and mobs. Nothing can hurt you while it is on.

Mind Illusions#

Three copies of you for 60 seconds.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 20 s
  • 8 Aspects of Existence

The clones look exactly like you, with your skin, armor, held items, render layers and name tag. Each has 20 health. They follow you like a tamed wolf, fight back, and attack whatever you hit. Mobs after you pick randomly between you and the clones near you. They vanish after 60 seconds or when you die and never drop anything. 20 second cooldown.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:clones

Mind Reading#

Look at a mob or player (up to 32 blocks) to see its health, effects, gear, inventory and target. With Locate Entity, read any mind by name from anywhere.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 1 s
  • 4 Aspects of Existence

Opens a view you can't take anything out of. The top row has a name tag whose tooltip shows the type, health, absorption, armor, owner and current target (plus food and level for players), followed by up to 8 active effects. Below that are the six equipment slots, then the inventory of a player, a mob that carries items (villagers, allays, piglins) or a horse with a chest.

With the Soul Stone's Locate Entity unlocked, the name tag also shows where the target is and in which dimension, and using Mind Reading while looking at nothing opens a menu: type a player's or named mob's name to read their mind from anywhere. Someone with the Mind Stone (mental immunity) can't be read ("That mind is shielded"), and soul immunity hides their location.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:mind_reading

Disguise#

Type a username and wear that player's skin and name.

  • Key press
  • Cooldown 1 s
  • 6 Aspects of Existence

The name can be 3 to 16 letters, digits or underscores. Everyone sees you with that player's skin and name tag. Reset puts your own skin back. Slim skins are drawn with slim arms. The disguise is only worn while the ability is unlocked, so losing the stone takes it off. Players with mental immunity (anyone with the Mind Stone) still see your real skin and name.

infinity:mind_stone · infinity:disguise

Empty Aether#

Insert Reality Stone#

With the Reality Stone in your off hand, turns an Empty Aether back into the Aether.

  • Key press
  • No unlock needed

infinity:empty_aether · palladium:command

Activation types#

Key press
Press its key once. With a cooldown you have to wait before using it again.
Toggle
Press once to turn it on and again to turn it off.
Hold
On while you hold the key down.
Scroll
Turn its toggle on, then use the mouse wheel.
Passive
Always on once unlocked, as long as you have the stone's power.
Upgrade
A skill tree unlock that turns on or changes other abilities. Does nothing by itself.
Right-click
Right-click with the item in your hand.
Skill tree switch
A free entry in the skill tree. Click it to turn a passive (or a setting) off or back on, and the new state shows above your hotbar. It locks itself again straight away, ready for the next click. Your choice is saved on you.

Cooldowns are in seconds (20 ticks is 1 second). Abilities marked "Press twice to confirm" ignore the first press and just show a warning. Press again within 3 seconds to go through with it.
