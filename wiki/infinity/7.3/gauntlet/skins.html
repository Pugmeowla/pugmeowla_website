Gauntlet Skins

Seventeen skins in 7.3.

On this page
  1. Putting a skin on
  2. Taking it off
  3. All skins

Putting a skin on#

Wear the gauntlet, hold the unlock item in your main hand and right-click. The item is used up. In 7.3 the skin belongs to you, not the gauntlet, so it shows on whatever gauntlet you wear. You can only have one skin at a time.

Taking it off#

Return to Original GauntletTakes your skin off and gives back its unlock item.
  • Key press
  • No unlock needed

infinity:infinity_gauntlet · palladium:command
