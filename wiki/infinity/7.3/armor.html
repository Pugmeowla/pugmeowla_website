Armor

Two full sets of Thanos' armor, Classic and MCU, made at Palladium's Tailoring Bench.

The two sets#

Both sets have a helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots, and both are netherite armor: the same protection, toughness and knockback resistance as a netherite set, fire resistant and Epic. They only differ in looks.

  • Classic is Thanos from the comics, in blue and gold. The chestplate hides your arms and chest so the armor's own model takes their place, in first person too.
  • MCU is Thanos from the films, in black and gold.

A full set gives 20 armor, 12 toughness and 0.4 knockback resistance. Each piece draws its part of the model through its own Palladium render layer.

Making it#

Both sets are made at Palladium's Tailoring Bench, with a Hammer in the tool slot. Each set's recipe lists:

Thanos' Armor (Classic)

Ingredients
12 Yellow Fabric, 12 Blue Fabric, 24 Uru Plate
Tool
Hammer

Thanos' Armor (MCU)

Ingredients
12 Yellow Fabric, 12 Black Fabric, 24 Uru Plate
Tool
Hammer

The fabrics come from Palladium. The recipes don't need unlocking first, and they show up under the Infinity Stone Core category.
