Soul Stone

A soul for a soul.

On this page
  1. Getting it
  2. Abilities
  3. Skill tree costs

Getting it#

  1. Kill one of your own tamed pets to get a Best Friends Soul.
  2. Go to Vormir with /space_tp or a rocket.
  3. Trade the soul to the Soul Guardian at the top of Vormir's Edge.
