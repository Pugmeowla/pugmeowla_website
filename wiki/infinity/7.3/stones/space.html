Space Stone

Teleporting, telekinesis and black holes.

On this page
  1. Overview
  2. Abilities
  3. Skill tree costs
  4. Notes

Overview#

The Space Stone is in the Tesseract in the Norse Village. Hold the stone or the Tesseract, put it in the gauntlet or use the Space Cosmi-Rod to get its power. Drop the Tesseract and hit it to get the stone out.

Force FieldA shield bubble that blocks attacks but stops you moving.
  • Toggle
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Fall Damage Immunity

Gives a huge armor and toughness boost and draws a shield around you while you're rooted in place. A KubeJS script cancels hits under 23 damage from anything with an attacker, and every hit from Iron Golems, Wardens, Vindicators, Piglin Brutes and Zombified Piglins.

Water BreathingBreathe underwater.
  • Passive
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Force Field

infinity:space_stone · palladium:command

TelekinesisHold to grab a mob or player (up to 20 blocks) and move it around.
  • Hold
  • 1 Aspect of Existence

Requires Force Field

Needs CoreWithStuff. Scroll to move what you're holding between 2 and 30 blocks away and left-click to throw it. Bedrock, barriers and obsidian can't be grabbed.

TeleportTeleports you to where you are looking, through a short portal.
  • Key press

Teleport CommandLets you use /space_tp .

Takes you and everything within 3 blocks to those coordinates in any dimension. See /space_tp.

Notes#

  • Telekinesis and the Teleport GUI come from CoreWithStuff. Telekinesis only picks up mobs and players in 7.3, not blocks.
  • The Teleport Command is the main way to reach the planets without a rocket. See /space_tp.
