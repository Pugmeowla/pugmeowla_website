Finding the Stones

One stone per container, one container per structure. Here's where they are and how to crack them open.

You don't have to take a stone out to use it: every container gives its stone's power while you hold it (the Eye while worn or held). You do need the bare stone to put it in the Infinity Gauntlet.

Locating the structures#

The five stone structures are each placed once per world, in a ring around the world origin (0, 0), like strongholds. Approximate distances from the origin, worked out from their structure sets:

StructureWhere it can generateDistance from 0, 0
Sanctum SanctorumOverworldabout 7,300–14,000 blocks
Norse VillageOverworldabout 18,500–35,300 blocks
SanctuaryThe Endabout 7,300–14,000 blocks
Aether ChamberSvartalfheim (underground, around Y −60)about 4,400–8,400 blocks
Morag TempleMorag (on the ocean floor)about 18,500–35,300 blocks

Ways to find them:

  • /locate: with cheats, /locate structure infinity:sanctum (or norse_village, sanctuary, aether, morag_temple) while in the right dimension.
  • Explorer maps from the Soul Guardian on Vormir (see below).
  • JEI info pages on each stone and container describe where they are.
Decoys

Each of these structures also has a rare "fake" copy (1% chance per region) that looks the same but has nothing in the frame or on the stand. If the container is missing, you may be in a decoy.

Reaching the other worlds#

Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim aren't connected by portals. Once you have the Space Stone, unlock Teleport GUI and type the dimension ID (infinity:vormir, infinity:morag, infinity:svartelheim) and coordinates. The dimension field suggests every dimension the server knows. See Dimensions &amp; Travel for coordinates and what to expect on each world.

A route that works#

  1. Space Stone: travel to the Norse Village and take the Tesseract from the item frame. Unlock Force Field, then the Teleport GUI.
  2. Time Stone: the Eye of Agamotto stands on an armor stand inside the Sanctum. Wear it on your chest or in the Curios necklace slot, use Open Eye, then Remove Time Stone.
  3. Mind Stone: in the End, the Scepter hangs in a glow item frame in the Sanctuary.
  4. Reality Stone: portal to Svartalfheim and dig down to the Aether Chamber. Malekith guards the Aether.
  5. Power Stone: portal to Morag. The temple is underwater and trapped with dispensers of Arrows of Harming VII. Take the Orb from its stand and crack it (see the Orb).
  6. Soul Stone: get a Best Friend's Soul, then portal to Vormir (around X 212, Z 99) and trade with the Soul Guardian.

The Soul Stone and the Soul Guardian#

A soul for a soul. The first time you kill one of your own tamed pets (a tamed wolf, cat, parrot, or a tamed horse-type animal), you receive a Best Friend's Soul with the lore "How Could You". This happens once per player.

On Vormir, the Soul Guardian villager waits at about 212 121 99. It can't be hurt by anything except /kill or the void, and isn't turned into a witch by lightning. Its trades, each usable once:

You giveYou get
Best Friend's SoulSoul Stone

Nether Star + 16 Aspects of ExistenceMorag Temple Explorer Map
Red Dye + 16 Aspects of ExistenceAether Chamber Explorer Map

Known limitation: explorer maps

A bought map is filled in a few seconds after it lands in your inventory, searching from the Overworld spawn. The Sanctum and Norse Village maps can find their Overworld structures; the Morag Temple, Aether Chamber and Sanctuary are in other dimensions, so those three maps may stay blank.

If the Soul Guardian is lost, an operator can bring it back with /infinity soulguardian respawn. The Soul Guardian Telephone can also summon it to you.
