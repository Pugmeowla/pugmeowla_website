Every stone ability is paid for with Aspects of Existence. Every Infinity Temple chest has 1 to 4 of them, sometimes with some Shards of Existence too. You can also make them from Shards (see JEI). The Soul Guardian takes them for its explorer maps as well.

Unlocking abilities#

  1. Get the stone's power (hold the stone or its container, put it in the gauntlet, and so on).
  2. Open Palladium's powers screen and pick the stone's tree.
  3. Click an ability whose requirements you meet. The Aspects are taken from your inventory and it stays unlocked.

A few entries are free and work as switches. Flight Toggle, Screen Toggle, Daylight Cycle Toggle and Radiation Texture Toggle flip a setting every time you click them. Some abilities also need other stones' powers at the moment you buy and use them, like Black Hole, Meteor Storm and Immortality. Operators can unlock or lock everything with /infinity unlock_stones.

Power Stone levels#

The Power Stone has a level from 0 to 11. It's kept in the DamageCycle scoreboard objective and shown on the purple bar while you have the stone. Buy the levels in the tree one after another, then pick the one you want:

  1. Turn on Damage Control in the ability bar.
  2. Scroll up to go up a level and down to go down. You can't go past the highest level you've bought.

  • Power Blast does 2level per hit. At full power it turns rainbow and explodes where it hits blocks.
  • Rocket Burst damage is multiplied by level ÷ 11, so it does nothing at level 0. At level 11 it turns into Power Cyclone.
  • Meteor Storm uses a moon 1 + level blocks across.
  • Your attack damage goes back to normal when you lose the Power Stone.

If an operator locks your level unlocks again (with /infinity lock_stones for example), you drop to the highest level you still have.

Soul Storage#

Once you have Soul Storage, everything you kill gives you a soul, up to 50. They're kept in the SoulStorage scoreboard objective and shown on the orange bar. Clones and mobs you've already drained don't give one.

AbilitySoulsWhat it does
Soul Heal−1Heals 2 hearts. Does nothing at full health or with no souls.
Soul Drain+1Drains the mob you're looking at, which then stands still and turns grey.
Soul Drain while sneaking−1Gives a drained mob its soul back.

Advancements#

The mod's advancement tab starts with Pugmeowla's Infinity Stone Core and follows the hunt: the gauntlet cast (Infinity Is Within Reach), making the gauntlet ("Fine, I'll do it myself"), each container and its stone (The Jewel of Odin's Treasure Room, The Scepter of Loki, The Converter of Matter into Dark Matter, The Power of a Celestial, The Sorcerers Quest, plus one for each stone), the Cosmi-Rod (It's called the Universal Weapon. Oh, really? and Dance Battle), the hammer (It's Clobberin' Time!), all six stones (Six Stones, One Gauntlet), the Snap (I Am Inevitable) and destroying the stones (Peace in our Time).
