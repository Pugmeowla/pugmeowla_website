Every stone ability is unlocked with Aspects of Existence. Every Infinity Temple chest has 1 to 4 of them, sometimes with some Shards of Existence too. You can also make them from Shards (see recipe). The Soul Guardian takes them for its explorer maps as well.

Unlocking abilities#

  1. Get the stone's power (hold the stone or its container, put it in the gauntlet, and so on).
  2. Open Palladium's powers screen and pick the stone's tree.
  3. Click an ability whose requirements you meet. The Aspects are taken from your inventory and it stays unlocked.

A few entries are free and work as switches, marked with a toggle icon instead of a padlock. Click one and it flips its setting, shows On or Off above your hotbar, and is ready for the next click. Most passives have one. A passive's toggle shows up once you own it, and your choices are saved on you.

Some abilities also need other stones' powers at the moment you buy and use them, like Black Hole, Meteor Storm and Immortality. Operators can unlock or lock everything with /infinity unlock_stones.
