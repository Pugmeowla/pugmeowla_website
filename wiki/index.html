Version 8.0 · Minecraft 1.20.1 · Forge · Palladium

Pugmeowla'sInfinity Stone Core

The 1.20.1 port of Infinity Craft, built on Palladium. It brings back the Infinity Gauntlet and all six stones from Infinity Craft and Lucraft Core, with new abilities and structures. Find the stones across the Overworld, the End and four other planets, work through each stone's skill tree, and then decide if you want to snap.

The six stones

Space StonePortals, telekinesis and black holes. Mind StoneFlight, illusions, mind reading and disguises. Reality StoneRewrite blocks, change size, weather and visibility. Power StoneScaling damage, beams, rockets and meteor storms. Time StoneSpeed up, slow down or freeze the whole world. Soul StoneSouls, the Soulworld and near-immortality.

What's in it

Every stone is a Palladium power with its own skill tree. A stone works on its own when you hold it, inside its container (Tesseract, Scepter, Aether or Eye of Agamotto), in the Curios head slot, in a Cosmi-Rod, or together with the others in the Infinity Gauntlet. You buy abilities with Aspects of Existence, which you find in Infinity Temples. Once all six stones are in one gauntlet you can snap, and the Snap burns your arm for a whole day.

Worth a look

The SnapWipe out half of all life, send every hostile mob away, or destroy the stones. And what it does to you afterwards. Space Stone portalsOpen a portal to any dimension and coordinates, or jump to the block you're looking at. Power levelsEleven levels, each one doubling your damage. With all six stones it goes infinite. The OrbEvery world gets its own left and right combination guarding the Power Stone. Soul StorageKeep up to 50 souls and spend them on healing, or rip one out of a mob. Custom gauntlets and skinsMake any item or power act as an Infinity Gauntlet, or add a skin, with one JSON file.
