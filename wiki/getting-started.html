Getting Started

What you need installed, how the controls work, and roughly how a playthrough goes.

What the mod is#

Pugmeowla's Infinity Stone Core is the 1.20.1 port of Infinity Craft, made as an addon for Palladium. It takes the Infinity content from Infinity Craft, Lucraft Core and Heroes Expansion and rebuilds it with Palladium's newer systems. It adds:

  • The six Infinity Stones. Each one is a Palladium power with its own skill tree.
  • The Infinity Gauntlet, the Snap and 19 gauntlet skins.
  • The containers the stones come in: Tesseract, Scepter, Aether, Orb and Eye of Agamotto, plus the Cosmi-Rod.
  • A structure for each stone, four planets to visit (Vormir, Morag, Svartalfheim and the Garden) and the Soulworld. See Dimensions.
  • JSON systems for addon makers: custom stones, skins, gauntlets and ground items.

Put the mod and the required mods in your mods folder. On a server, the server and every player need the same mods. The full list of integrations is on Compatibility.

Controls#

Most controls come from Palladium.

  • Ability keys: abilities that need a key show up in Palladium's ability bar and use Palladium's ability keys. Scroll abilities (power levels, Slow Time, Size Changing) use the mouse wheel while their toggle is on.
  • Skill trees are in Palladium's powers screen.
  • G Open Gauntlet (under Infinity Stones in the controls menu) opens the stone menu of the gauntlet in your main hand, or the one you're wearing. Right-clicking with an Infinity Gauntlet in your main hand does the same.
  • Telekinesis and Black Hole take over the mouse while they're on. Scroll moves things, left-click throws, right-click locks in place once you have Spatial Lock.
Wear the gauntlet in your off hand or the Curios hands slot

That's where it gives you every stone inside and shows on your arm. It still works in the main hand, but a few abilities are made for the off hand.

Buying abilities#

Holding a stone, or having it in the gauntlet, gives you that stone's power. Nothing is unlocked at the start. Open the stone's skill tree and click an ability to buy it with Aspects of Existence from your inventory. Lots of abilities need another one first. What you unlock is saved on you, so you keep it when you take the stone out and put it back. Costs are on Skill Trees and Progression.

How a playthrough usually goes#

  1. Get set up. Loot Infinity Temples for Aspects and Shards of Existence, and village weaponsmith chests for the Infinity Gauntlet Cast.
  2. Make the Infinity Gauntlet. The recipe is in JEI. See The Infinity Gauntlet.
  3. Get the Space Stone first. The Tesseract is in the Norse Village. Its Teleport GUI gets you to the other planets, unless you'd rather build a rocket.
  4. Get the rest: Time from the Sanctum Sanctorum, Mind from the Sanctuary in the End, Reality from the Aether Chamber on Svartalfheim, Power from the Orb on Morag and Soul on Vormir. The full route is on Finding the Stones.
  5. Fill out the trees, unlock the six stone abilities like Immortality and full power, and think about whether the Snap is worth it.

A few tips#

  • Stones, containers and the gauntlet don't despawn, burn or blow up when dropped. They lie on the ground until you right-click them. More on ground items.
  • Don't hold a bare Power Stone in your hand. It hits you with cosmic radiation. Put it in the gauntlet, the head slot or a Cosmi-Rod. See Power Stone.
  • The Soul Guardian on Vormir sells explorer maps to the stone structures.
