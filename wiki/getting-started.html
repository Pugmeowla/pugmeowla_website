Getting Started

Everything you need before your first stone: requirements, controls, and the shape of a playthrough.

What the mod is#

Pugmeowla's Infinity Stone Core is an addon for Palladium that reimagines Infinity Craft and the Lucraft Core / Heroes Expansion Infinity content for Minecraft 1.20.1. It adds:

  • The six Infinity Stones, each a Palladium power with its own skill tree of abilities.
  • The Infinity Gauntlet with six stone slots, the Snap, and 19 gauntlet skins.
  • The stones' containers: Tesseract, Scepter, Aether, Orb and Eye of Agamotto, plus the Cosmi-Rod.
  • Unique structures that hide each stone, and four alien dimensions (Vormir, Morag, Svartalfheim and the Garden) plus the Soulworld.
  • Data-driven systems for addon makers: custom stones, skins, gauntlets and ground items without Java.

Controls#

The mod uses Palladium's ability system, so most controls are Palladium's:

  • Ability keys: abilities that need a key appear in Palladium's ability bar. Use Palladium's ability keys to activate them; scroll-based abilities (Power levels, Slow Time, Size Changing) use the mouse wheel while their toggle is on.
  • Skill trees: open Palladium's powers screen to see each stone's tree and buy abilities.
  • G Open Gauntlet (category Infinity Stones in the controls menu): opens the stone menu of the gauntlet in your main hand, or the one you're wearing. Right-clicking an Infinity Gauntlet in your main hand does the same.
  • Telekinesis and Black Hole take over the mouse while active: scroll to move, left-click to throw, right-click to spatially lock (once unlocked).
Wear the gauntlet in the off hand or the Curios hands slot

That's where it gives you all its stones' powers and is drawn on your arm. In the main hand it still works for some abilities, but the gauntlet's own tree recommends the off hand or Curios.

How abilities and unlocking work#

Holding a stone (or having it in the gauntlet) gives you that stone's power. Nothing is unlocked at first: open the stone's skill tree and click an ability to buy it with Aspects of Existence from your inventory. Many abilities need an earlier one first. Unlocks are saved on your player, so taking the stone out and putting it back keeps your progress. Full costs are on Skill Trees &amp; Progression.

A typical playthrough#

  1. Gear up. Loot Infinity Temples for Aspects of Existence and Shards of Existence, and village weaponsmith chests for the Infinity Gauntlet Cast.
  2. Make the Infinity Gauntlet. Its recipe is in JEI. See The Infinity Gauntlet.
  3. Get the Space Stone first. The Tesseract sits in the Norse Village in the Overworld. The Space Stone's Teleport GUI is the way to reach the other worlds.
  4. Collect the rest: the Time Stone from the Sanctum Sanctorum, the Mind Stone from the Sanctuary in the End, the Reality Stone from the Aether Chamber on Svartalfheim, the Power Stone from the Orb on Morag, and the Soul Stone on Vormir. The step-by-step route is on Finding the Stones.
  5. Grow your trees, unlock the six-stone abilities (Immortality, full power), and decide whether the Snap is worth it.

First tips#

  • Stones, containers and the gauntlet never despawn, burn or blow up when dropped; they lie on the ground and are picked up with right-click. More on ground items.
  • Don't carry a bare Power Stone in your hand without protection: it bombards you with cosmic radiation. Put it in the gauntlet, the head slot or a Cosmi-Rod. See Power Stone.
  • The Soul Guardian on Vormir sells explorer maps that lead to the stone structures.
