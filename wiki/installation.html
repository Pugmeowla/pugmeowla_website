Installation and Compatibility

Everything you need to install, and every mod Infinity Stone Core works with.

Versions#

Mod version8.0 (these pages)
Minecraft1.20.1
LoaderForge 47.x (built on 47.4.20). There's no Fabric build.
Mod IDinfinity

Required mods#

ModVersionUsed for
Palladium1.20.1-4.2.0 or newer (built on 4.5.6)The whole power system: stones, abilities, skill trees, render layers and addon packs. Its own items (the vibranium ingot, vibranium circuit and flux capacitors) also unlock some gauntlet skins.
GeckoLib4.x for 1.20.1 (built on 4.8.4)Animated and custom models: the Orb, the Eye of Agamotto, gauntlet skins, stones in their sockets and Malekith.
Chrono API1.0.0 or newerChanging the tick rate and pausing time: Slow Time, Freeze Time and the Time Stone's immunity to them.
Pehkui3.7.12Reality Stone Size Changing and Invisibility scaling, and the Mind Stone's Intangibility hitbox and reach.

Installing#

  1. Install Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.x.
  2. Put Infinity Stone Core, Palladium, GeckoLib, Chrono API and Pehkui in your mods folder.
  3. Add any of the optional mods below that you want.

On a server, the server and every player need the same mods.

Optional mods and integrations#

None of these are needed. Each one adds something when it's installed.

ModWhat you get
Immersive Portals (with Cloth Config)Space Stone portals opened from the Teleport GUI menu become real portals: you see the other side through them and walk straight through, both ways, even between dimensions. Without it they work as before. See Space Stone.
Ad AstraThe Garden, Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim as planets. You need a Tier 4 rocket to get to them.
Cosmic HorizonsA Marvel solar system (dimension infinity:marvel) with the same four planets to fly to.
Curios API RecommendedHands slot for the Infinity Gauntlet (its visibility button works too), head slot for the six stones, necklace slot for the Eye of Agamotto.
JEIRecipes, plus info pages on the stones, containers, cast, shards, uru and dwarf star meteor saying where to find them.
Tinkers' ConstructTitanium, gold titanium alloy and ferro titanium gold alloy as materials, four molten fluids (uru, titanium, gold titanium alloy, ferro titanium gold alloy) and casting for the gauntlet and the ferro titanium gold ingot.
Fsang's HeropackSteel and gold titanium share item tags with Fsang's, and Fsang's uru ingots can be used for uru plates.
Pugmeowla's Infintrix (with Alien Evolution, Into the Omniverse or Omnitrix Evolution)Omnitrixes work as custom gauntlets.
Infinity ProtocolThe Iron Man MK85 suit works as a gauntlet, with the stones only showing during its nano gauntlet animation.
Pugmeowla's Herbs of WakandaThe Black Panther necklace gives the Infinity Gauntlet power (chest or Curios necklace).
Pugmeowla's Infinity GeneratorIts generator shows up in the Infinity Stone Core creative tab.
Ultor Infinitus (Infinity Ultron)A player with the InfinityUltron tag can fire the Mind Stone beam from their head.
PowerborneA thrown Stormbreaker near someone wearing the gauntlet switches off the Soul Stone's defenses and hurts them.
TrinketsGlove slot data for a Fabric build. Not used on Forge
