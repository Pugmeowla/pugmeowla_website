Dimensions & Travel

Four alien worlds, a pocket dimension for souls, and a slice of space. How to get there and what you'll find.

Getting there#

There are no portal blocks to these worlds. The intended way in is the Space Stone's Teleport GUI: type the dimension ID and coordinates and step through. The dimension field suggests every dimension the server has, so you don't need to remember the IDs.

Teleport GUI examples
Dimension: infinity:vormir       X: 212   Y: 130   Z: 99      (the Soul Guardian)
Dimension: infinity:morag        X: 0     Y: 120   Z: 0
Dimension: infinity:svartelheim  X: 0     Y: 120   Z: 0
Dimension: infinity:garden       X: 0     Y: 100   Z: 0

Portals always put you on safe ground near the coordinates you typed, so a rough Y is fine. Operators can of course also use /execute in infinity:vormir run tp @s 212 130 99.

Space mods

The mod ships planet data for the Cosmos mod (a "Marvel" solar system with the Garden, Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim as planets) and Ad Astra-style planet files. Those only do anything when that mod is installed.

Vormir#

infinity:vormir · terrain of Vormir Sand and Vormir Stone, no sky light, sea level 63.

The planet of the Soul Stone. Its great cliff and the Soul Guardian are always in the same place, roughly X 0–271, Z 0–191, with the Guardian at 212 121 99. See Structures and Soul Stone.

Morag#

infinity:morag · terrain of Morag Stone, no sky light, sea level 100.

A flooded planet. The Morag Temple with the Orb sits on the ocean floor, far from the origin, so bring water breathing (the Space Stone has it).

Svartalfheim#

infinity:svartelheim · terrain of Svartelheim Stone and Svartelheim Sand, no sky light, sea level 100.

Home of the dark elves. Uru meteor craters are everywhere on the surface, and the Aether Chamber is buried near the bottom of the world.

The Garden#

infinity:garden · normal day and night, with garden, plains and river biomes.

The peaceful farm planet where Thanos retires. Beds and respawn anchors work and raids can happen. There are no stones or structures here; it's a place to go after it's all over.

The Soulworld#

infinity:soulworld · a flat world of smooth red sandstone under a thin layer of water, always noon.

  • Getting in: the Soul Stone's Enter / Exit Soulworld (you and everything within 5 blocks, at Y 72), the Snap (hostile mobs and other players), or Destroy Stones.
  • Getting out: Exit Soulworld sends everyone back to where they came from. Anyone without a way back can walk to X 0, Z 0: a 5 × 5 shaft drops all the way down to an End portal at the bottom of the world.
  • The Snap cell at 1000 78 1000 is a lit bedrock room with an oak door on its south side. Snapped mobs and players arrive inside it.
  • Entities sent back by Exit Soulworld that didn't arrive through Enter Soulworld land in the Overworld above the same X and Z with slow falling.

Marvel (space)#

infinity:marvel · an empty space dimension used as the Marvel solar system for the Cosmos mod. Without Cosmos it isn't meant to be visited.
