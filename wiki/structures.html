You can find any of them with /locate structure <id> while you're in the right dimension. There aren't screenshots of the structures here yet, the descriptions are from the structure files.

Stone structures#

The five stone structures are placed like strongholds: exactly one per world, at a random angle on a ring around 0, 0. The distances are rough and measured from the world origin.

Norse Village#

A small Norse church (15 × 24 × 23) in the Overworld, about 18,500 to 35,300 blocks out. Dark oak signs and a lectern tell its story, and the Tesseract hangs in an invisible item frame behind them. After the Nine Realms were at peace, Odin left the Tesseract here in the care of Asgardian worshippers. It's a slightly redone version of the Norse Village from Lucraft's Heroes Expansion.

Sanctum Sanctorum#

Doctor Strange's sanctum (36 × 36 × 25), home of the Masters of the Mystic Arts, in the Overworld about 7,300 to 14,000 blocks out. The Eye of Agamotto stands on an armor stand inside.

Sanctuary#

Thanos's old domain (38 × 31 × 41), also called Chitauri space, out on the End islands about 7,300 to 14,000 blocks from the origin. The Scepter is on display in a glow item frame.

Aether Chamber#

A big buried chamber (56 × 118 × 57) that starts down around Y −60 on Svartalfheim, about 4,400 to 8,400 blocks from the origin. The Dark Elves sealed the Aether in here after Bor decided it was too dangerous to destroy. It sits in a glow item frame, guarded by Malekith: a husk with his own model, 100 health, 20 armor and 10 toughness, permanent Speed VI, Strength VI and Resistance III, and Malekith's Sword. He's a proper fight, so bring armor.

Morag Temple#

The flooded temple from Guardians of the Galaxy (71 × 28 × 34), on the ocean floor of Morag, about 18,500 to 35,300 blocks out. Two rows of dispensers full of Arrows of Harming VII cover the hall. The Orb rests on an armor stand.

Vormir's Edge#

Vormir's cliff isn't a normal structure. It's built one chunk at a time, always in the same place on Vormir, covering X 0 to 271 and Z 0 to 191 from Y 63 up. The Soul Guardian stands at the top at about 212 121 99 and only spawns once per world. Vormir's Edge was built by DogBoi64. The Soul Guardian's trades are on Finding the Stones.

Older worlds

If your world already had Vormir from an older version of the mod, it keeps it, and the Soul Guardian isn't spawned a second time.

Decoys#

The Sanctum, Norse Village, Sanctuary, Aether Chamber and Morag Temple each have a fake twin (*_fake) with the same building and nothing in the frame or on the stand. Fakes are rare (1% per 100 chunk region) and can show up anywhere their biome is allowed. If you find one close to spawn, check how far from the origin you are.

How they used to be#

  • Before v3.3 the Morag Temple generated in the End.
  • In v3.3 and earlier the Aether Chamber was much smaller and generated in the Nether.

Other structures#

Infinity Temple#

A small temple (11 × 7 × 11) all over the Overworld (one every 42 to 67 chunks, 69% chance). In the middle is a Temple Beacon that shines a beam into the sky with no pyramid, so you can spot temples from far away. The chest always has 1 to 4 Aspects of Existence, plus 4 to 8 rolls of ores, ingots, diamonds, emeralds, golden apples, Palladium metals, the mod's own metals and sometimes Shards of Existence.

Crashed Kree Ship#

A small crashed ship (24 × 8 × 7) in plains (one every 50 to 100 chunks, 20% chance). The chests are a joke ("Haha empty chests ;)"). The real prize is the Cosmi-Rod in an invisible item frame. Like the Norse Village, it's a redone version of the one from Heroes Expansion.

Dwarf Star Meteor#

A small crater (14 × 7 × 13) with Dwarf Star Meteor blocks in it. Common all over the Overworld (every 40 to 80 chunks, 95% chance).

Uru Meteor#

The same crater shape on Svartalfheim, made of Svartelheim Stone with Uru Ore in it.
