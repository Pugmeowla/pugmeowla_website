Activation types#

ActivationHow you use it
Key pressPress its key once. With a cooldown you have to wait before using it again.
TogglePress once to turn it on and again to turn it off.
HoldOn while you hold the key down.
ScrollTurn its toggle on, then use the mouse wheel.
PassiveAlways on once unlocked, as long as you have the stone's power.
UpgradeA skill tree unlock that turns on or changes other abilities. Does nothing by itself.
Right-clickRight-click with the item in your hand.

Cooldowns are in seconds (20 ticks is 1 second). Abilities marked "Press twice to confirm" ignore the first press and just show a warning. Press again within 3 seconds to go through with it.
