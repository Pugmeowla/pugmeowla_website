What it does

The Infinity Generator is an addon by currentbrick that needs Infinity Stone Core. Put an Infinity Stone in it and it makes Forge Energy (FE) for any machine that takes it. The stone isn't used up, so the generator runs for as long as the stone stays inside.

Making it

The recipe comes from Infinity Stone Core, and the generator shows up in its creative tab.

RowIngredients
Top{{item dwarf_star_alloy_plate}}, Vibranium Circuit, {{item dwarf_star_alloy_plate}}
MiddleSteel block, Nether Star, steel block
Bottom{{item dwarf_star_alloy_plate}}, Vibranium Flux Capacitor, {{item dwarf_star_alloy_plate}}

The Vibranium Circuit and Vibranium Flux Capacitor are Palladium items. Any steel block works (#forge:blocks/steel), like {{item steel_block}}. Mine the generator with an iron pickaxe or better: it drops itself, keeping its name.

Using it

{{figure src="models/infinity_generator.png" caption="Empty"}} {{figure src="models/infinity_generator_power.png" caption="Power Stone"}} {{figure src="models/infinity_generator_space.png" caption="Space Stone"}} {{figure src="models/infinity_generator_reality.png" caption="Reality Stone"}} {{figure src="models/infinity_generator_soul.png" caption="Soul Stone"}} {{figure src="models/infinity_generator_time.png" caption="Time Stone"}} {{figure src="models/infinity_generator_mind.png" caption="Mind Stone"}}
  • Right-click it to open its menu, which has one slot. Only the six Infinity Stones and the {{item tesseract}} fit.
  • The block changes to show which stone is inside, and the Tesseract counts as the Space Stone.
  • While a stone is in, it makes energy every tick until it's full, and sends it out to every block touching it that takes FE, on all six sides.
  • Take the stone out and it stops making energy, but keeps sending out what it has stored.
Stones in a generator

A stone sitting in a generator isn't in anyone's gauntlet, so nobody gets its power, and there's still only one of each stone per world.

Config

The generator's numbers are in its own common config file. The defaults are huge on purpose: it's powered by an Infinity Stone.

SettingDefaultWhat it does
energyGenerated1,000,000FE made every tick while a stone is inside
maxCapacity1,000,000FE it can store
maxTransfer1,000,000FE it can send to each neighbouring block per tick

Each setting can go up to 2,147,483,647.
