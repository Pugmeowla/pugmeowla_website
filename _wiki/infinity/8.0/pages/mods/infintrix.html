What it does

Pugmeowla's Infintrix is an addon pack that needs Infinity Stone Core and an Omnitrix mod: Alien Evolution, Into the Omniverse or Omnitrix Evolution. While an Omnitrix is on your wrist it works as an Infinity Gauntlet. You don't need the real gauntlet at all.

The Prototype and Ultimatrix gloves only appear once at least one stone is in. Slim-armed players get a slim version of each glove, and the stones move to fit slim arms on every Omnitrix.

Using it

  • Put the Omnitrix on, then press G (Open Gauntlet) to open the stone menu. It's the same menu as the {{page gauntlet/index.html Infinity Gauntlet}}'s.
  • With a stone in, you get the {{page stones/index.html stone's powers}} and the gauntlet's, including {{page gauntlet/snap.html the Snap}}.
  • Each Omnitrix is a power gauntlet, so its stones are kept on you, one set per Omnitrix. Take the Omnitrix off and they come back the next time you put it on.
  • An Infinity Gauntlet in your off hand or Curios hands slot takes priority over the Omnitrix.

What the stones do to the Omnitrix

{{stone power}} in the Omnitrix
The Omnitrix recharges straight away when it times out, so you can transform again at once.
All six stones
Unlocks every ability of the Omnitrix you're wearing, and gives you Master Control.
The Snap's burn
Master Control is taken away and the Omnitrix times out: 5 minutes for the Omniverse Omnitrix, about 2.5 minutes for the others. The instant recharge stays off until it has nearly recharged.
