What it does

Pugmeowla's Infinity Protocol is an addon pack that needs Infinity Stone Core and Satsu's Iron Man Addon or Fsang's Heropack. It turns their Iron Man suits into Infinity Gauntlets: the stones sit on the suit's left hand, inside a nano gauntlet, the way Tony Stark built his own.

The Nano Gauntlet is the gold nano gauntlet from the Infinity Stone Core skins, and the Endosymbiotic gauntlet is the {{page gauntlet/skins.html Endosymbiotic Gauntlet skin}}'s model with its glowing layer.

Using it

  • Wear the suit, then press G (Open Gauntlet) to open the stone menu, the same one as the {{page gauntlet/index.html Infinity Gauntlet}}'s.
  • Each suit is an item gauntlet, so the stones are kept on the suit item. Hand the suit to someone else and the stones go with it.
  • On Satsu's suits, the stones and the nano gauntlet stay hidden while the suit is still assembling, and appear the moment the arm (or the whole suit) has finished forming. You have the stones' powers either way.
  • An Infinity Gauntlet in your off hand or Curios hands slot takes priority over the suit.

Infinite energy

On Satsu's suits, with all six stones in and the armor active, the suit never runs dry: its arc reactor energy, armor energy, nanites, Pym particles and mana keep refilling, and the armor keeps repairing itself. While the armor is active with the gauntlet power, both arms are shown in first person.
