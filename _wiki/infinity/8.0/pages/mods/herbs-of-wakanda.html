What it does

Pugmeowla's Herbs of Wakanda is its own Black Panther mod and works without Infinity Stone Core. With both installed, its Black Panther's Necklace becomes an Infinity Gauntlet. Everything else in the mod is on its own pages.

Modrinth CurseForge

The Black Panther's Necklace

{{figure src="models/wakanda_necklace.png" caption="The Black Panther's Necklace with all six stones."}}
  • Making it: at Palladium's Tailoring Bench, from 14 Tri-Polymer Black and 15 Vibranium Plates, with Shears as the tool.
  • Wearing it: on your chest, or in the Curios necklace slot.
  • The stones: press G (Open Gauntlet) while wearing it to open the stone menu, the same one as the {{page gauntlet/index.html Infinity Gauntlet}}'s. The stones are drawn in a row across the necklace on your chest.
  • The powers: with a stone in, you get the {{page stones/index.html stone's powers}} and the gauntlet's, including {{page gauntlet/snap.html the Snap}}.
  • The stones are kept on the necklace item, so they go wherever it goes. An Infinity Gauntlet in your off hand or Curios hands slot takes priority over it.
