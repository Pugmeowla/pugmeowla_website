Config

There's one config file, config/infinity-common.toml, with five settings:

[orb]
	#Number of left/right turns in the Orb's combination lock.
	#Each world gets its own random combination; changing this generates a new one.
	#Range: 1 ~ 64
	combinationLength = 15

[tesseract]
	#Dimensions the Tesseract never takes players to, as IDs ("modid:name") or whole mods ("modid:*").
	#Every other dimension, including other mods', is visited in turn wherever there is a safe place to land.
	excludedDimensions = ["infinity:soulworld"]

[structures]
	#Whether the real Infinity structures generate in new chunks: aether, crashed_kree_ship,
	#dwarf_star_meteor, infinity_temple, morag_temple, norse_village, sanctuary, sanctum and uru_meteor.
	realStructures = true
	#Whether the fake look-alikes of aether, morag_temple, norse_village, sanctuary and sanctum generate in new
	#chunks. They share the real structures' IDs.
	fakeStructures = true

[recipes]
	#Whether a recipe asking for one mod's titanium, steel, uru, etc. accepts the same material from any mod
	#(its Forge tag). Applies when recipes load: on world load or /reload.
	unifyMaterials = true

Switching structures off only affects chunks generated after the change. /locate and the Soul Guardian's explorer maps skip switched-off structures too, so they never point at one that isn't there. Vormir's Edge isn't affected.

unifyMaterials switches off {{page items.html#shared-materials shared materials}} in recipes. The items stay in their shared tags, so recipes that already ask for a tag still take any of them.

Server admins can also switch off any of the mod's abilities in game with /infinity ability disable. Everything else is set up through data: the {{page addons/index.html addon systems}}, Palladium power files, and tags like #infinity:snap_hostile and #infinity:projectiles.

Old worlds

Things from older versions get converted when you load the world:

  • Stones and containers placed on the ground as armor stand props turn into ground items when their chunk loads.
  • The old one rod per stone Cosmi-Rods turn into a single empty Cosmi-Rod.
  • Old Empty Eye of Agamotto items still work and turn into the normal Eye.
  • Whether the Power Stone was taken moves from an old marker block on Morag into world data, and old force loaded chunks on Morag and the Soulworld are released.
  • Worlds that already built Vormir keep it, and the Soul Guardian isn't spawned twice.
  • Projectiles stopped by the old Time Stone function get let go.

Known problems

  • Slow Time and Time Freeze change the tick rate for the whole server.
  • /infinity gauntlet skin doesn't need a permission level, and clear gives back the skin's unlock item.
  • Big Meteor Storms can lag.
  • Some in-game descriptions are older than the code. The Soul Guardian Telephone sends the Guardian back after one minute, not ten, and finds it wherever it is, and Summon Zombie summons six zombies, not seven.
