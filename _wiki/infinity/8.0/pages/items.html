Thanos armor

Two full sets of netherite armor, Classic and MCU, made at Palladium's Tailoring Bench. See {{page armor.html Armor}}.

Fancy Bottle

Hold a {{item fancy_bottle}} in your main hand and you get a small power with one entry that costs 3 experience levels. Buying it uses up the bottle and gives you a Bottle o' Enchanting, so you can store experience.

Materials

There are five metals, each with a dust, ingot, nugget and plate (gold titanium alloy also has a handle and uru has a blade), plus iron, gold and coal dusts and plates. They go into the gauntlet, the swords, the Soul Guardian Telephone and some skin unlock items. {{item titanium_ingot}} {{item steel_ingot}} {{item gold_titanium_alloy_ingot}} {{item dwarf_star_alloy_ingot}} {{item uru_ingot}}

  • Uru: mine {{item uru_ore}} in meteor craters on Svartalfheim.
  • Dwarf Star Alloy: comes from {{item dwarf_star_meteor}} blocks in Overworld meteor craters.
  • Titanium goes along with Palladium's titanium. Titanium, gold titanium alloy and ferro titanium gold alloy are also Tinkers' Construct materials.
  • The {{item hammer}} you use for plates and dusts comes back to you after crafting.

Shared materials

Many hero mods add their own titanium, steel or uru. Infinity Stone Core treats them as one material: each one is in a shared Forge item tag, and any recipe from any mod that asks for one of them takes the whole tag, so you can use whichever you have. This happens as recipes load, so it needs no other mods. Items from mods you don't have are skipped. You can switch it off in the {{page configuration.html#config config}}.

Blocks

Storage blocks for each metal, the ores ({{item uru_ore}}, {{item dwarf_star_meteor}}), the ground blocks of the other planets ({{item vormir_sand}}, {{item vormir_stone}}, {{item morag_stone}}, {{item svartelheim_stone}}, {{item svartelheim_sand}}) and the {{item temple_beacon}} from Infinity Temples. Vormir Stone is very hard (7.5) and needs the right tool to drop.

Creative tabs

Items are split over two tabs. Infinity Stone Core has the stones, containers, gauntlet, Cosmi-Rod, swords, armor and special items. Infinity Stone Core Resources has the hammer, materials and blocks. The Temple Beacon and the molten buckets aren't in either.
