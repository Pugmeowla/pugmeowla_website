Pugmeowla'sInfinity Stone Core

A full Infinity expansion for 1.20.1, made as an addon for Palladium as well as being a standard Java mod. It isn't just a port: it brings over the Infinity content from Lucraft Core, Heroes Expansion, Speedster Heroes, Star Tech, Man! The Legendary Mod? and Infinity Craft, rebuilds it with Palladium's newer systems, and goes much further with new abilities, structures, planets and addon systems. Find the stones across the Overworld, the End and four other planets, work through each stone's skill tree, and then decide if you want to snap.
