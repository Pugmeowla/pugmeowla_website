Installing

  1. Install Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.x.
  2. Put Infinity Stone Core, Palladium, GeckoLib, Chrono API and Pehkui in your mods folder.
  3. Add any of the optional mods below that you want.

On a server, the server and every player need the same mods.

Optional mods

None of these are needed. Each one adds something when it's installed.

Immersive Portals
With Cloth Config. Space Stone portals opened from the {{ab space/teleport}} menu become real portals: you see the other side through them and walk straight through, both ways, even between dimensions. Without it they work as before. See {{page stones/space.html#immersive-portals Space Stone}}.
Ad Astra
The Garden, Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim as planets. You need a Tier 4 rocket to get to them.
Cosmic Horizons
A Marvel solar system (dimension infinity:marvel) with the same four planets to fly to.
Curios API Recommended
Hands slot for the Infinity Gauntlet (its visibility button works too), head slot for the six stones, necklace slot for the Eye of Agamotto.
JEI
Recipes, plus info pages on the stones, containers, cast, shards, uru and dwarf star meteor saying where to find them.
Tinkers' Construct
Titanium, gold titanium alloy and ferro titanium gold alloy as materials, four molten fluids (uru, titanium, gold titanium alloy, ferro titanium gold alloy) and casting for the gauntlet and the ferro titanium gold ingot.
Fsang's Heropack
Fsang's steel, gold titanium, uru, adamantium, mysterium, kryptonite and ruby quartz work in any recipe that asks for the same material from another mod, see {{page items.html#shared-materials Shared materials}}.
Pugmeowla's Herbs of Wakanda
The Black Panther's Necklace works as an Infinity Gauntlet (chest or Curios necklace), with the stones drawn on it. See {{page mods/herbs-of-wakanda.html Herbs of Wakanda}}.
Ultor Infinitus (Infinity Ultron)
A player with the InfinityUltron tag can fire the Mind Stone beam from their head.
Powerborne
A thrown Stormbreaker within 10 blocks of someone wearing the gauntlet locks their stone abilities, apart from the Space Stone's portals, Power Blast and Damage Control.
Trinkets
Glove slot data for a Fabric build. Not used on Forge

Pugmeowla's addons

These need Infinity Stone Core and build on its content.

Pugmeowla's Infinity Generator
A block that turns an Infinity Stone into Forge Energy. It shows up in the Infinity Stone Core creative tab. See {{page mods/infinity-generator.html Infinity Generator}}.
Pugmeowla's Infintrix
With Alien Evolution, Into the Omniverse or Omnitrix Evolution. Omnitrixes work as custom gauntlets, and the stones change how they work. See {{page mods/infintrix.html Infintrix}}.
Pugmeowla's Infinity Protocol
Makes nanotech Iron Man suits work as gauntlets: Mark 50, 80, 81, 82, 85, 86, Model 50 and Model Prime from Satsu's Iron Man Addon, and Mark 50 and Model L from Fsang's Heropack. The stones go into the suit item, show in its tooltip, and are drawn on the left hand while it's formed. See {{page mods/infinity-protocol.html Infinity Protocol}}.
