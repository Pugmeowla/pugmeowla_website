What 7.3 is

Pugmeowla's Infinity Stone Core is a full Infinity expansion for 1.20.1, not just a port. In 7.3 it's a Palladium addon pack that loads like a mod, with the gameplay done through Palladium powers, functions and KubeJS scripts. It has the six {{page stones/index.html Infinity Stones}}, the {{page gauntlet/index.html Infinity Gauntlet}} and its skins, the containers and the {{item cosmi-rod}}, a structure for every stone and four {{page dimensions.html planets}}. What to install is on {{page installation.html Installation}}.

Using abilities

  • Abilities use Palladium's ability bar and keys, and the skill trees are in Palladium's powers screen.
  • Scroll abilities (power levels, Slow Time, Size Changing) use the mouse wheel while their toggle is on.
  • The gauntlet's stone menu opens through CoreWithStuff, see {{page gauntlet/index.html#stone-menu The Infinity Gauntlet}}.

Buying abilities

Each stone is a Palladium power with a skill tree. Click an ability to buy it with {{item aspects_of_existance}} from your inventory. Many need another ability first. Costs are on {{page progression.html Progression}}.

How a playthrough goes

  1. Loot Infinity Temples for Aspects of Existence, and village weaponsmith chests for the {{item infinity_gauntlet_cast}}.
  2. Make the Infinity Gauntlet (recipe in JEI).
  3. Get the Space Stone from the Norse Village and unlock its Teleport Command, or build a rocket.
  4. Collect the rest. The route is on {{page finding-stones.html Finding the Stones}}.
  5. Fill out the trees and decide whether to {{page gauntlet/snap.html snap}}.
The Power Stone

Holding the Power Stone in your hand without protection blows up everything around you with TNT and gives you Wither. Keep it in the gauntlet or in a Cosmi-Rod.
