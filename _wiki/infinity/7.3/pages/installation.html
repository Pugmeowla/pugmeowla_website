Versions

Mod version7.3 (these pages)
Minecraft1.20.1
LoaderForge 47.x
TypeA Palladium addon pack that loads like a mod (pack ID infinity)

Required mods

ModUsed for
PalladiumThe addon pack itself: powers, abilities, skill trees, render layers and the items and blocks.
KubeJSThe commands, Telekinesis, the Force Field and Intangibility protection, Immortality, the molten metals and the stone insert animation.
CoreWithStuffThe gauntlet's stone menu, the stones' power screens and the Space Stone's Teleport GUI. Without it the gauntlet falls back to the old insert system, which can have syncing problems, and the Teleport GUI stays locked.
GeckoLibAnimated and custom models: the Orb, gauntlet skins and Malekith.
Chrono APIThe Time Stone's Slow Time and Time Freeze.
PehkuiThe Reality Stone's Size Changing and Invisibility, and the Mind Stone's Intangibility.
Curios APIHands slot for the gauntlet, head slot for the Mind Stone and necklace slot for the Eye of Agamotto.
PantheonSentListed as a dependency for 7.3.
Colorful HeartsListed as a dependency for 7.3.
Huge Structure BlocksPlaces Vormir's Edge, which is too big for normal structure blocks.

Installing

  1. Install Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.x.
  2. Put Infinity Stone Core and every required mod above in your mods folder.
  3. Add any of the optional mods below that you want.

On a server, the server and every player need the same mods.

Optional mods

None of these are needed. Each one adds something when it's installed.

Cosmic HorizonsA Marvel solar system with Morag, Svartalfheim and Vormir to fly to.

Ultor Infinitus (Infinity Ultron)A player with the InfinityUltron tag gets the Infinity Ultron look and can use the Mind Stone from their head.
PowerborneA thrown Stormbreaker within 10 blocks of someone wearing the gauntlet hurts them and switches off the Soul Stone's defenses.

Pugmeowla's addons

These need Infinity Stone Core and build on its content.

AddonWhat it adds
Pugmeowla's Infinity Generator7.3 adds the recipe for its generator: dwarf star alloy plates, steel blocks, a Nether Star and Palladium vibranium parts.
Pugmeowla's Infintrix (with Alien Evolution, Into the Omniverse or Omnitrix Evolution)An equipped Omnitrix works as a gauntlet. Hold a stone and right-click, no Infinity Gauntlet needed.
