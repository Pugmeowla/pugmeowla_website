Versions

Mod version
7.3 (these pages)
Minecraft
1.20.1
Loader
Forge 47.x
Type
A Palladium addon pack that loads like a mod (pack ID infinity)

Required mods

Palladium
The addon pack itself: powers, abilities, skill trees, render layers and the items and blocks.
KubeJS
The commands, Telekinesis, the Force Field and Intangibility protection, Immortality, the molten metals and the stone insert animation.
CoreWithStuff
The gauntlet's stone menu, the stones' power screens and the Space Stone's Teleport GUI. Without it the gauntlet falls back to the old insert system, which can have syncing problems, and the Teleport GUI stays locked.
GeckoLib
Animated and custom models: the Orb, gauntlet skins and Malekith.
Chrono API
The Time Stone's Slow Time and Time Freeze.
Pehkui
The Reality Stone's Size Changing and Invisibility, and the Mind Stone's Intangibility.
Curios API
Hands slot for the gauntlet, head slot for the Mind Stone and necklace slot for the Eye of Agamotto.
PantheonSent
Listed as a dependency for 7.3.
Colorful Hearts
Listed as a dependency for 7.3.
Huge Structure Blocks
Places Vormir's Edge, which is too big for normal structure blocks.

Installing

  1. Install Minecraft 1.20.1 with Forge 47.x.
  2. Put Infinity Stone Core and every required mod above in your mods folder.
  3. Add any of the optional mods below that you want.

On a server, the server and every player need the same mods.

Optional mods

None of these are needed. Each one adds something when it's installed.

Ad Astra
Morag, Svartalfheim and Vormir as planets. You need a Tier 4 rocket to get to them.
Cosmic Horizons
A Marvel solar system with Morag, Svartalfheim and Vormir to fly to.
Tinkers' Construct
Casting the gauntlet with the Infinity Gauntlet Cast (it replaces the normal recipe), and casting with the molten metals.
JEI
Recipes.
Fsang's Heropack
Steel and gold titanium share item tags with Fsang's, and Fsang's uru can be used for uru plates.
Create
An extra recipe for Aspects of Existence.
Pugmeowla's Herbs of Wakanda
The Black Panther necklace gives the Infinity Gauntlet power.
Ultor Infinitus (Infinity Ultron)
A player with the InfinityUltron tag gets the Infinity Ultron look and can use the Mind Stone from their head.
Powerborne
A thrown Stormbreaker within 10 blocks of someone wearing the gauntlet hurts them and switches off the Soul Stone's defenses.
Trinkets
The same as the Curios hands slot, for Fabric: the gauntlet works in the glove slot.

Pugmeowla's addons

These need Infinity Stone Core and build on its content.

Pugmeowla's Infinity Generator
7.3 adds the recipe for its generator: dwarf star alloy plates, steel blocks, a Nether Star and Palladium vibranium parts.
Pugmeowla's Infintrix
With Alien Evolution, Into the Omniverse or Omnitrix Evolution. An equipped Omnitrix works as a gauntlet. Hold a stone and right-click, no Infinity Gauntlet needed.
Pugmeowla's Infinity Protocol
The Iron Man MK85 suit from Satsu's Iron Man Addon gets the gauntlet power while you have an Infinity Gauntlet.
