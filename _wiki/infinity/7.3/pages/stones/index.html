Where a stone's power comes from

Holding the stone (either hand)
All six. The Power Stone blows up around you if you do this.
Holding the container (either hand)
Space (Tesseract), Reality (Aether), Time (Eye of Agamotto), Mind (Scepter)
Wearing the Eye of Agamotto
Time, on the chest or in the Curios necklace slot
Curios head slot
Only the Mind Stone gives its power there in 7.3
The {{page gauntlet/index.html Infinity Gauntlet}}
Every stone in it
That stone's Cosmi-Rod, in your main hand
One stone

In 7.3 the Power Stone's power is called infinity:power_stone_passive. The others use the same names as 8.0.

Abilities that need more than one stone

  • {{ab space/black_hole_on}} needs the Space and Power Stones.
  • {{ab power/meteor_storm}} needs the Power and Space Stones.
  • The rainbow {{ab power/power_blast_aim}} needs level 11 and all six stones.
  • {{ab soul/immortal}} and the {{page gauntlet/snap.html Snap}} need all six.
