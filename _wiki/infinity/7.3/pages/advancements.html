--- title: Advancements description: Every advancement in 7.3's Infinity Stone Core tab, what it says in game and how to get it. lead: The mod's advancement tab follows the hunt for the stones. icon: renders/infinity_gauntlet_cast.png ---

All of these are in their own Pugmeowla's Infinity Stone Core tab. The frame tells you how they show up: a task is a normal square, a goal is rounded and a challenge is spiky and plays the challenge sound.

Getting started

  • Pugmeowla's Infinity Stone CoreTask

    The start of infinite posibilities...

    Granted the first time you join a world with the mod. It opens the tab.

  • Infinity Is Within ReachTask

    Obtain the Infinity Gauntlet Cast

    Get an {{item infinity_gauntlet_cast}}, found in village weaponsmith chests.

  • "Fine, I'll do it myself"Challenge

    Create the Gauntlet

    Make the {{item infinity_gauntlet}}. Every container and stone below comes after this one.

  • It's Clobberin' Time!Task

    Obtain a hammer

    Get a {{item hammer}}.

  • It's called the Universal Weapon. Oh, really?Challenge

    Obtain the Cosmi-Rod

    Get the {{item cosmi-rod}} from a Crashed Kree Ship.

  • Dance BattleChallenge

    Kill a villager or player with the Cosmi-Rod

    Kill a villager or another player while holding the Cosmi-Rod in your main hand.

The stones

Each container has its own advancement, and its stone comes after it. The Soul Stone has no container.

  • The Power of a CelestialChallenge

    Obtain the Orb

    Get the {{item orb}} from the Morag Temple.

  • PowerChallenge

    Obtain the Power Stone

    Get the {{item power_stone}}. Comes after the Orb.

  • The Jewel of Odin's Treasure RoomChallenge

    Obtain the Tesseract

    Get the {{item tesseract}} from the Norse Village.

  • SpaceChallenge

    Obtain the Space Stone

    Get the {{item space_stone}}. Comes after the Tesseract.

  • The Converter of Matter into Dark MatterChallenge

    Obtain the Aether

    Get the {{item aether}} from the Aether Chamber.

  • RealityChallenge

    Obtain the Reality Stone

    Get the {{item reality_stone}}. Comes after the Aether.

  • SoulChallenge

    Obtain the Soul Stone

    Get the {{item soul_stone}} from the Soul Guardian on Vormir.

  • The Sorcerers QuestChallenge

    Obtain the Eye of Agamoto

    Get the {{item eye_of_agamotto}} from the Sanctum Sanctorum.

  • TimeChallenge

    Obtain the Time Stone

    Get the {{item time_stone}}. Comes after the Eye of Agamotto.

  • The Scepter of LokiChallenge

    Obtain the Scepter

    Get the {{item scepter}} from the Sanctuary.

  • MindChallenge

    Obtain the Mind Stone

    Get the {{item mind_stone}}. Comes after the Scepter.

The endgame

  • Six Stones, One GauntletChallenge

    Collect all 6 stones

    Put all six stones in your gauntlet.

  • I Am InevitableChallenge

    What did it cost?

    Pick Kill Half from the Snap wheel.

  • Peace in our TimeChallenge

    Everything...

    Pick Destroy Stones from the Snap wheel.

Hard to get

  • 1.5 Trillion AspectsChallenge

    How did you pull this off?!

    Nothing in 7.3 gives this one, so it can only be granted with commands. Comes after Power.
