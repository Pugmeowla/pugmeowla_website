The stone structures are placed like strongholds, one of each per world, thousands of blocks from 0, 0. The distances are on {{page structures.html Structures}}. /locate structure works in the right dimension.

Getting to the planets

  • The Space Stone: unlock Teleport Command and use /space_tp <x> <y> <z> <dimension>, for example /space_tp 212 130 99 infinity:vormir. See {{page commands.html#space-tp Commands}}.
  • Ad Astra: Morag, Svartalfheim and Vormir are Ad Astra planets. You need a Tier 4 rocket.
  • Cosmic Horizons: the planets are in its Marvel solar system.

The Orb's combination

In 7.3 the Orb has the same 15 turn combination in every world. Each wrong turn sends you back to the start. If you just want the answer:

Left, Right, Right, Left, Right, Left, Left, Right, Right, Left, Right, Left, Right, Right, Left

More on {{page stones/containers.html#orb the Orb}}.
