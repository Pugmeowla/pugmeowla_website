--- title: Commands description: The /chrono commands players and server admins use. lead: Deus Chrono-Machina registers one root command, /chrono. Almost all of it is used by the mod itself; this is the part that's useful to you. icon: deus/icons/detect.png ---

/chrono locate

/chrono locate

Needs permission level 2 (operators). Lists where Maltruant's five fragments and the altar are in the current world. Click a position to put a /tp command for it in your chat box. Anything not generated yet shows as unspawned.

/alienautoadd

An Alien Evolution command. Adds an alien to your Omnitrix without its DNA, so it's the quick way to try the Chronosapien.

Summoning mobs

/summon chrono:time_beast
/summon chrono:maltruant

Time Beasts and Maltruant summoned this way come through a time portal, like from their spawn eggs. Other entity IDs: chrono:exo_skull, chrono:subdora and chrono:ship.

Internal commands

The rest of /chrono (beamImpact, grayblock, timeflow, skillPulse, maltruant ai and so on) is run by Clockwork's Palladium powers and for debugging Maltruant. Running them by hand can leave time stopped or blocks gray, so leave them alone.
