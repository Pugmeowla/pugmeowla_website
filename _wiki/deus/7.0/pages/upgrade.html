Getting Ship

{{figure src="deus/models/ship.png" caption="Ship" pixel="false"}}
  • Find one. Alien Evolution crash sites have a small chance (0.1%) of a wild Ship nearby.
  • Tame it with a Galvan Circuit. Feed it Upgrade Crystals to change its color.
  • Carry it: sneak + right-click your Ship with an empty bucket to get a {{item ship_bucket}}.

More about Ship on the Mobs page.

Fusing and separating

{{figure src="deus/models/clockwork_upgrade.png" caption="The Upgrade fusion" pixel="false"}}
  1. Transform into the Chronosapien.
  2. Right-click your Ship. It wraps around you and you become the Upgrade fusion (the "Clockwork-Techno Bubble Fusion" advancement).
  3. To split up, use the Omnitrix's detransform or separate action. Ship pops back out next to you.

The fusion ends if your health drops to 2 hearts or less, so it can't carry you through a fight you're losing.
