Time Beast

{{figure src="deus/models/time_beast.png" caption="Time Beast" pixel="false"}} {{figure src="deus/photos/time_beast.jpg" caption="A Time Beast in game" pixel="false"}}

A beast that travels through time. Time Beasts guard Maltruant's Area and arrive through red time portals, both from their spawn egg and from /summon. Where they walk, blocks age into Aged Blocks and crumble. Full knockback resistance.

Drops: 2 to 4 {{item timeclock_gear}}, an Enchanted Golden Apple, an enchanted book (Time Reborn I most often, or Sharpness V, Protection V, Mending), gold, a Nether Star or Dragon's Breath, plus {{item timeclock_key}} (5%), {{item disc_timebeast}} (15% and 20%) and {{item chronosapien_dna}} (0.1%).

Maltruant's devotees

Exo-Skull and Subdora are hunting Maltruant's fragments too. The first time you get within 16 blocks of a fragment, two portals open next to you and they come through, one on each side. They can also turn up during raids (15% chance per raid, once). Neither will fight Maltruant.

Ship

{{figure src="deus/models/ship.png" caption="Ship" pixel="false"}}

A Galvanic Mechamorph (20 health, 10 armor). Wild ones are rare, near Alien Evolution crash sites. Tame it with a Galvan Circuit, recolor it with Upgrade Crystals, and right-click it as Clockwork to fuse into the Upgrade form. Sneak + right-click with an empty bucket for a {{item ship_bucket}}.
