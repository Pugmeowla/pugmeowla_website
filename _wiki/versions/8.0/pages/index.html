What's in it

Every stone is a Palladium power with its own skill tree. A stone works on its own when you hold it, inside its container (Tesseract, Scepter, Aether or Eye of Agamotto), in the Curios head slot, in a Cosmi-Rod, or together with the others in the Infinity Gauntlet. You buy abilities with Aspects of Existence, which you find in Infinity Temples. Once all six stones are in one gauntlet you can snap, and the Snap burns your arm for a whole day.
