Textures and inspiration

Textures and assets come from these mods, which this one carries on from:

  • Infinity Craft
  • Lucraft Core
  • Heroes Expansion (the Norse Village and Crashed Kree Ship are redone versions of its structures)
  • Speedster Heroes
  • Star Tech, Man! The Legendary Mod? (the Power Stone's cyclone, rockets, radiation and the Cosmi-Rod's Shockwave)

The Infinity Gauntlet Datapack by GoldSlimeling91 was an inspiration too. Vormir's Edge was built by DogBoi64.
