Putting a skin on

  1. Wear your Infinity Gauntlet (off hand or Curios hands slot) with no skin on it.
  2. Hold the skin's unlock item in your main hand. The skin's ability shows up in the gauntlet's ability bar.
  3. Right-click. One unlock item gets used up, the skin goes on and a sound plays (an anvil, for most of them).

The skin is saved on that gauntlet, so it keeps it worn, held, on the ground or in a chest. Every skin also has an entry in the gauntlet's skill tree with its description. If you're holding an Infinity Gauntlet in your main hand, the skin goes on that one instead of the one you're wearing.

A few work differently. The Gauntlet with Fingers goes on as soon as you hold its item, the Nano Gauntlet can go over the Burnt Gauntlet, and the Fantastic Four Skin doesn't show in the ability bar.

Where new skins come from

New skins come from donations on Ko-fi. If you want one:

  • You send the texture, and the model too if it's a custom shape. Custom models have to keep the stones where they are.
  • You pick the item that unlocks it. It can be a custom item, but it has to be obtainable in survival with a single crafting recipe.
  • More details on the Discord.

Commands

/infinity gauntlet skin set <skin> and /infinity gauntlet skin clear change the skin of the gauntlet in your main hand (or the one you're wearing) directly. See {{page commands.html#infinity-gauntlet-skin Commands}}.

Making your own

A skin is one JSON file and its textures. No Java, models or render layers needed. See {{page addons/skins.html Skin Files}}.
