Getting one

{{itemcards infinity_gauntlet infinity_gauntlet_cast}}

Find an {{item infinity_gauntlet_cast}} in a village weaponsmith's chest, then make the gauntlet in a crafting table or cast it with Tinkers' Construct (JEI has the recipe). The gauntlet doesn't stack, is Epic rarity and doesn't burn.

Where to wear it

The gauntlet gives you the infinity:infinity_gauntlet power, and every stone inside it, while it's in one of these slots:

SlotNotes
Curios handsThe best spot. It's drawn on your arm, in first person too. The Curios visibility (eye) button hides it and its stones.
Off handWorks the same, but you can't see it in first person.
Main handWorks and has its own held model, but your main hand is taken up and some abilities are made for the off hand.

If you have more than one, the off hand gauntlet wins, then the Curios hands one, then any {{page addons/custom-gauntlets.html custom gauntlet}} you're wearing. Your off arm shows while the gauntlet is worn, even with nothing in it.

Other things that work as a gauntlet
  • Pugmeowla's Infintrix (with Alien Evolution, Into the Omniverse or Omnitrix Evolution): an equipped Omnitrix works as a gauntlet. Hold a stone and right-click, no Infinity Gauntlet needed. Infintrix sets this up as a custom gauntlet.
  • Pugmeowla's Herbs of Wakanda: the Black Panther necklace, on your chest or in the Curios necklace slot, gives the Infinity Gauntlet power.

Controls

InputWhat it does
G (Open Gauntlet)Opens the stone menu of the gauntlet in your main hand, or the one you're wearing (custom gauntlets too). You can change the key under Controls, Infinity Stones.
Right-click with the gauntlet in your main handOpens its stone menu.
Shift-click a stone in your inventory (menu open)Puts it in a free slot. The six Infinity Stones go to their own slot if it's free.
Palladium ability keysUse the stones' abilities and the gauntlet's own (the Snap, skins).

The stone menu

{{figure src="gui/infinity_gauntlet.png" caption="The stone menu (textures/gui/infinity_gauntlet.png). Six sockets above your inventory."}}
  • Six slots, one per socket on the gauntlet. By default the order is Space, Mind, Reality, Power, Time, Soul.
  • Any stone fits any slot. A stone is drawn on whichever socket you put it in.
  • The same stone can't go in twice, and each slot holds one stone.
  • Anything set up as a stone fits, including stones from addons (see {{page addons/stones.html Custom Stones}}).
  • If you opened the menu from your hand, that gauntlet is locked in your hotbar until you close it.
  • The stones are stored on the gauntlet item, so they go wherever the gauntlet goes. Drop it, put it in a chest or give it to a friend.

Putting a stone in plays the insert sound, puffs particles in the stone's color and plays a short hand animation. Taking one out plays the removal sound. You get or lose the stone's power straight away.

The Time Stone and the Eye

When the Time Stone leaves your worn gauntlet, the gauntlet runs clear @s infinity:eye_of_agamotto, which removes any Eye of Agamotto in your inventory. Put the Eye in a chest first if you're moving the stone between them.

Tooltip and looks

  • The tooltip lists the stones inside in their colors, always in the same order (Power, Space, Reality, Soul, Time, Mind, then addon stones).
  • The inventory icon colors each socket pixel with the stone in that slot.
  • Worn, held or on the ground, each stone is drawn as a glowing cube in its socket. Skins change the gauntlet around the stones, see {{page gauntlet/skins.html Gauntlet Skins}}.
  • On the ground the gauntlet stands upright and keeps its stones and skin. Right-click to pick it up.

The gauntlet's skill tree

The infinity:infinity_gauntlet power has a tree too. It has info entries (use the Curios hands slot, Trinkets for Fabric, Infintrix omnitrixes, the Snap and what happens after), one entry per {{page gauntlet/skins.html skin}}, and {{ab gauntlet/normal_gauntlet}}. The {{page gauntlet/snap.html Snap}} abilities show up in the ability bar once all six stones are in.

Trinkets

The mod has Trinkets data (a glove slot) for a Fabric build, but 8.0 is Forge only, so use Curios.
