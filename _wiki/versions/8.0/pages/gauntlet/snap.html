What you need

  • All six stones in your gauntlet, so you have all six stone powers at once.
  • Not still being burnt from your last Snap.

The four abilities then show up in the gauntlet's ability bar. Each one needs a second press within 3 seconds. The first press only shows a warning, like "Press again to snap away half of all life".

What happens when you snap

There's a flash at your hand, and everyone hears a Wither scream and a crack of thunder. Over the next second the targets turn to white dust, a few each tick.

SnapWhoWhere they go
Kill HalfHalf of all living things in your current dimension, players included, but not you. It takes half of each chunk on its own (an odd one out is a coin flip), so no area is spared and none is wiped out completely.Killed.
Kill All HostileEvery hostile mob in every dimension, meaning everything in the #infinity:snap_hostile tag (zombies, skeletons, creepers, spiders, endermen, illagers, the Warden, the Wither, the Ender Dragon and more).The Snap cell in the Soulworld.
Kill Other PlayersEvery other player on the server.The Snap cell in the Soulworld.

The Snap cell is a closed, lit bedrock room at 1000 78 1000 in the Soulworld with one oak door. Players who got snapped can walk out and make their way home, see {{page dimensions.html#soulworld The Soulworld}}.

Only one Snap can happen on a server at a time. If one's already going (or the Soulworld is missing) you get "A snap is already in progress or the Soulworld is unavailable."

Destroy Stones

Press it twice to start a 7 second ritual. You're slowed to a stop, a beacon hum plays, and every second a burst in one stone's color goes off with a rising chime. One press before it finishes cancels it. When it finishes:

  • All the stones in your gauntlet are destroyed.
  • You get burnt (see below) and sent to the Soulworld at Y 72.
  • You get the Peace in our Time advancement.

The burn

Every Snap and Destroy Stones burns whoever did it:

  • Your max health drops to half a heart, and you're healed to that.
  • Most stone abilities lock. The Space Stone's Teleport GUI and Jump Teleport and the Power Stone's Radiation Texture Toggle still work. You can't snap again.
  • Your arm is drawn burnt and the gauntlet gets the Burnt Gauntlet skin. If it had a skin before, you get that skin's unlock item back.
  • It lasts one full Minecraft day (24,000 ticks, about 20 minutes). Dying doesn't end it early.

Once the day is over your health and abilities come back by themselves.

Fixing the gauntlet

The {{page gauntlet/skins.html#skin-nano-gauntlet Nano Gauntlet skin}} (unlocked with a Palladium Vibranium Flux Capacitor) is the only skin you can put over the Burnt Gauntlet while you're burnt. It fixes how the gauntlet and your arm look, but the half heart and locked abilities stay until the day is up.

For server admins

Operators can do any of the Snaps without a gauntlet using /infinity gauntlet snap. It burns whoever runs it, same as the real thing.

Advancements: I Am Inevitable ("What did it cost?") for snapping away half of all life with the gauntlet, and Peace in our Time for destroying the stones.
