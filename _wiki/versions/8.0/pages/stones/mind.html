You get the Mind Stone's power by holding the stone or the Scepter in either hand, wearing the stone in the Curios head slot, putting it in your gauntlet, or putting it in a Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Scepter and left-click it to split it into the Mind Stone and an {{item empty_scepter}}. Right-click the Empty Scepter on the ground with the stone to put it back together.

How some of it works

Pacify and the peaceful team

Pacify uses a scoreboard team called peaceful that the mod makes when the world loads. Mobs on your team don't target you. The Soul Stone's {{ab soul/summon_zombie}} puts its zombies on the same team, so with both stones you get a zombie army that's on your side.

Clones

Clones are their own entity, infinity:clone. Casting Mind Illusions again replaces the ones you have. They aren't saved with the world, so a restart gets rid of them, and they don't give souls. Killing one also doesn't count as killing a pet for the Best Friend's Soul.

Disguise

The name you pick is saved on you and sent to everyone, along with your name tag. It has to be a valid Minecraft name. The skin is loaded the same way as any player skin.

Mental immunity

Anyone channeling the Mind Stone has mental immunity, a hidden passive that's always on. Other players' Mind Reading can't read them, Pacify doesn't put them on the peaceful team, and they see a disguised player's real skin and name. Addons can give it to other powers, see {{page addons/immunities.html Immunities}}.

Reading minds from afar

With the Soul Stone's {{ab soul/locateplayer_command}} unlocked too, Mind Reading shows where its target is and in which dimension. Use it while looking at nothing and a menu opens: type the name of a player, or of a mob named with a name tag, to read their mind wherever they are.
