Where a stone's power comes from

Each stone is a Palladium power: infinity:space_stone, infinity:mind_stone, infinity:reality_stone, infinity:power_stone, infinity:time_stone and infinity:soul_stone. You have it whenever one of these is true:

SourceStonesNotes
Holding the stone itself (either hand)All sixThe Power Stone hurts you if you hold it like this.
Holding its container (either hand)Space ({{item tesseract}}), Mind ({{item scepter}}), Reality ({{item aether}}), Time ({{item eye_of_agamotto}} with the stone in it)The Orb doesn't give you the Power Stone's power.

Curios head slotAll sixNeeds Curios.

{{item cosmi-rod}} with the stone in its socketAny one stoneHeld in either hand.

Unlocked abilities are saved on you, not on the stone. If you lose the stone you lose the power, and when you get it back your unlocks are still there. Some abilities, like flight, also check where the stone is coming from when you use them.

Things every stone does

  • On the ground a stone lies still. It can't be destroyed, burnt, blown up or despawn, and it rests at the bottom of the world instead of falling out. Right-click to pick it up.
  • No copies: while a player is carrying a stone, any copy of it on the ground disappears.
  • One Power Stone and one Time Stone per world: the Orb and the Eye of Agamotto remember for the whole world whether their stone has been taken.
  • Look: every stone uses the LucraftCore stone cube, tinted in its color, unlit, with two scrolling glints. It looks the same in your inventory, in the gauntlet and on the ground.
