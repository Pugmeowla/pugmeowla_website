You get the Space Stone's power by holding the stone or the Tesseract in either hand, wearing the stone in the Curios head slot, putting it in your gauntlet, or putting it in a Cosmi-Rod. To get it out of the Tesseract, drop the Tesseract and hit it.

Its {{ab space/teleport}} is the easiest way to Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim, which is why it's worth getting first.

How portals work

  • Portals come in pairs. One end opens 2.5 blocks in front of you and the other at the destination. Each end takes about 3 seconds to open fully.
  • Anything that walks into one end, players and mobs, comes out the other on safe ground. There's a one second cooldown per entity so you don't bounce straight back.
  • Teleport GUI portals stay open until someone hits one end. Then both close. The chunks at both ends stay loaded while they're open.
  • Jump Teleport portals close by themselves after 3 seconds. If there's no block in range you get "No block found within range for the portal."
  • Addons can change the menu background, the portal color and size, and switch to a fire style (an orange ring of flames, like a sling ring portal). See {{page addons/abilities.html Ability Reference}}.

Telekinesis controls

InputWhile you're holding something
Ability keyTurns it off and puts the thing down. Blocks go back into the world if they can.
Mouse wheelMoves it closer or further away (2 to 24 blocks).
Left-clickThrows it. Faster and bigger things hit harder.
Right-clickLocks it in place, once you have {{ab space/spatial_lock}}.

A block you're holding turns into a floating block entity. If it can't go back into the world it drops as an item after 30 seconds. Picking up something that's spatially locked unlocks it.

Tips

  • Force Field stops you moving, so it's for tanking hits, not running away. Jump Teleport is for running away.
  • Black Hole and Meteor Storm both need the Power Stone too. Put both in a gauntlet and you get all of them.
  • The Teleport GUI and Jump Teleport still work after the Snap, when the rest of the stone abilities are locked.
