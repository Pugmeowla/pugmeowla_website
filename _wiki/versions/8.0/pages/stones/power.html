You get the Power Stone's power by holding it, wearing it in the Curios head slot, putting it in your gauntlet, or putting it in a Cosmi-Rod. The Orb is different from the other containers: it does not give you the power. You have to crack its lock and take the stone out. There's only one Power Stone per world.

Cosmic radiation

Holding the Power Stone in your main or off hand, when it isn't also in your gauntlet, head slot or a Cosmi-Rod, isn't safe. After half a second it overloads. You get Wither, you can't move, a cyclone of energy fires rockets around you, and radiation cracks spread over your skin. Get it somewhere safe quickly.

Levels

The Power Stone has a level from 0 to 11, stored in the DamageCycle scoreboard objective and shown on the purple bar. Buying levels in the tree raises your highest level. Damage Control and the mouse wheel set your current level. While you have the stone your base attack damage is 2level, and Energy Blast, Power Blast, Rocket Burst and Meteor Storm all scale with it. Level 11 with all six stones counts as full power and the damage goes infinite. The full table is on {{page progression.html#power-levels Power Levels}}.

Radiation cracks

The glowing cracks on your skin during Power Cyclone (or when the stone overloads) can be turned off with the free {{ab power/radiation_texture_toggle}}. It still works while you're burnt from the Snap.
