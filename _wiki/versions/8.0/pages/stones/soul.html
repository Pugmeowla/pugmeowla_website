You get the Soul Stone's power by holding it, wearing it in the Curios head slot, putting it in your gauntlet, or putting it in a Cosmi-Rod.

Getting it

  1. Kill one of your own tamed pets. The first time you do, you get a {{item best_friends_soul}} ("How Could You").
  2. Go to Vormir, either with the Space Stone's {{ab space/teleport}} (dimension infinity:vormir, around X 212, Z 99) or with a rocket.
  3. Trade the Best Friend's Soul to the Soul Guardian for the Soul Stone.

More about the Soul Guardian and its other trades is on {{page finding-stones.html#soul-stone Finding the Stones}}.

The Soulworld

Enter Soulworld takes you and everything within 5 blocks to infinity:soulworld, a flat orange world of smooth red sandstone with a thin layer of water on top. Everyone lands at Y 72 above the same X and Z. Use the same slot again (it turns into Exit Soulworld) to send everyone back to where they were. More on {{page dimensions.html#soulworld Dimensions}}.

When the defenses stop working

  • After the Snap: while you're burnt, Health, Regeneration and Immortality are locked, along with most other stone abilities.
  • Powerborne's Stormbreaker: if you're wearing the Infinity Gauntlet and a thrown Stormbreaker from the Powerborne addon gets within 10 blocks of you, Saturation, Effect Immunity, Regeneration, Health and Immortality lock and you take 1 damage every tick.
