You get the Reality Stone's power by holding the stone or the Aether in either hand, wearing the stone in the Curios head slot, putting it in your gauntlet, or putting it in a Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Aether and hit it to get the stone. To turn it back into the Aether, hold the stone in your off hand and use {{ab aether/insert_reality_stone}} with an {{item empty_aether}}, or right-click an Empty Aether on the ground while holding the stone.

Size Changing steps

Each scroll while Size Control is on moves one step. These are the height and width scales set through Pehkui:

DirectionSteps
Scroll up (bigger)2×, 3×, 4×, 5×. You also move faster.
Normal1×
Scroll down (smaller)0.9×, 0.8×, 0.7×, 0.6×, 0.5×, 0.4×, 0.3×, 0.2×, 0.1×
Pehkui

Size Changing, and the scaling part of Invisibility, use Pehkui's /scale command. They don't do anything without Pehkui.

Copy and paste

  • Copy works up to 30 blocks away and won't copy unbreakable blocks.
  • Paste changes the block you're looking at, or every non-air block within 5 blocks of it while you're sneaking.
  • Paste checks build permissions and claims, so it won't work on land you can't build on, and it never touches unbreakable blocks.
  • Your saved block stays through death and relogging. The Paste icon shows what it is.
