Orb

{{itemcards orb}}

The Orb from the Morag Temple vault has a combination lock. It does not give you the Power Stone's power while it's closed. Hold it in either hand and its abilities show up in the ability bar. You hold it in both hands in front of your chest, a half in each, and in first person it sits in the middle of your view. Rotate Left rolls the left half down and Rotate Right the right half up, and opening it spins both back into line and pulls them apart around the Power Stone:

{{abilities orb}}

Unlocking the combination

Get a pen and paper ready

Unlocking the Orb is trial and error. Write down every turn as you find it, because one wrong turn sends you back to the start.

  • Every world has its own random sequence of left and right turns, 15 turns long by default. The length is in the {{page configuration.html#config config}}, and changing it makes a new sequence.
  • Use Rotate Left and Rotate Right. A right turn just clicks. A wrong one plays a low note, puffs smoke and sends you back to the start.
  • A wrong turn tells you the other direction was right for that step, so write the sequence down as you go.
  • After the last correct turn an iron door sound plays, sparks fly, and the Orb is unlocked for you. Unlocking is per player, but the sequence is the same for everyone in the world.

Opening it and taking the stone

  • Once it's unlocked, Open / Close Orb opens the shell and the Power Stone grows into view. You can open it as often as you like after that.
  • While it's open the Power Stone is exposed: it's as unstable as holding the bare stone, with the explosions that come with it, but none of its abilities can be used until you take it out.
  • With the Orb open, right-click to take the Power Stone. There's only one per world, so if someone already took it you'll see "The Power Stone has already been taken from an Orb."
  • To put it back, open the Orb and right-click while holding the Power Stone in your other hand.
  • Lock Orb locks it for you again and forgets that you opened it, if you want to unlock it again.
  • Picking the Orb up again starts the lock over, unless you've already unlocked it, in which case it stays open for you.

Operators can skip the puzzle with /infinity unlock_orb.

Tesseract

{{itemcards tesseract}}

The Tesseract was kept in Odin's vault before it ended up in the Norse Village, looked after by the Asgardian worshippers there. In either hand it gives you the Space Stone's power, and it counts as a Space source for {{ab power/meteor_storm}}. Its glowing core spins inside the cube like it did in Heroes Expansion.

Jumping between dimensions

Right-click with the Tesseract to jump to the next dimension, like the Heroes Expansion Tesseract (which picked one at random). No skill tree unlock is needed.

  • It goes through every dimension on the server in turn: the Overworld, the Nether and the End first, then all the others (this mod's planets and other mods' dimensions) by ID. Keep clicking to cycle round.
  • You land on solid, safe ground near the same spot (scaled like a Nether portal), otherwise near that world's spawn, otherwise near 0, 0. A dimension with nowhere safe to stand is skipped. If none has one, you get "The Tesseract finds nowhere safe to go."
  • For 5 seconds after you land, the new world itself can't hurt you (no air, another mod's heat or cold, suffocating). Mobs and players still can.
  • There's a one second cooldown between jumps.
  • The Soulworld is left out by default. Server owners can leave out more dimensions, or whole mods, in the {{page configuration.html#config config}}.

Opening it

Drop it and left-click it to break it open. The Tesseract rises in a growing burst of blue rays for 5 seconds and ends in a sphere of light, and then the Space Stone falls out. You can't pick it up while it's opening.

Aether

{{itemcards aether empty_aether}}

The Aether is the Reality Stone in liquid form, the weapon Malekith tried to use to put the universe back into darkness. In either hand it gives the Reality Stone's power. Left-click it on the ground and its lids swing open, red light bursts out and pours upward in a column for 5 seconds, and it ends in an explosion that leaves the Reality Stone behind. Eye of Agamotto

{{itemcards eye_of_agamotto}}

Agamotto, the first Sorcerer Supreme, made the Eye to hold the Time Stone, and the Masters of the Mystic Arts kept it in the Sanctum Sanctorum. Wear it on your chest (it counts as iron armor) or in the Curios necklace slot. While it still has the stone, wearing or holding it gives you the Time Stone's power. Its abilities:

Be careful where you wear it

In the chest slot the Eye takes damage like any armor and can break, and the Time Stone is lost with it. In the Curios necklace slot it never breaks, so wear it there if you can.

{{abilities eye}}
  • Open Eye opens it with an animation, and the stone starts glowing once it's open. It stays open (or closed) when you take it off, put it on a suit stand or put it back on.
  • Remove Time Stone: right-click with an empty hand while it's open. It gives you the stone and leaves the Eye empty. There's only one Time Stone per world, so if it's already gone you'll see "The Time Stone has already been taken from an Eye of Agamotto."
  • Insert Time Stone: right-click while it's open and empty, holding the Time Stone in either hand.
  • Hitting the Eye while it's on the ground also lets the stone out. The Eye stays where it is, and always lies there closed.
  • Every Eye shows the world's one Time Stone: once it's out, every Eye of Agamotto item and worn Eye is drawn without it, and crafting a new Eye (which uses up a Time Stone) puts it back in all of them.
  • Old Empty Eye of Agamotto items from earlier versions still work, and turn into the normal Eye when you put the stone in.

Scepter

{{itemcards scepter empty_scepter}}

Loki's Scepter is an iron tier sword with the Mind Stone in it. In either hand it gives the Mind Stone's power and the {{ab scepter/scepter_blast}}, fired from the arm holding it. Left-click it on the ground to split it into the Mind Stone and an Empty Scepter. Right-click an Empty Scepter on the ground while holding the Mind Stone to put it back together (or the other way round).

The Scepter, the Aether and the Eye of Agamotto glow brighter while you use one of their stone's glowing abilities, like the stones themselves. See {{page stones/index.html#glow The Infinity Stones}}.

Items on the ground

The stones, all the containers, the gauntlet, the Cosmi-Rod and both swords behave differently when you drop or throw them:

  • They land where you threw them and lie flat instead of floating and spinning.
  • They can't be destroyed, burnt or blown up, and they never despawn.
  • If one with a stone in it (or a bare stone) falls into the void, it reappears at a random spot on the Overworld's surface within 20,000 blocks of spawn, and the whole server is told where. Anything else rests at the bottom of the world.
  • Walking over them doesn't pick them up. Right-click them. They keep all their data (a gauntlet's stones and skin, names, enchantments).
  • Containers with a stone break open when you hit them, and stones and empty containers join up when you right-click one with the other. The Tesseract and the Aether play an opening animation first.
  • While someone is carrying a stone, any copy of it on the ground disappears, so stones can't be duplicated.

Addons can make their own items work like this, see {{page addons/ground-items.html Ground Items}}.
