Highlights

  • A real Java mod. 7.3 was an addon pack held together with KubeJS scripts, Palladium functions and CoreWithStuff. 8.0 is rebuilt from the ground up as a standard Java mod that's still a Palladium addon, so it's faster, more reliable and needs far fewer other mods.
  • Every stone got bigger. Around 25 new abilities, including Mind Illusions, Mind Reading, Disguise, Spatial Lock, Jump Teleport, Energy Blast, Super Punch, Rocket Burst, Power Cyclone, Soul Storage, Soul Heal and Soul Drain.
  • A new gauntlet. Any stone fits any slot, its own stone menu opens with G, the stones stay on the gauntlet item, and skins belong to the gauntlet.
  • A new Snap. Four separate abilities you press twice to confirm, and Kill Half really kills now.
  • New ways to travel. Space Stone portals rebuilt from scratch, see-through portals with Immersive Portals, and a Tesseract that jumps between dimensions.
  • Everything under /infinity. All commands moved there, with new admin commands.
  • Built for addons. JSON systems for custom stones, skins, gauntlets, ground items and skill tree themes.

The Snap

  • 7.3's Snap wheel with five options is now four separate abilities: Kill Half of All Entities, Kill All Hostile, Kill Other Players and Destroy Stones. Each one needs pressing twice within 3 seconds, so you can't snap by accident.
  • Kill Half really kills. In 7.3 the snapped half was sent to the Soulworld. Now they turn to dust. Kill All Hostile and Kill Other Players still send their targets to a locked cell in the Soulworld.
  • Bring Back the Snapped is gone, since nothing is stored in the Soulworld any more.
  • Destroy Stones is a 7 second ritual with a burst for each stone, and it earns Peace in our Time.
  • The burn works the same way: half a heart and locked abilities for a Minecraft day.

More on {{page gauntlet/snap.html The Snap}}.

The stones

  • All six now work in the Curios head slot. In 7.3 only the Mind Stone did.
  • Stones glow brighter while you use their abilities, and so do the Scepter, Aether and Eye of Agamotto. See {{page stones/index.html#glow The Infinity Stones}}.
  • Each stone's skill tree is drawn in its own color, and most passives have a free toggle so you can switch them off.
  • The Power Stone's power is now infinity:power_stone (it was infinity:power_stone_passive).

{{stone power}}

  • New: {{ab power/impower}} (a damaging bolt that scales with your power level) replaces 7.3's Impower, which gave Health Boost and Resistance.
  • New: {{ab power/super_punch}}, {{ab power/rocket_burst}} and {{ab power/power_cyclone}}.
  • Changed: Cosmic Explosion was a ring of TNT around you. It's now the unlock for Rocket Burst and Power Cyclone.
  • Changed: {{ab power/meteor_storm}} breaks a moon apart over the spot you look at, sized by your power level, instead of dropping 20 deepslate meteors.
  • Changed: at full power, {{ab power/power_blast_aim}} explodes where it hits instead of summoning TNT.
  • Changed: holding the bare stone used to set off a ring of TNT and give you Wither. Now the stone becomes unstable, and a cyclone of energy fires rockets around you.

{{stone space}}

  • New: {{ab space/jump_teleport}} (a portal pair to the block you look at) replaces 7.3's look-at Teleport.
  • New: {{ab space/spatial_lock}} freezes whatever you hold with Telekinesis in place.
  • Removed: the Teleport Command and /space_tp. The rebuilt {{ab space/teleport}} menu does the same job without CoreWithStuff.
  • Changed: {{ab space/telekinesis}} picks up blocks as well as mobs, is a toggle instead of a hold, and throws what you hold like a projectile.
  • Changed: {{ab space/black_hole}} follows where you look and can be thrown, instead of staying put.
  • Changed: the tree starts at Force Field instead of being one long line, and the portals work with Immersive Portals.

{{stone reality}}

  • Changed: Block Duplication, which added blocks you looked at to your inventory, became Copy Block and Paste Block. Copy any block, then turn other blocks (or everything around you) into it.
  • Removed: Effects, which gave everything within 100 blocks Blindness, Nausea, Darkness and Slowness.

{{stone soul}}

  • New: {{ab soul/soul_storage}}, {{ab soul/soul_heal}} and {{ab soul/soul_drain}}. Kill things to store up to 50 souls, spend them on healing, or pull the soul out of a mob.
  • Changed: Exit Soulworld sends everyone back to the exact spot they came from. In 7.3 it dropped them above the same X and Z in the Overworld.
  • Changed: {{ab soul/effect_immune}} stops bad effects from being applied at all, instead of clearing them every tick.

{{stone time}}

  • Changed: {{ab time/projectile_stop}} catches every projectile within 6 blocks (not just a set list), holds it as long as it's near you and lets it go 2 seconds after.
  • Changed: the Time Command uses /infinity time set and always moves time forward.
  • Changed: Slow Time and Freeze Time talk to Chrono API directly instead of running its commands, and keep you moving at normal speed.

{{stone mind}}

  • New: {{ab mind/clones}}, {{ab mind/mind_reading}} and {{ab mind/disguise}}.
  • Changed: {{ab mind/intangibility}} has a 10 second cooldown.

Worlds and exploration

  • Vormir's Edge is built one chunk at a time as you explore, instead of all at once, so Huge Structure Blocks isn't needed.
  • The Garden is now a planet in Ad Astra too.
  • The world remembers whether the Power Stone and Time Stone were taken in its own save data, instead of a barrier block on Morag.
  • Six new advancements, including a hidden one. See {{page advancements.html Advancements}}.
  • Old 7.3 worlds are converted when they load. What changes is on {{page configuration.html#world-upgrades Configuration}}.

For addon makers and server owners

  • {{page addons/stones.html Custom stones}}, {{page addons/skins.html gauntlet skins}}, {{page addons/custom-gauntlets.html custom gauntlets}} with their own menus, {{page addons/ground-items.html ground items}}, {{page addons/stones.html#containers glowing stone containers}} and {{page addons/ability-overlays.html skill tree themes}}, all from JSON, in your own namespace so addons can't overwrite each other.
  • {{page addons/immunities.html Immunities}}: mental, reality, soul and spatial immunity, as abilities, conditions, tags and a Java API.
  • 48 ability types and 18 conditions you can use in your own Palladium powers. See the {{page addons/abilities.html Ability Reference}}.
  • A {{page configuration.html#config config file}} (with switches for the real and fake structures) and /infinity ability disable to switch any ability off on a server.
