Getting one

Find an {{item infinity_gauntlet_cast}} in a village weaponsmith's chest and make the gauntlet (JEI has the recipe). With Tinkers' Construct installed you cast it instead.

Where to wear it

The gauntlet gives you the infinity:infinity_gauntlet power in your off hand, main hand, the Curios hands slot or the Trinkets glove slot. The off hand or Curios hands slot works best, and it can't be seen in first person when it's in your off hand.

  • With Pugmeowla's Infintrix and an Omnitrix mod, an equipped Omnitrix works as a gauntlet. Hold a stone and right-click.
  • The Black Panther necklace from Pugmeowla's Herbs of Wakanda gives the gauntlet power too.

The stone menu

The menu comes from CoreWithStuff. Hold the gauntlet in your main hand and right-click, or use Open GUI while you're wearing it. Each stone has its own slot:

Slot123456
Stone{{stone soul}}{{stone reality}}{{stone space}}{{stone power}}{{stone time}}{{stone mind}}

Putting a stone in gives you its power, plays the insert sound and a short arm animation. Taking it out takes the power away. The menu is per player, and the gauntlet's tree warns that the old insert system can have syncing problems.

The gauntlet's skill tree

The tree has info entries (Curios, Trinkets, Infintrix, CoreWithStuff and the Snap), an entry for every {{page gauntlet/skins.html skin}}, and Return to Original Gauntlet. The {{page gauntlet/snap.html Snap wheel}} shows up once all six stones are in.
