Weapons

The {{item double_edged_sword}} (12 damage, 1.0 speed) and {{item single_edged_sword}} (10 damage, 2.0 speed) are netherite tier. The {{item scepter}} is an iron tier sword. The Cosmi-Rod has a separate item for each stone, see Cosmi-Rod.

Thanos armor

Classic and MCU sets of netherite armor, creative only.

Fancy Bottle

Hold a {{item fancy_bottle}} and spend 3 experience levels in its power screen to turn it into a Bottle o' Enchanting.

Materials

Titanium, steel, gold titanium alloy, dwarf star alloy and uru, each with dusts, ingots, nuggets and plates. {{item uru_ore}} is in the meteor craters on Svartalfheim, and {{item dwarf_star_meteor}} in Overworld craters.

Blocks

Storage blocks for each metal, the ores, and the ground blocks of Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim. There's no Temple Beacon in 7.3.
