Stone structures

One of each per world, on a ring around 0, 0.

Norse Village

Overworld, about 18,500 to 35,300 blocks out. Has the {{item tesseract}}.

Sanctum Sanctorum

Overworld, about 7,300 to 14,000 blocks out. Has the {{item eye_of_agamotto}}.

Sanctuary

The End, about 7,300 to 14,000 blocks out. Has the {{item scepter}}.

Aether Chamber

Underground on Svartalfheim, about 4,400 to 8,400 blocks out. Has the {{item aether}}, guarded by Malekith.

Morag Temple

On the ocean floor of Morag, about 18,500 to 35,300 blocks out, with Arrows of Harming VII traps. Has the {{item orb}}.

Vormir's Edge

Built in one go the first time the world loads, at 0 63 0 on Vormir, with the Soul Guardian at 212 121 99.

Other structures

Infinity Temple

Common across the Overworld. The chest has 1 to 4 {{item aspects_of_existance}} and other loot.

Crashed Kree Ship

In plains. Has the {{item cosmi-rod}}.

Dwarf Star Meteor

Craters with {{item dwarf_star_meteor}} all over the Overworld.

Uru Meteor

Craters with {{item uru_ore}} on Svartalfheim.
