Overview

The Reality Stone is the {{item aether}}, deep in the Aether Chamber on Svartalfheim. Hold the stone or the Aether, put it in the gauntlet or use the Reality Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Aether and hit it to get the stone out, and use an {{item empty_aether}} to put it back.
