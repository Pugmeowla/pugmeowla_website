Overview

The Mind Stone is in the {{item scepter}} in the Sanctuary in the End. Hold the stone or the Scepter, wear the stone in the Curios head slot, put it in the gauntlet or use the Mind Cosmi-Rod. Drop the Scepter and hit it to get the stone out.
