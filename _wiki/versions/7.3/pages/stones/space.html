Overview

The Space Stone is in the {{item tesseract}} in the Norse Village. Hold the stone or the Tesseract, put it in the gauntlet or use the Space Cosmi-Rod to get its power. Drop the Tesseract and hit it to get the stone out.

Skill tree costs
