Overview

The Power Stone is in the {{item orb}} in the Morag Temple. You have to unlock the Orb to get it out (see the Orb). In 7.3 its power is called infinity:power_stone_passive, and you get it from holding the stone, the gauntlet or the Power Cosmi-Rod.

Unprotected

Holding the bare stone without it also being in the gauntlet sets off a ring of TNT around you after half a second and gives you Wither. Put it in the gauntlet or a Cosmi-Rod.
