Getting it

  1. Kill one of your own tamed pets to get a {{item best_friends_soul}}.
  2. Go to Vormir with /space_tp or a rocket.
  3. Trade the soul to the Soul Guardian at the top of Vormir's Edge.

7.3 doesn't have Soul Storage, Soul Heal or Soul Drain. Those came in 8.0. The defensive passives switch off while you're burnt from the Snap, or while a thrown Powerborne Stormbreaker is close.
