Getting to the planets

The Space Stone

Unlock Teleport Command and use /space_tp <x> <y> <z> <dimension>.

Ad Astra

Morag, Svartalfheim and Vormir are Ad Astra planets. You need a Tier 4 rocket.

Cosmic Horizons

The planets are in its Marvel solar system.

The planets

  • Vormir (infinity:vormir): Vormir's Edge and the Soul Guardian.
  • Morag (infinity:morag): a flooded planet with the Morag Temple.
  • Svartalfheim (infinity:svartelheim): uru craters and the Aether Chamber.
  • The Garden (infinity:garden): Thanos's farm planet. Not an Ad Astra planet in 7.3.

The Soulworld

infinity:soulworld is a flat world of red sandstone under water. The Soul Stone takes you there and back, and snapped mobs and players end up at 1000 78 1000.
