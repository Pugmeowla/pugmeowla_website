Tesseract

Gives the Space Stone's power in either hand. Drop it and hit it to get the stone out.

Scepter

An iron tier sword with the Mind Stone. In your main hand it fires the Scepter Power Blast. Drop it and hit it to get the stone and an {{item empty_scepter}}.

Aether

Gives the Reality Stone's power in either hand. Drop it and hit it to get the stone out. To put it back, hold the stone in your off hand and use:

{{abilities aether}}

Orb

The Orb has a combination lock. Hold it and use Rotate Left and Rotate Right. A wrong turn plays a low note, puffs smoke and sends you back to the start. In 7.3 the combination is the same in every world (it's on {{page finding-stones.html#the-orb Finding the Stones}} if you want it). Unlocking is per player and lasts until you lock it again.

{{abilities orb}}

Once it's open, right-click to take the Power Stone, or right-click with the Power Stone in your other hand to put it back. Only one Power Stone can be taken per world. 7.3 keeps track of that with a barrier block at 69420 -64 69420 on Morag.

Eye of Agamotto

Wear it on your chest or in the Curios necklace slot, or hold it, for the Time Stone's power. Drop it and hit it to get the stone out, which leaves an {{item empty_eye_of_agamotto}}.

Cosmi-Rod

In 7.3 there's a separate Cosmi-Rod item for each stone. Hold an empty {{item cosmi-rod}} in your main hand, hold a stone in your off hand and right-click to put it in. Sneak and right-click to take it out. The stone's Cosmi-Rod gives that stone's power in your main hand.

{{abilities cosmi_rod}}

Soul Guardian Telephone

{{abilities telephone}}

The Guardian goes back to Vormir after 1,200 ticks (a minute), and the phone has a 24,000 tick (one day) cooldown.

On the ground

Stones, containers and the gauntlet dropped near a player turn into props on invisible armor stands. Hitting a container that holds a stone breaks it open.
