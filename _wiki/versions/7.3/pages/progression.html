Power Stone levels

Same idea as 8.0. The level goes from 0 to 11 and lives in the DamageCycle objective. Buy the levels in order (2, 4, 8, 12, 18, 26, 36, 48, 64, 88 and 128 Aspects of Existence, 434 in total), turn on Damage Control and scroll to pick one. Your base attack damage becomes 2level, so 1 at level 0 up to 2048 at level 11. Power Blast does the same 2level damage, and at level 11 with all six stones it turns rainbow and does near infinite damage.
