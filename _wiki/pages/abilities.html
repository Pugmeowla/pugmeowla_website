Activation types

ActivationHow you use it
Key pressPress the ability's key once. If it has a cooldown, it can't be used again until the cooldown is over.
TogglePress once to turn on, again to turn off.
HoldActive while you hold the key down.
ScrollTurn the ability's toggle on, then use the mouse wheel.
PassiveAlways on once unlocked, while you have the stone's power.
UpgradeA skill-tree unlock that enables or changes other abilities and does nothing by itself.
Right-clickRight-click with the item in hand.

Cooldowns are listed in seconds (20 ticks = 1 second). Abilities marked "Press twice to confirm" ignore the first press and only show a warning; press again within 3 seconds.
