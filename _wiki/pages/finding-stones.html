You don't have to take a stone out to use it: every container gives its stone's power while you hold it (the Eye while worn or held). You do need the bare stone to put it in the {{page gauntlet/index.html Infinity Gauntlet}}.

Locating the structures

The five stone structures are each placed once per world, in a ring around the world origin (0, 0), like strongholds. Approximate distances from the origin, worked out from their structure sets:

Ways to find them:

  • /locate: with cheats, /locate structure infinity:sanctum (or norse_village, sanctuary, aether, morag_temple) while in the right dimension.
  • Explorer maps from the Soul Guardian on Vormir (see below).
  • JEI info pages on each stone and container describe where they are.
Decoys

Each of these structures also has a rare "fake" copy (1% chance per region) that looks the same but has nothing in the frame or on the stand. If the container is missing, you may be in a decoy.

Reaching the other worlds

Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim aren't connected by portals. Once you have the Space Stone, unlock {{ab space/teleport}} and type the dimension ID (infinity:vormir, infinity:morag, infinity:svartelheim) and coordinates. The dimension field suggests every dimension the server knows. See {{page dimensions.html Dimensions & Travel}} for coordinates and what to expect on each world.

A route that works

  1. Space Stone: travel to the Norse Village and take the {{item tesseract}} from the item frame. Unlock Force Field, then the Teleport GUI.
  2. Time Stone: the {{item eye_of_agamotto}} stands on an armor stand inside the Sanctum. Wear it on your chest or in the Curios necklace slot, use Open Eye, then Remove Time Stone.
  3. Mind Stone: in the End, the {{item scepter}} hangs in a glow item frame in the Sanctuary.
  4. Reality Stone: portal to Svartalfheim and dig down to the Aether Chamber. Malekith guards the {{item aether}}.
  5. Power Stone: portal to Morag. The temple is underwater and trapped with dispensers of Arrows of Harming VII. Take the {{item orb}} from its stand and crack it (see the Orb).
  6. Soul Stone: get a {{item best_friends_soul}}, then portal to Vormir (around X 212, Z 99) and trade with the Soul Guardian.

The Soul Stone and the Soul Guardian

A soul for a soul. The first time you kill one of your own tamed pets (a tamed wolf, cat, parrot, or a tamed horse-type animal), you receive a {{item best_friends_soul}} with the lore "How Could You". This happens once per player.

On Vormir, the Soul Guardian villager waits at about 212 121 99. It can't be hurt by anything except /kill or the void, and isn't turned into a witch by lightning. Its trades, each usable once:

Known limitation: explorer maps

A bought map is filled in a few seconds after it lands in your inventory, searching from the Overworld spawn. The Sanctum and Norse Village maps can find their Overworld structures; the Morag Temple, Aether Chamber and Sanctuary are in other dimensions, so those three maps may stay blank.

If the Soul Guardian is lost, an operator can bring it back with /infinity soulguardian respawn. The {{item soul_guardian_telephone}} can also summon it to you.
