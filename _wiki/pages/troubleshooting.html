Abilities

I can't unlock anything in the skill tree

  • Abilities cost {{item aspects_of_existance}}; they're taken from your inventory. Loot Infinity Temples.
  • Many abilities need an earlier one first. Check the "Requires" line on the stone's page.
  • Black Hole and Meteor Storm also need the Power and Space Stones at the same time; Immortality needs all six.
  • If you've done the Snap, almost everything is locked for a Minecraft day. See {{page gauntlet/snap.html#burn The burn}}.

An ability isn't in my ability bar

Passive abilities and upgrades (Water Breathing, Speed, Spatial Lock…) never show in the bar; they just work. Some abilities only appear under certain conditions: Rocket Burst below level 11, Power Cyclone only at level 11, Exit Soulworld only in the Soulworld, skin abilities only while holding the unlock item, the Snap only with six stones.

Size Changing or Invisibility does nothing

Install Pehkui. These use its /scale command.

Time Freeze / Slow Time froze everyone

That's how they work: they change the whole server's tick rate. Turn the ability off to return to 20 TPS.

Stones and containers

"The Power Stone has already been taken from an Orb"

There's only one Power Stone per world. Someone took it already; it's in their inventory, a chest, a gauntlet or on the ground somewhere. The same goes for the Time Stone and the Eye of Agamotto.

I keep getting the Orb combination wrong

Every wrong turn resets the lock to the start, but tells you the right direction for that step was the other one. Write the sequence down as you learn it. The length is set in config/infinity-common.toml. An operator can use /infinity unlock_orb.

I'm hurt by holding the Power Stone

The bare Power Stone in your hand is unprotected. Put it in the gauntlet, the Curios head slot or a Cosmi-Rod.

My stone disappeared from the ground

Stones are unique: while any player carries a stone, copies of it lying on the ground vanish. Check whether someone already has it.

My Eye of Agamotto vanished from my inventory

When the Time Stone leaves your worn gauntlet, the gauntlet removes Eyes of Agamotto from your inventory. Store the Eye in a chest first.

I can't find a structure / the explorer map is blank

The stone structures are placed once per world, thousands of blocks from the origin (see {{page finding-stones.html#locating-the-structures Finding the Stones}}). Explorer maps for Morag, Svartalfheim and the End are filled from the Overworld and may stay blank; use /locate structure in that dimension, or travel and search.

How do I get to Vormir / Morag / Svartalfheim?

With the Space Stone's Teleport GUI. Type infinity:vormir (etc.) and coordinates. See {{page dimensions.html#travel Dimensions}}.

The Soul Guardian is gone

An operator can run /infinity soulguardian respawn. If it's just away, the Soul Guardian Telephone can call it, and it goes back to Vormir on its own after a minute.

Gauntlet

The gauntlet isn't drawn on my arm

  • In the off hand it isn't visible in first person. Use the Curios hands slot.
  • In the Curios menu, the hands slot's visibility (eye) button hides it.

A skin won't apply

The gauntlet must have no skin (return to the original first), you must be wearing it, and the unlock item must be in your main hand. The Burnt Gauntlet can only be replaced by the Nano Gauntlet skin while you're burnt.

I'm stuck in the Soulworld

Leave the Snap cell through its door and walk to X 0, Z 0: a shaft drops to an End portal. Or use Exit Soulworld if you came in with the Soul Stone.

Addon files

My custom stone, skin or gauntlet doesn't show up

  • Restart the game; these files are only read at startup.
  • The file must be in the infinity namespace (data/infinity/gauntlet_skins/…), not your own.
  • File names starting with _ are ignored.
  • Search logs/latest.log for Gauntlet stone, Gauntlet skin, Custom gauntlet or Ground item: every skipped file or ignored value is logged once with the reason.
  • Skins need a registered unlock_item; stones and ground items need a registered item.

My skin shows the normal gauntlet

Its texture or model is missing, so it fell back to the default (logged). Check the file names: textures/models/<id>.png and textures/item/gauntlet_skins/<id>.png, and for a custom model all three .geo.json files.

Still stuck?

Ask on the Discord, and include your mod list and logs/latest.log.
