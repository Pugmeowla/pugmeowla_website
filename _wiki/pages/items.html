Thanos armor

Two full sets, Classic (comics) and MCU, each with a helmet, chestplate, leggings and boots. Both are netherite-material armor with custom Palladium render layers, and the Classic chestplate hides your own body parts so the armor model replaces them. They're in the creative tab; there's no survival recipe.

Fancy Bottle

Hold a {{item fancy_bottle}} in your main hand and it grants a small power whose single entry costs 3 experience levels. Buying it uses up the Fancy Bottle and gives you a Bottle o' Enchanting, so you can store experience.

Materials

The mod adds five metal families, each with dust, ingot, nugget and plate forms (gold-titanium also has a handle, uru a blade), plus iron, gold and coal dusts and plates. They go into the gauntlet, the swords, the Soul Guardian Telephone and skin unlock items. {{item titanium_ingot}} {{item steel_ingot}} {{item gold_titanium_alloy_ingot}} {{item dwarf_star_alloy_ingot}} {{item uru_ingot}}

  • Uru: mine {{item uru_ore}} in meteor craters on Svartalfheim.
  • Dwarf Star Alloy: from {{item dwarf_star_meteor}} blocks in Overworld meteor craters.
  • Titanium pairs with Palladium's titanium; titanium, gold-titanium alloy and ferro-titanium-gold alloy also exist as Tinkers' Construct materials.
  • The {{item hammer}} used for plates and dusts is given back to you after crafting.

Blocks

Storage blocks for each metal, the ores ({{item uru_ore}}, {{item dwarf_star_meteor}}), the terrain blocks of the alien worlds ({{item vormir_sand}}, {{item vormir_stone}}, {{item morag_stone}}, {{item svartelheim_stone}}, {{item svartelheim_sand}}) and the {{item temple_beacon}} found in Infinity Temples. Vormir Stone is very hard (7.5) and needs the right tool to drop.

Creative tabs

Items are split over two creative tabs: Infinity Stone Core (stones, containers, gauntlet, Cosmi-Rod, swords, armor, special items) and Infinity Stone Core Resources (hammer, materials and blocks). The Temple Beacon and molten buckets aren't in a tab.
