Aspects of Existence

{{itemcards aspects_of_existance shards_of_existance}}

Every stone ability is bought with {{item aspects_of_existance}}. Find them in the chest of every Infinity Temple (1–4 per chest, plus a chance of Shards of Existence), or make them from Shards (see JEI). They're also the currency for the Soul Guardian's explorer maps.

How unlocking works

  1. Get the stone's power (hold the stone, its container, put it in your gauntlet…).
  2. Open Palladium's powers screen and pick the stone's tree.
  3. Click an ability whose requirements are met. Its cost in Aspects of Existence is taken from your inventory and it's unlocked for good.

Some unlocks are free (Flight Toggle, Screen Toggle, Daylight Cycle Toggle, Radiation Texture Toggle): clicking these in the tree flips a setting each time. A few abilities also need other stones' powers at the same moment you buy and use them, such as Black Hole, Meteor Storm and Immortality. Operators can unlock or lock everything with /infinity unlock_stones.

Power Stone levels

The Power Stone's strength is a level from 0 to 11, kept in the DamageCycle scoreboard objective and shown on the purple bar while you have the stone. Buy each level in the tree, in order, then choose your active level:

  1. Turn on Damage Control from the ability bar.
  2. Scroll up to raise the level, down to lower it. You can't go above the highest level you've bought.

  • Power Blast does 2level damage per hit; at full power it turns rainbow and explodes where it hits blocks.
  • Rocket Burst damage is multiplied by level ÷ 11 (no damage at level 0) and becomes Power Cyclone at level 11.
  • Meteor Storm's moon is 1 + level blocks big.
  • Your base attack damage goes back to normal when you lose the Power Stone.

If an operator locks the level unlocks again (for example with /infinity lock_stones), your level drops to the highest one you still have.

Soul Storage

With {{ab soul/soul_storage}} unlocked, every entity you kill stores one soul, up to 50. Souls are kept in the SoulStorage scoreboard objective and shown on the orange bar. Clones and mobs whose soul has been drained don't count.

AbilitySoulsEffect
{{ab soul/soul_heal}}−1Restores 2 hearts. Does nothing at full health or with no souls.
{{ab soul/soul_drain}}+1Drains the looked-at mob, which then stands still in greyscale.

Advancements

The mod's advancement tab starts with Pugmeowla's Infinity Stone Core and follows the hunt: obtaining the gauntlet cast (Infinity Is Within Reach), making the gauntlet ("Fine, I'll do it myself"), each container and its stone (The Jewel of Odin's Treasure Room, The Scepter of Loki, The Converter of Matter into Dark Matter, The Power of a Celestial, The Sorcerers Quest, and one per stone), the Cosmi-Rod (It's called the Universal Weapon. Oh, really? and Dance Battle), the hammer (It's Clobberin' Time!), all six stones (Six Stones, One Gauntlet), the Snap (I Am Inevitable) and destroying the stones (Peace in our Time).
