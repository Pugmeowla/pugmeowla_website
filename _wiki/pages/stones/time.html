You have the Time Stone's power while holding it, wearing it in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, socketed in a Cosmi-Rod, or while an Eye of Agamotto that still holds the stone is held, worn on your chest or in the Curios necklace slot. There is only one Time Stone per world: see the Eye of Agamotto for taking it out.

Whenever a time power is running, your arm is raised, green time rings are drawn on you and a green tint covers your screen (switch the tint off with the free Screen Toggle).

Slow Time steps

Change Flow of Time changes the server's tick rate through Chrono API. Each step is 4 ticks per second:

Step−5−4−3−2−1012345
Ticks per second4036322824201612842

Scroll down to slow time (positive steps) and up to speed it up. The green bar shows the step, with the middle being normal speed. While time is slowed you get hidden Speed and Haste so you keep up, and you're whitelisted from the tick-rate change.

Server-wide

Change Flow of Time and Time Freeze affect the whole server, every player included, until you turn them off. On a shared server, agree on it first.

The time command

With {{ab time/time_set}} unlocked and the Time Stone active, /infinity time set <time> works for you without operator rights. It always moves the world forward to the requested time of day, so setting 1000 at 13000 skips to the next morning. See Commands.
