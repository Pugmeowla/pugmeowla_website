You have the Soul Stone's power while holding it, wearing it in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, or socketed in a Cosmi-Rod.

Obtaining it

  1. Kill one of your own tamed pets. The first time you do, you get a {{item best_friends_soul}} ("How Could You").
  2. Travel to Vormir with the Space Stone's {{ab space/teleport}} (dimension infinity:vormir, around X 212, Z 99).
  3. Trade the Best Friend's Soul to the Soul Guardian for the Soul Stone.

Details about the Soul Guardian and its other trades are on {{page finding-stones.html#soul-stone Finding the Stones}}.

The Soulworld

Enter Soulworld takes you, and everything within 5 blocks, to infinity:soulworld: an endless orange plain of smooth red sandstone under a layer of water. Everyone arrives at Y 72 above the same X and Z. Use the same ability slot again (it becomes Exit Soulworld) to send everyone back to exactly where they were. More on {{page dimensions.html#soulworld Dimensions}}.

When the defenses switch off

  • After the Snap: while you're burnt, Health, Regeneration and Immortality (like most stone abilities) are locked.
  • Powerborne's Stormbreaker: while you wear the Infinity Gauntlet and a thrown Stormbreaker from the Powerborne addon is within 10 blocks of you, Saturation, Effect Immunity, Regeneration, Health and Immortality are locked and you take 1 damage every tick.
