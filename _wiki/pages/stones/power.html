You have the Power Stone's power while holding it, wearing it in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, or socketed in a Cosmi-Rod. Unlike the other containers, the Orb does not give you the stone's power: you have to crack its lock and take the stone out. There is only one Power Stone per world.

Cosmic radiation

Holding the bare Power Stone in your main or off hand, without it also being in your gauntlet, head slot or a Cosmi-Rod, is unprotected. After half a second the stone overloads: you get Wither, you're rooted in place, a cyclone of energy fires rockets around you, and radiation cracks spread over your skin. Move it somewhere protected as soon as you can.

Levels in short

The Power Stone has a level from 0 to 11, stored in the DamageCycle scoreboard objective and shown on the purple bar. You raise the maximum by buying levels in the skill tree and pick the current level with Damage Control and the mouse wheel. While the stone is active your base attack damage is 2level, and Energy Blast, Power Blast, Rocket Burst and Meteor Storm all scale with it. At level 11 with all six stones the level counts as maxed: damage becomes infinite. Full table: {{page progression.html#power-levels Power Levels}}.

Radiation overlay

The glowing cracks drawn on your skin during Power Cyclone (and when the stone overloads) can be switched off with the free {{ab power/radiation_texture_toggle}} unlock. It still works while you're burnt from the Snap.
