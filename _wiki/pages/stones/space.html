You have the Space Stone's power while holding the stone or the Tesseract in either hand, wearing the stone in the Curios head slot, with it in your gauntlet, or socketed in a Cosmi-Rod. Get it out of the Tesseract by dropping the Tesseract and hitting it.

Get this stone first: its {{ab space/teleport}} is how you reach Vormir, Morag and Svartalfheim.

How portals behave

  • A portal is a pair: one end opens 2.5 blocks in front of you, the other at the destination. Each end grows open over 3 seconds.
  • Anything that walks into either end, players and mobs alike, comes out of the other one on safe ground. Each entity has a one second cooldown so it doesn't bounce back.
  • Teleport GUI portals stay open until someone hits one end; both ends then close. The chunks at both ends stay loaded while the pair is open.
  • Jump Teleport portals close on their own after 3 seconds. If no block is in range you get the message "No block found within range for the portal."
  • The menu's background, the portals' color, size, and a fire style (an orange ring of flames like Doctor Strange's sling ring) are configurable by addons: see {{page addons/abilities.html Ability Reference}}.

Telekinesis controls

InputWhile holding something
Ability keyToggle off: set it down (blocks are placed back where possible).
Mouse wheelMove it closer or further (2–24 blocks).
Left-clickThrow it. Faster, bigger things hurt more when they hit.
Right-clickSpatial Lock it in place (after unlocking {{ab space/spatial_lock}}).

A held block becomes a floating block entity. If it can't be placed back it drops as an item after 30 seconds in the air. Picking up a spatially locked target frees it.

Tips

  • Force Field roots you in place: use it to tank a fight, not to escape one. Jump Teleport is the escape.
  • Black Hole and Meteor Storm both need the Power Stone too. With both stones in a gauntlet you get all of them.
  • The Teleport GUI and Jump Teleport keep working after the Snap, when the other stone abilities are locked.
